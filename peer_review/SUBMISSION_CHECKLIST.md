# Submission Checklist

This checklist is intentionally strict. A document touching this subject matter
should not be represented as peer-review-ready unless every item below is true.

## Core Scope

- The submission states explicitly whether it is:
  - an axiomatic mathematical framework,
  - an empirical assessment framework,
  - or a philosophical interpretation.
- The submission does not blur those categories.
- Every theorem is either proved in the manuscript or stated with a precise
  external citation.
- Every assumption is labeled as an assumption, not smuggled into prose.

## Mathematical Discipline

- No unresolved references remain.
- No `??` placeholders remain.
- No proof sketches remain in sections presented as complete results.
- Standard results are cited rather than redescribed as if novel.
- Custom definitions are labeled as model-specific choices.
- Claims of existence or consistency are backed either by a concrete model or a
  transparent relative-consistency statement.

## Editorial Discipline

- No “complete”, “definitive”, or “canonical” language appears unless it is
  internal project language and clearly marked as such.
- No claims about cognition, consciousness, or alignment appear without being
  identified as interpretation, hypothesis, or empirical target.
- Submission prose avoids hype, product framing, and business language.
- Every section has a clear purpose and a clear relationship to the paper’s
  main thesis.

## Empirical Discipline

- If empirical claims are made, the manuscript names:
  - the measured quantities,
  - the dataset or sampling process,
  - the scoring procedure,
  - the statistical evaluation method,
  - and the limitations.
- If empirical claims are not made, the manuscript says so directly.

## Recommended Gate

At present, only the concise referee manuscript should be prepared for external
review. The full compendium should remain an internal reference document until
its unresolved references, sketches, and speculative extensions are removed or
reworked.
