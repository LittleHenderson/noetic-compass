# About the Author

**Little Henderson** inspects flight-grade satellite hardware for a living.

As a Quality Inspection Technician III at Amazon Leo, formerly Project Kuiper,
he operates Signal Pathway Verification stations on satellite PCB assemblies —
validating that every trace meets program specification before hardware
advances, and isolating whether a failure originates in the test fixture, the
cabling, the probe contact, or the board itself rather than passing an
unresolved failure downstream. He holds buy-off authority on hardware moving
between build stages, and serves as the technical reference for Level 1 and
Level 2 inspectors on IPC interpretation and defect classification.

He is a Certified IPC Specialist in IPC-A-610, IPC J-STD-001, the J-STD-001
Space Applications Electronic Hardware Addendum, and IPC/WHMA-A-620, and
inspects to Class 3 acceptability under AS9100. Before Amazon Leo he inspected
space-grade boards at Jabil Defense and Aerospace, where thermal cycling and
radiation exposure drive the tighter workmanship limits this book keeps
invoking.

This book came off that bench. The acceptance criteria, the test points, the
bills of materials, the failure-mode tables, the insistence that a board does
what its schematic and its test report say it does and nothing more — those are
not literary devices. They are the working habits of aerospace quality
inspection, pointed at cognition.

He is also the creator of the Cognitive Architecture System, filed as USPTO
Provisional Application #64/037,173, and the developer of Noetic Compass, a
cross-model AI interpretability suite. He is completing a B.S. in Computer
Science with an artificial intelligence emphasis.
