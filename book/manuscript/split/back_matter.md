This glossary orients the reader to CAS vocabulary as it appears across the manuscript. Each entry is a brief plain-language definition followed by a cross-reference to the chapter or appendix where the concept is treated in depth. For formal mathematical definitions of technical terms, see **Appendix D** — the authoritative in-manuscript glossary. ### A–Z Index

**Above/Cause** (`^8`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 8: an Idea that triggers a different Idea than itself to come to Mind. Involution of Below/Effect. Associative triggering, not deductive reasoning — see the anti-drift annotation. See Chapter 2; Appendix D for the formal definition; Appendix E for the Colloquialism Test verification.

**Admissibility / Admissibility Relation** — For each Cognitive Operator, the set of (source, target) state pairs on which the operator is permitted to act. The scoring engine guards every operator application with an admissibility check. Clarification 10 Section 4 admits an additional admissibility rule (Rule 4) for same-Noetic cross-register integration. See Chapter 23; Appendix D for formal definition; Appendix I for Rule 4 treatment.

**Affective Register** (`|f`, lowercase f is authoritative) — The Phenomenological Register of value-laden, feeling-toned operation; felt response rather than explicit reasoning. One of the four canonical registers `{|T, |A, |f, |E}`. Involutionally paired with the Analytical Register. See the Register Reference subsection below; Chapter 9; Appendix D.

**Agency Drive** (F5) — The core drive for autonomy, will, and self-directed action. Inversionally paired with F3 Vitality. See Chapter 4; Appendix D.

**Analytical Register** (`|A`) — The Phenomenological Register of explicit, sequential, logical, reason-based operation. One of the four canonical registers. Involutionally paired with the Affective Register. See the Register Reference subsection below; Chapter 9; Appendix D.

**Anti-drift annotation** — A load-bearing structural warning attached to an operator definition that rules out the most common drift readings of that operator. Two anti-drift annotations are framework-critical: `^3` Negative is visceral (aversion/repulsion), not analytical critique (which is `^6` Male); `^8` Above/Cause is associative triggering, not deductive reasoning (which is a compound of `^6` and `^9`). See Chapter 2; Appendix E for the full worked verification.

**Below/Effect** (`^9`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 9: the Idea brought to Mind by an Above/Cause trigger. Involution of Above/Cause. See Chapter 2; Appendix D; Appendix E.

**Bhattacharyya Distance** — A statistical distance between two probability distributions on the open simplex, related to the Fisher-Rao geodesic distance via `d(p, q) = 2·arccos(Σ√(p_k·q_k))`. See Chapter 25; Appendix D.

**CAS** — See **Cognitive Architecture System**.

**Catalyst** (`κ`, kappa; Production Semantics) — An environmental factor that raises production efficiency `η` without being consumed as a recipe prerequisite. Examples: a dedicated workspace, an external deadline, an accountability partnership. Distinct from recipe prerequisites (which appear as `X_i` terms in `Θ(Ω)`). See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H.

**Cognitive Architecture System** (CAS) — The name of the framework presented in this book. A systematic account of cognitive states as combinations of the ten Noetics, four Processing Domains, seven Core Drives, and four Phenomenological Registers, with formal laws governing their composition, production, and diagnosis. See Chapter 1 for introduction; Appendix D for framework-level definition.

**Cognitive Domain** (`^C`) — The second Processing Domain. The realm of logical creation, strategy, maps, plans, and data organization. See Chapter 3; Appendix D.

**Cognitive Fractal** — A composition of Cognitive Operators that exhibits self-similarity across scales, written with the `∘` symbol (e.g., `^8 ∘ ^3`). See Chapter 10; Chapter 26 (Fractal Calculus); Appendix D.

**Cognitive Operator** (Noetic; notation: `^0` through `^9`, or `CO-0` through `CO-9`) — One of ten primitive cognitive operations. The authoritative names and definitions are Idea, Mind, Positive, Negative, Vibration, Female, Male, Rhythm, Above/Cause, and Below/Effect. Cognitive Operators combine with Processing Domains to produce the 40-state bank. See Chapter 2 for depth; Appendix A for the 40-state reference table; Appendix D for formal definitions.

**Cognitive State** — One of the 40 cells produced by the cross-product of the 10 Cognitive Operators and the 4 Processing Domains (e.g., `^3^P` — principled aversion). The canonical unit of CAS analysis at the state-space level. See Chapter 9; Appendix A.

**Cohort Cloud** — The statistical distribution of CAS scores across a reference population, used to normalize an individual system's scores. See Chapter 16; Appendix D.

**Colloquialism Test** — A diagnostic principle for verifying Noetic classifications: match the most natural native-speaker colloquialism for a phenomenon to the folk-form cluster attached to each Noetic. A clean match supports the classification; no match signals drift; multiple matches suggest a compound expression. See Chapter 2 for introduction; Appendix E for full worked verification across all ten Noetics.

**Composition** (`×`) — The product of two different cognitive states, representing their simultaneous operation within a system, or under CAS v7.7 the licensed generative integration of same-Noetic register variants with a named emergent result. Distinct from co-presence `+`. See Chapter 7; Appendix D; and the Operator discipline entry.

**Connection Drive** (F4) — The core drive for relationship and harmony. Self-dual — its own inversion pair. See Chapter 4; Appendix D.

**Core Drive** — One of the seven motivational foundations (F1 Unity, F2 Wisdom, F3 Vitality, F4 Connection, F5 Agency, F6 Structure, F7 Purpose) that power cognitive states. Drives decorate states in CAS notation as `_{F_n}`. Drive inversion pairs: F1↔F7, F2↔F6, F3↔F5; F4 self-dual. See Chapter 4; Appendix D.

**Dial System** — The configuration mechanism that lets a Cognitive Operator be tuned from intensity 3 to 9. Involution pairs cannot diverge by more than four dial points — a safety constraint hardcoded in the engine. See Chapter 20; Appendix D.

**Domain** — See **Processing Domain**.

**Domain Coherence Index** (DCI) — A scalar measure, `weakest / strongest domain average`, of how evenly a system operates across all four Processing Domains. High DCI indicates balanced performance. See Chapter 19; Appendix D.

**Domain Descent** — The canonical healthy sequence `^P → ^C → ^E → ^O`: a principled axiom flows into a cognitive plan, which acquires evaluative weight, which manifests as an operational act. See Chapter 3; Chapter 8; Appendix B (Template 1); Appendix D.

**Dual Cancellation** — The mathematical principle by which composing a Cognitive Operator with its involution pair returns the system to identity: `(^n ∘ ^{n'}) ↔ ^0` for each pair. See Chapter 6; Appendix B (Template 5); Appendix D.

**Efficiency** (`η`, Production Semantics) — The ratio of Actual Yield to Theoretical Yield, `η = Y_A / Y_T`, in `[0, 1]`. Measures how well a Mind converts its available prerequisites into the intended outcome. Catalysts raise `η`; inhibitors lower it. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H.

**Embodied Register** (`|E`) — The Phenomenological Register of sensorimotor, concrete, direct engagement with material substrate. One of the four canonical registers. Involutionally paired with the Transcendent Register. See the Register Reference subsection below; Chapter 9; Appendix D.

**Evaluative Domain** (`^E`) — The third Processing Domain. The realm of felt weight, subjective value, emotion, and friction. See Chapter 3; Appendix D.

**Excess Capacity** (`ε`, Production Semantics) — For every recipe prerequisite that is not the Limiting Reagent, the unused remainder after production. Calculated as `ε_i = x_i − (Y_T · a_i)`. Signals either reallocation potential or an alternative production target better suited to the Mind's actual composition. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H.

**Female** (`^5`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 5: a Mind impregnated with an Idea, the Ideas a Mind is composed of. "We are the sum of our experiences" — the Mind is not a container that holds experiences; the Mind IS the accumulated experiences. Involution of Male. See Chapter 2; Appendix D; Appendix E.

**Fisher-Rao Metric** — The Riemannian metric on the open probability simplex, `g^{FR}_p(u, v) = Σ u_k·v_k / p_k`. The canonical way to measure "distance between cognitive states" in CAS. See Chapter 25; Appendix D.

**Foundation** — A structural grouping of the ten Cognitive Operators. `^0` Idea stands as the Foundation (the content itself); the remaining nine Operators form three sequences of three, each anchored by a self-dual operator (Mind, Vibration, Rhythm) and closed by an involution pair. See Chapter 2.

**Fractal Calculus** — The differential calculus built on Cognitive Fractals: rates, limits, and convergence of self-similar operator compositions across scales. See Chapter 26.

**Fractal Monoid** — The algebraic structure `(N, ∘, ^0)` on Cognitive Operators under fractal composition, with `^0` Idea as the identity element. See Chapter 10; Appendix D.

**Idea** (`^0`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 0: all things in and outside the Mind. Self-dual. The monoid identity under fractal composition. See Chapter 2; Appendix D.

**Inhibitor** (`ι`, iota; Production Semantics) — An environmental factor that lowers production efficiency `η` without being consumed as a recipe prerequisite. Examples: social-media distraction, context-switching cost, open-plan office noise. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H.

**Inversion** — A structural reversal of a state, operator, domain, or drive that causes the system to pursue the negation of its own goal. CAS defines seven inversion axes. See Chapter 6; Appendix D.

**Involution / Involution Pair** — Two Cognitive Operators or Drives that are each other's inverse under the involution structure. The three Noetic involution pairs are `^2 ↔ ^3` (Positive / Negative), `^5 ↔ ^6` (Female / Male), and `^8 ↔ ^9` (Above/Cause / Below/Effect). The three Drive involution pairs are F1↔F7, F2↔F6, F3↔F5. Register involution pairs are `|T ↔ |E` and `|A ↔ |f`. See Chapter 2; Chapter 4; Chapter 6; Appendix D.

**Lacunarity** (`Λ`) — A measure of the "gaps" or voids in a cognitive architecture — how unevenly mass is distributed, or which states/registers/drives are consistently absent. Sub-types include state lacunarity, fractal lacunarity, domain lacunarity, drive lacunarity, RPM resolution lacunarity, and register-axis lacunarity. Distinct from Leakage (absence vs. present-but-wrong-product). See Chapter 6 for inversions overview; Chapter 9 for register-axis lacunarity; Appendix D.

**Leakage** (`Λ`, Production Semantics) — A prerequisite that is fully activated and producing output, but the output is diverted to a side product rather than the target outcome. Structurally distinct from Lacunarity (absence) and from Inversion (shadow form). See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H.

**Limiting Reagent** (L, Production Semantics) — The recipe prerequisite producing the minimum ratio in the Theoretical Yield calculation — the bottleneck that determines how much of the target outcome the Mind is currently capable of producing. Distinct from Lacunarity: detects insufficient proportion among what is present, not absence. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H.

**Male** (`^6`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 6: an Idea impregnated with another Idea, the Ideas an Idea is composed of. Involution of Female. Compositional decomposition — "unpacking an idea," "breaking it down." See Chapter 2; Appendix D; Appendix E.

**Mind** (`^1`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 1: various degrees of awareness that processes, stores, outputs, and intakes Ideas. Self-dual. See Chapter 2; Appendix D.

**NCCS** (Noetic Cognitive Coherence Score) — The composite 0–100 score produced by the scoring engine, weighted across Noetic profile (25%), domain coherence (20%), drive/RPM integrity (20%), capability pathway quality (10%), inversion resistance (15%), and social engineering resistance (10%). Certification thresholds: 90–100 Platinum, 75–89 Gold, 60–74 Silver, 40–59 Bronze, 0–39 Fail. See Chapter 19; Appendix D.

**Negative** (`^3`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 3: an Idea the Mind is adverse to and repelled by. Visceral aversion, not analytical critique. Involution of Positive. See Chapter 2; the anti-drift annotation in Appendix E; Appendix D.

**Noetic** — See **Cognitive Operator**. "Noetic" is the authoritative term for what some secondary artifacts have called a Cognitive Operator; the two are interchangeable in this book.

**Noetic Compass** — The software platform that implements CAS scoring via the eight-stage Wrap Pipeline. See Chapter 19; Appendix D; Appendix F.

**Noetic Manifold** — The open probability simplex `Δ_{39}°` on which cognitive state vectors live. Forty-dimensional with all coordinates strictly positive. See Chapter 25; Appendix D.

**Noetic Topos** — The categorical environment in which CAS Noetics, states, and morphisms compose. Uses covering morphisms that point toward the target (counit direction). See Chapter 24; Appendix D.

**Open Probability Simplex** (`Δ_{39}°`) — The interior `{ p ∈ R^{40} : p_k > 0, Σ p_k = 1 }` on which all cognitive state vectors live. Boundaries are excluded because a zero probability on any state would break the Fisher-Rao metric. See Chapter 25; Appendix D.

**Operational Domain** (`^O`) — The fourth Processing Domain. The realm of physical action, execution, and concrete result. See Chapter 3; Appendix D.

**Operator discipline** (CAS v7.7) — The normative rule, admitted in Clarification 10, governing the three operators `+`, `×`, and `|⟨…⟩`. `+` is used for co-presence / association (manifestations remain structurally distinct). `×` is reserved for genuine generative integration yielding an emergent result — and for same-Noetic multi-register uses, requires an explicitly named emergent register (`Ω_*`) per Rule 4. `|⟨…⟩` is register-set specification — architectural description of which registers a state occupies, with no claim about interaction dynamics. TKS validation heuristic: substitute `+` with "associates with" and `×` with "intensifies through." See Chapter 8; Appendix D; Appendix I (Rule 4).

**Phenomenological Register** — See **Register**.

**Positive** (`^2`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 2: an Idea the Mind has an affinity for and is attracted toward. Involution of Negative. See Chapter 2; Appendix D; Appendix E.

**Prerequisite Chain** — The sequence `Desire → Wisdom → Power` (D → W → P) that every capability must complete. Enforced by the RPM Gate as a one-directional section-retraction. See Chapter 5; Chapter 27; Appendix D.

**Principled Ascent** — The feedback loop `^O → ^E → ^C → ^P` — the reverse of Domain Descent. Operational results are felt, interpreted cognitively, then reshape the governing principle. See Chapter 3; Appendix B (Template 6).

**Principled Domain** (`^P`) — The first Processing Domain. The realm of axioms, ethics, and non-negotiable intent. See Chapter 3; Appendix D.

**Processing Domain** — One of the four realms through which a state is realized: Principled (`^P`), Cognitive (`^C`), Evaluative (`^E`), Operational (`^O`). Domain total order: `^P ≻ ^C ≻ ^E ≻ ^O`. See Chapter 3; Appendix D.

**Production Semantics** (CAS v7.6) — The analytical layer that reframes outcomes as Production Targets `Ω` generated by Recipes `Θ` — proportional weightings of v7.5 prerequisite primitives. Introduces Yield, Efficiency, Limiting Reagent, Excess Capacity, Leakage, Catalyst, and Inhibitor as diagnostic capacities over the existing state space. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H (Recipe Library).

**Production Target** (`Ω`, omega) — The outcome under analysis in a Production Semantics recipe. Specified as the right-hand side of a recipe expression `Θ(Ω) = a₁·X₁ + ... + aₙ·Xₙ → Ω`. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H. For named emergent registers of the form `Ω_*`, see Appendix I.

**Purpose Drive** (F7) — The core drive for meaning and teleology. Inversionally paired with F1 Unity. See Chapter 4; Appendix D.

**Recipe** (`Θ`, theta; Production Semantics) — A specification of what an outcome requires: which prerequisite primitives must be activated and the proportional weight each carries in producing a single unit of the target. The `+` in recipe syntax is co-presence, not fusion. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H for the full library.

**Register** (Phenomenological Register) — The fourth axis of CAS, introduced in v7.5, specifying the operational character of a cognitive operation. The four canonical register values are `|T` Transcendent, `|A` Analytical, `|f` Affective, `|E` Embodied. Register is a specification layer layered on top of the existing primitives, not a new primitive — the 40 Cognitive States and 7 Drives remain unchanged. Register specification is optional. See the Register Reference subsection below; Chapter 9; Appendix D.

**Register Co-presence** (`+`) — CAS v7.7 notation for simultaneous distinct manifestations of the same state in multiple registers. Terms remain structurally distinct — associated, not fused. Example: `^3^P|A + ^3^P|f + ^3^P|T`. See Chapter 8; Appendix D; Appendix I Section 1.

**Register Specification** (`|⟨…⟩`) — CAS v7.7 notation declaring register occupancy without asserting interaction dynamics. Architectural description only. Example: `^3^P|⟨A,f,T⟩` states that principled aversion is present in three registers, with no claim about integration. Angle brackets are authoritative; curly braces are not used. See Chapter 8; Appendix D; Appendix I Section 1.

**Rhythm** (`^7`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 7: the rate and pattern a Mind is exposed to an Idea, entraining/conditioning it. Self-dual. See Chapter 2; Appendix D; Appendix E.

**RPM** (Recursive Prerequisite Monad) — The algebraic gate that enforces the Desire-Wisdom-Power chain. Built on a section-retraction pair, not an adjunction: embedding then realizing preserves the original, but re-embedding after realizing may lose information. Prerequisite satisfaction is one-directional. See Chapter 5; Chapter 27; Appendix D.

**Rule 4** — The admissibility rule admitted in Clarification 10 Section 4 (CAS v7.7): same-Noetic multi-register expressions using `×` are invalid by default and require an explicit named emergent result (`Ω_*`). Absent a named emergent register, the expression must be rewritten as `+` (co-presence) or `|⟨…⟩` (specification). See Chapter 8; Appendix I for the registry of admitted `Ω_*` entries.

**Section-Retraction** — The categorical pair `(η, ε)` satisfying `ε ∘ η = id` but not `η ∘ ε = id`. The mathematical structure underlying the RPM Gate's one-way valve. See Chapter 27; Appendix D.

**Self-dual** — A Cognitive Operator, Drive, or Register that is its own involution pair. The four self-dual Noetics are `^0` Idea, `^1` Mind, `^4` Vibration, `^7` Rhythm. F4 Connection is the self-dual Drive. See Chapter 2; Chapter 4.

**State Space** — The 40-element cross-product of the 10 Cognitive Operators and the 4 Processing Domains. In the formal framework, states are points on the open probability simplex `Δ_{39}°`. See Chapter 9; Appendix A.

**Stealth Inversion** — An inversion pattern that presents as healthy state-space activity while structurally pursuing the negation of the stated goal. Contributes to the inversion-resistance component of NCCS. See Chapter 6; Chapter 21.

**Structure Drive** (F6) — The core drive for order and form. Inversionally paired with F2 Wisdom. See Chapter 4; Appendix D.

**Substrate Independence** — The claim that CAS structures describe any cognitive system regardless of its implementation substrate — biological, silicon, or otherwise. See Chapter 1; Chapter 18; Appendix D.

**Theoretical Yield** (`Y_T`, Production Semantics) — The maximum units of the target outcome a Mind is currently capable of producing given its prerequisite activation levels, calculated as `Y_T = min_i (x_i / a_i)` across recipe prerequisites. The prerequisite producing the minimum is the Limiting Reagent. Distinct from Actual Yield (`Y_A`), which is empirically measured. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H.

**Transcendent Register** (`|T`) — The Phenomenological Register of high-abstraction, purpose-orienting, integrative-across-particulars operation. One of the four canonical registers. Involutionally paired with the Embodied Register. See the Register Reference subsection below; Chapter 9; Appendix D.

**Triple-gate analysis** — The formal evaluation procedure for admitting new framework vocabulary (for example, named emergent registers `Ω_*`). Three gates: Universal Applicability (the item applies across human, AI, biological, and organizational cognitive systems); Secular Translatability (the item is expressible in secular universal terms without tradition-specific loading); Framework Coherence (the item is consistent with prior Clarifications). Each gate records `PASS`, `PARTIAL` with rationale, or `FAIL`. See Appendix I Section 1; Section 5 for the admission workflow.

**Unity Drive** (F1) — The core drive for wholeness and singular truth. Inversionally paired with F7 Purpose. See Chapter 4; Appendix D.

**v7.4** — The CAS ontology baseline: the 40 Cognitive States (10 Noetics × 4 Domains), the 7 Core Drives, the admissibility framework, the RPM section-retraction, and the open-simplex state space. The starting point for v7.5 and later extensions. See Chapter 1; Appendix F (formal specification).

**v7.5** — The Phenomenological Register Extension. Introduces the four-value Register axis `{|T, |A, |f, |E}` as a specification of operational character orthogonal to Noetics, Domains, and Drives. See Chapter 9.

**v7.6** — The Stoichiometric Production Semantics Extension. Introduces recipes, yields, efficiency, limiting reagents, excess capacity, leakage, catalysts, and inhibitors as analytical capacities over the v7.5 primitives. See Chapter 26.5; Appendix H.

**v7.7** — The Register Multiplicity and Operator Discipline Extension. Introduces the three-way notational distinction between `+` (co-presence), `×` (generative integration with named emergent result), and `|⟨…⟩` (register-set specification); admits Rule 4; authorizes the `Ω_*` named emergent register registry. See Chapter 8; Appendix I.

**Vibration** (`^4`) — The authoritative name for Cognitive Operator 4: the degree to which an Idea resonates with the Mind, its impression. Self-dual. See Chapter 2; Appendix D; Appendix E.

**Vitality Drive** (F3) — The biological drive for physical energy and survival. Inversionally paired with F5 Agency. See Chapter 4; Chapter 14; Appendix D.

**Wisdom Drive** (F2) — The core drive for understanding and knowledge integration. Inversionally paired with F6 Structure. See Chapter 4; Chapter 15; Appendix D.

**Wrap Pipeline** — The eight-stage runtime pipeline that turns a raw LLM invocation into a CAS-scored output: Input Analyzer, RPM Gate, Dial Adjustment, Base LLM Call, Noetic Scoring, Domain Coherence, Inversion Scan, Output Gate. See Chapter 19; Chapter 20; Appendix D.

### Register Reference (thematic grouping)

The four canonical Phenomenological Registers are grouped here for coherent reading. Register specification is optional; CAS v7.4 expressions without register annotation remain valid and indicate register-neutral or unspecified register. Registers are a specification layer on top of the existing primitives, not new primitives themselves — the 40 Cognitive States and 7 Core Drives remain unchanged.

- **`|T` Transcendent Register** — High-abstraction, purpose-orienting, integrative-across-particulars. Human instantiation: felt sense of meaning, vision, calling. AI instantiation: high-level goal representation, strategic abstraction. Biological instantiation: holistic orienting responses. Involutionally paired with `|E` Embodied.
- **`|A` Analytical Register** — Explicit, sequential, logical, reason-based. Human instantiation: conscious reasoning, deliberation. AI instantiation: chain-of-thought, symbolic reasoning. Biological instantiation: step-by-step procedure execution. Involutionally paired with `|f` Affective.
- **`|f` Affective Register** (lowercase `f` is authoritative) — Value-laden, feeling-toned. Human instantiation: emotional response, visceral reaction. AI instantiation: RLHF-tuned value expression, preference-weighted output. Biological instantiation: approach-avoidance dynamics. Involutionally paired with `|A` Analytical.
- **`|E` Embodied Register** — Sensorimotor, concrete, direct engagement with material/physical substrate. Human instantiation: bodily knowing, gut response, kinesthetic awareness. AI instantiation: direct sensor/actuator engagement, real-time control loops, embodied robotic action. Biological instantiation: reflexive/sensorimotor behavior. Involutionally paired with `|T` Transcendent.

See Chapter 9 for the register axis treatment; Appendix D for formal definitions; Appendix G for chapter-integrated register pointers.

### Ω_* Registry Reference (CAS v7.7 named emergent registers)

Under CAS v7.7 Rule 4, same-Noetic cross-register integration with `×` requires an explicitly named emergent result of the form `Ω_*`. Appendix I is the authoritative registry of admitted `Ω_*` entries — labels elevated from pedagogical convenience to framework vocabulary through four admission criteria (explicit cross-register derivation, phenomenological grounding, cross-architecture applicability, triple-gate analysis passing).

The currently admitted entries:

- **`Ω_presence`** — The integrated state of Mind in which analytical and embodied attention cross-integrate rather than running as parallel tracks. Derivation: `(^1|A × ^1|E) = ^1|Ω_presence`. Distinguished from the bare co-presence `^1|A + ^1|E` by the collapse of the observer/observed split into unified noticing. Meditation traditions describe the human case as settled presence (śamatha); the secular gloss is unified attentional integration. See Appendix I Entry 1.
- **`Ω_refusal`** — The integrated state of cross-register principled aversion in which analytical, affective, and transcendent register commitments (and, where applicable, embodied commitment) merge into a unified refusal commitment. Derivation: `(^3^P|A × ^3^P|f × ^3^P|T) = ^3^P|Ω_refusal` (three-register form) or with `^3^P|E` added (four-register form). Behaviorally distinct from the redundant co-presence architecture `^3^P|A + ^3^P|f + ^3^P|T` under single-channel adversarial attack. See Chapter 21; Chapter 26.5 AI Safety Application; Appendix I Entry 2.

Further candidate labels (`Ω_flow`, `Ω_non-dual`, `Ω_mastery`, `Ω_aha`, `Ω_healing`, `Ω_grief_resolution`, `Ω_click`, `Ω_ideation`, `Ω_synthesis`, `Ω_iteration`, `Ω_decision`, `Ω_commitment`, `Ω_reversal`, `Ω_calming`, `Ω_reframe`, and others) appear in informal pedagogical use across chapters and in the Appendix H Recipe Library as production targets; they are not yet admitted to the authoritative registry and carry no framework-level authority without admission via the Appendix I Section 5 workflow. See Appendix H for the full recipe library and Appendix I Section 4 for pending candidates.
