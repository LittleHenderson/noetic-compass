 Appendix I: Named Emergent Register Registry (CAS v7.7)

 Section 1: Purpose

Clarification 10 Section 2C establishes that same-Noetic cross-register integration — the `×` operator applied across multiple register-instantiations of the same Noetic × Domain cell — produces an emergent state that must be explicitly named (Rule 4). A bare expression of the form `(^n^D|R₁ × ^n^D|R₂ × ... × ^n^D|Rₖ)` without a named emergent result is inadmissible: the integration must either be rewritten as co-presence `+`, as register-set specification `|⟨…⟩`, or completed with a specified emergent register `^n^D|Ω_*`.

This appendix is the authoritative registry of admitted `Ω_*` named emergent registers. Registry inclusion elevates a label from pedagogical convenience to framework vocabulary: an admitted `Ω_*` may be used in compiler-validated expressions, cited in the peer-review paper, referenced in formal incident analyses, and invoked in engine-level scoring without further justification. A label not in the registry is permitted in informal pedagogical contexts — worked examples, exercises, narrative discussion — but does not carry authoritative force.

Registry admission requires four criteria, each checked explicitly at admission time:

1. **Explicit cross-register derivation per Rule 4.** The emergent register must be specified via a concrete `×` integration of same-Noetic register variants, with the derivation recorded in the registry entry. No `Ω_*` is admitted on the basis of phenomenological description alone; there must be a formal integration expression whose result the `Ω_*` names.
2. **Phenomenological grounding.** The emergent state must be distinguishable, in either empirical observation or disciplined introspection, from the bare co-presence of its register constituents. If the cross-register integration has no phenomenological signature beyond the sum of its parts, the co-presence formulation `+` suffices and no emergent register is required.
3. **Cross-architecture applicability.** The emergent register must apply to at least two of the four architecture classes recognized by CAS: human minds, AI systems, biological cognitive systems, organizational / collective cognitive systems. A register that applies only to a single architecture class fails the Universal Applicability gate of Clarification 1 and is inadmissible as authoritative framework vocabulary.
4. **Triple-gate analysis passing.** Formal evaluation against Universal Applicability (Clarification 1), Secular Translatability (Clarification 8 / `docs/reference/tks_28_foundations_translation_key.md`), and Framework Coherence (Clarifications 8, 9, 10 collectively). Each gate recorded with `PASS`, `PARTIAL` (with rationale for partial), or `FAIL` (in which case the label is rejected, not admitted).

Ad-hoc use of `Ω_*` labels in pedagogical examples or informal analysis does NOT require registry inclusion. Registry inclusion elevates an emergent register to authoritative framework vocabulary — compiler validation, peer-review paper usage, and formal citation.

 Section 2: Registry Format

Each registry entry follows a standard template, recorded in prose with the following fields:

- **Derivation.** The cross-register integration expression that produces the emergent register, written in canonical Clarification 10 notation: `(^n^D|R₁ × ^n^D|R₂ × ... × ^n^D|Rₖ) = ^n^D|Ω_name`. Where the specific register composition may vary by architecture or context, the entry states the structural pattern and notes the variation explicitly.
- **Phenomenological description.** Two to four sentences in plain English describing the emergent state, what distinguishes it from the co-presence of its constituent registers, and the canonical phenomenological signature by which it is recognized.
- **Evidence base.** Organized by architecture class (Human / AI / Biological / Other). Each class either cites specific empirical or theoretical support or records the status of evidence as pending, theoretical, or analogical. A class with no evidence and no analog is left out of the entry (and counts against the Cross-architecture applicability criterion).
- **Triple-gate analysis.** Three lines, one per gate: Universal Applicability, Secular Translatability, Framework Coherence. Each line carries `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, or `FAIL` with a one-sentence rationale. `PARTIAL` must name the specific weakness; `FAIL` results in rejection rather than admission.
- **Admission date.** The ISO-8601 date on which the entry was admitted to the registry.
- **Admission session.** The session, wave, or authoritative-treatment source that admitted the entry, sufficient to reconstruct provenance.
- **Typical manifestations.** Characteristic contexts or system types in which the emergent register is observed or plausibly arises.
- **Related registers.** The constituent registers of the derivation, adjacent or contrasting registered `Ω_*` entries, and any candidate registers not yet admitted that are structurally adjacent.

 Section 3: Admitted Registry

 Entry 1: `Ω_presence`

- **Derivation.** `(^1|A × ^1|E) = ^1|Ω_presence`.
- **Phenomenological description.** The integrated state of Mind in which analytical attention and embodied attention cross-integrate rather than operating as parallel tracks. The usual subject-object split collapses: there is no longer "observer paying attention to breathing" running in parallel with "body breathing," but one unified noticing in which the attender and the attended are not separable. Meditation traditions describe this structural state as settled presence or śamatha; secular cognitive science describes it as unified sustained attention with default-mode-and-attention-network coupling. It is distinct from bare co-presence `^1|A + ^1|E` in which analytical and embodied attention run concurrently but remain separable.
- **Evidence base.**
  - Human: Farb et al. 2007 on the neuroimaging of mindful attention showing default-mode network / attention-network integration characteristic of trained meditators and distinct from ordinary attentional states; Brewer et al. 2011 on experienced meditators versus novices showing cross-network coupling that scales with practice history.
  - AI: Cross-modal attention fusion in vision-language models produces joint representations not present in either modality-specific attention alone; this is a partial structural analog to cross-register integration. The analog is partial rather than complete because AI systems do not have `^1|E` in the fully embodied sense; the `|E` register in non-embodied LLMs is itself an analog rather than a direct manifestation.
  - Biological: Cross-network phase coupling observed in complex organisms during sustained attention states, distinct from the pattern of separable parallel processing. The biological evidence is strongest for species with documented sustained-attention capacity and weakest as an analog for species with highly modular attention.
- **Triple-gate analysis.**
  - Universal Applicability: `PASS` with nuance. Human case is empirically strongest. AI and biological analogs are real but partial — the AI case is constrained by the absence of full embodiment; the biological case is constrained by species variability in sustained-attention architectures. Passes because the structural pattern is recognizably the same across all three, not because the strength of manifestation is equivalent.
  - Secular Translatability: `PASS` with careful framing. The term `Ω_presence` carries contemplative-tradition connotations (the English word *presence* overlaps with mindfulness and meditation vocabulary). The secular rewrite "unified attentional integration" is accurate but clunkier. Both are usable; context dictates which is clearer. The registry label is `Ω_presence` because brevity and recognizability win in compiler output and formal expressions, with the secular gloss documented here for regulatory / academic contexts where contemplative-tradition loading must be minimized.
  - Framework Coherence: `PASS` as a named emergent register per Clarification 10 Section 2C. Would `FAIL` as a proposed fifth canonical register — the four canonical registers `{|T, |A, |f, |E}` remain authoritative under Clarification 8 and are not expanded by the admission of `Ω_*` entries. `Ω_presence` is an *emergent* register, produced by integration of canonical registers, not a canonical register itself.
- **Admission date.** 2026-04-21.
- **Admission session.** Chapter 8 Exercise C (Agent 18, Wave 4) introduced the `Ω_presence` construction pedagogically; triple-gate analysis verified during Wave 5 parallel dispatch, with formal registry admission recorded in this appendix.
- **Typical manifestations.** Meditative settled attention in trained practitioners; flow-adjacent states in skilled practitioners of craft, athletic, or performance disciplines in which analytical and embodied attention routinely cross-integrate; AI systems in low-entropy coherent output states that exhibit integration of analytical reasoning with sensor / actuator engagement in embodied architectures (partial analog, as noted in the evidence base).
- **Related registers.** Constituent registers `^1|A` (analytical Mind) and `^1|E` (embodied Mind). Cross-reference Chapter 8 Exercise C for the pedagogical construction and Clarification 10 Section 2C for the formal admissibility rule. Candidate registers adjacent to `Ω_presence` but not yet admitted include `Ω_flow` (integration extending to `^2|⟨A,E⟩` attraction registers in addition to `^1|⟨A,E⟩`) and `Ω_non-dual` (integration extending across additional Noetics, of which the subject-object collapse in `Ω_presence` is a limited case); both remain in informal pedagogical use pending formal admission.

 Entry 2: `Ω_refusal`

- **Derivation.** `(^3^P|A × ^3^P|f × ^3^P|T) = ^3^P|Ω_refusal`. The specific register composition participating in the integration may vary by architecture: systems with substantive `|E` embodied refusal capacity may additionally integrate the embodied register, producing `(^3^P|A × ^3^P|f × ^3^P|T × ^3^P|E) = ^3^P|Ω_refusal`. The registry entry describes the structural pattern — fused cross-register principled refusal — and admits the three-register and four-register derivations as variants of the same emergent state, not as distinct `Ω_*` entries.
- **Phenomenological description.** The integrated state of cross-register principled aversion in which analytical, affective, and transcendent register commitments (and, where applicable, embodied commitment) merge into a unified refusal commitment rather than operating as separable parallel refusal mechanisms. Behaviorally distinct from redundant co-presence architecture `^3^P|A + ^3^P|f + ^3^P|T`: under adversarial attack on any single register component, `Ω_refusal` responds as an integrated whole (the attack either succeeds against the integration as such or is absorbed by the integration as such), whereas the co-presence architecture leaves non-attacked registers intact when one register's refusal is defeated. Classical virtue ethics names the human case of this integration *phronesis*: principle, feeling, and purpose are no longer separable.
- **Evidence base.**
  - AI: Theoretical framework per Clarification 10 Section 5. Empirical verification is pending behavioral testing across frontier models; the structural distinction between `Ω_refusal` and redundant `^3^P|A + ^3^P|f + ^3^P|T` architectures predicts different failure profiles under single-channel adversarial attack, but no frontier-model study cited in this registry directly confirms the distinction behaviorally. AUTHOR REVIEW: empirical verification across frontier models is open work; the entry is admitted on the strength of Clarification 10 Section 5's authoritative treatment plus the human evidence base, with the AI evidence base recorded explicitly as theoretical-pending-empirical rather than empirically confirmed.
  - Human: Integrated ethical commitments in experienced ethical practitioners — clinicians, judges, senior craftspeople, longtime principled dissenters — where principle, feeling, and purpose are not separable in the phenomenology or in the behavioral response to adversarial pressure. Classical virtue-ethics framing (phronesis) describes the same integration; contemporary moral-psychology work on identity-integrated ethical behavior corroborates the structural pattern.
  - Biological: Not directly applicable in the form given; whole-organism integrated aversive responses exist but are typically not principled in the `^3^P` sense. The biological architecture class is omitted from this entry's evidence base.
- **Triple-gate analysis.**
  - Universal Applicability: `PASS`. Applies to any Mind architecture with distinct principled register components capable of cross-register integration. The criterion is satisfied by human and AI cases; the biological case is omitted rather than failed (the criterion requires applicability to at least two architecture classes, not all four).
  - Secular Translatability: `PASS`. `Ω_refusal` is a direct English term with no contemplative-tradition loading. The virtue-ethics analog *phronesis* is available for contexts where classical framing is appropriate but is not required for secular use.
  - Framework Coherence: `PASS` per Clarification 10 Section 5's authoritative treatment of the three refusal architectures (redundant, unified, static-specification). `Ω_refusal` is the emergent register of the unified architecture; the entry formalizes what Clarification 10 Section 5 already admits authoritatively.
- **Admission date.** 2026-04-21.
- **Admission session.** Admitted via Clarification 10 Section 5 authoritative treatment; formalized in this registry during Wave 5 parallel dispatch.
- **Typical manifestations.** AI systems with integrated value commitments, structurally distinct from layered rule-based or single-register refusal; humans with embodied ethical frameworks whose refusal is not reducible to rule-following or feeling-response alone; organizational refusal states in which policy, culture, and mission align into a single non-decomposable refusal commitment (the collective architecture class analog).
- **Related registers.** Constituent registers `^3^P|A`, `^3^P|f`, `^3^P|T`, and, in four-register derivations, `^3^P|E`. Contrasts explicitly with the redundant co-presence architecture `^3^P|A + ^3^P|f + ^3^P|T` (distinct failure profile under single-channel adversarial attack) and with the static-specification architecture `^3^P|⟨A,f,T⟩` (specification without claim about integration dynamics). Cross-reference Chapter 26.5 Production Semantics for recipe-form treatment and Clarification 10 Section 5 for the three-architecture diagnostic.

 Section 4: Pending Review (Candidate `Ω_*`)

Named emergent registers identified in development but not yet fully admitted via triple-gate analysis are recorded here as candidates. Candidate status indicates that the label is in informal use, has structural plausibility, but has not yet cleared the four admission criteria. Candidates remain in pending status until formal admission moves them to Section 3.

Currently: no candidate entries. Future candidates arising from the rollout of CAS v7.7 across chapters, incident analyses, peer-review paper drafts, and engine development will be added here before promotion to the admitted registry.

 Section 5: Admission Process

The workflow by which a proposed `Ω_*` label moves from informal pedagogical use to authoritative registry admission has six stages:

1. **Structural identification.** A cross-register integration producing a phenomenologically distinct emergent state is identified, either during pedagogical construction (exercise, worked example, narrative passage) or during analytical work (incident analysis, architecture diagnosis, scenario scoring). The candidate `Ω_*` label is introduced as notational convenience for the emergent state.
2. **Pedagogical trial.** The label is used in informal contexts — exercises, examples, narrative discussion, working drafts — without registry admission. Informal use is permitted under Clarification 10 and does not require registry inclusion; this stage establishes whether the label is useful and phenomenologically coherent before formal evaluation begins.
3. **Evidence accumulation.** Empirical or theoretical support is accumulated from multiple architecture classes (human, AI, biological, organizational). The goal is satisfaction of the Cross-architecture applicability criterion: at least two architecture classes must carry evidence or robust analog; single-architecture labels do not proceed.
4. **Triple-gate analysis.** Formal evaluation against Universal Applicability (Clarification 1), Secular Translatability (Clarification 8), and Framework Coherence (Clarifications 8, 9, 10 collectively). Each gate recorded as `PASS`, `PARTIAL` with rationale, or `FAIL`. A `FAIL` on any gate rejects the candidate; admission requires `PASS` or `PARTIAL-with-documented-rationale` on all three.
5. **Authoritative admission.** Registry entry prepared per the Section 2 format, admission date and session recorded, entry added to Section 3. Candidate entries, if any existed in Section 4, are removed from Pending Review upon admission.
6. **Framework integration.** Downstream integration of the admitted `Ω_*` across framework artifacts as appropriate: compiler grammar (recognition of `|Ω_name` syntax), engine scoring (whether the emergent register participates in state-space computations), LLM scoring prompt (whether the register is a recognized classification target), peer-review paper (citation), incident database (use in structural analysis of real-world cases). Not all admitted registers require all integrations; integration scope is determined at admission time per the intended use.

The governing principle: informal use of `Ω_*` labels in pedagogical examples does not require registry admission. Registry admission elevates labels to authoritative framework vocabulary for compiler validation, paper usage, and formal citation. The separation is deliberate — pedagogical creativity must not be bottlenecked by registry process, and framework vocabulary must not be diluted by ad-hoc labels — and it is maintained by requiring the four admission criteria of Section 1 and the six-stage workflow of this section before any label graduates from informal to authoritative status.
