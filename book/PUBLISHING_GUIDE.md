## 1. ISBN Application Guidance

To commercially publish the book, you will need International Standard Book Numbers (ISBNs). 

**Key Rules for ISBNs:**
- You need a **separate ISBN for every format** of the book (e.g., Paperback, Hardcover, EPUB/MOBI eBook, PDF, Audiobook).
- If you use a free ISBN provided by Amazon KDP, that ISBN is locked to Amazon and cannot be used for wide distribution (e.g., Barnes & Noble, IngramSpark).
- **Recommendation:** Buy your own block of 10 ISBNs from Bowker (if in the US, via myidentifiers.com) or your national ISBN agency. This ensures *you* are listed as the publisher of record across all platforms.

**Steps to Register:**
1. Purchase a block of 10 ISBNs.
2. Assign one ISBN to the Paperback, one to the Hardcover (if applicable), and one to the eBook.
3. Register the title, author (Little Henderson), description, and cover image in the Bowker database so it propagates to global booksellers.

## 2. Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing) Setup Instructions

Amazon KDP is the fastest route for self-publishing the book in paperback and Kindle formats.

**Preparation:**
- **Manuscript:** Compile the final DOCX or PDF for the Paperback interior. Ensure margins account for the "gutter" (the inside spine fold). Export an EPUB format for the Kindle version.
- **Cover:** You need a front-only image (JPG) for the Kindle version, and a full wrap-around PDF (Back + Spine + Front) for the print version.

**KDP Setup Steps:**
1. **Create Account:** Go to kdp.amazon.com and log in/create an account. Complete the tax and banking interviews.
2. **Create a New Title:** Click "+ Create" and select "Kindle eBook" or "Paperback".
3. **Details Page:** 
   - **Title/Subtitle:** *Thinking in Cognitive Architecture: How to Read, Build, and Apply the System That Maps How Minds Actually Work — Artificial and Human*
   - **Author:** Little Henderson
   - **Description:** Use the back cover copy generated in Phase 4.
   - **Keywords & Categories:** Select categories like `Computers > Artificial Intelligence`, `Psychology > Cognitive Psychology`, and `Mathematics > Applied`. 
4. **Content Page:** 
   - Upload your ISBN (or select Amazon's free one if you prefer).
   - Upload your manuscript file and cover.
   - **Launch the Previewer:** Carefully check the print preview for formatting errors, bleed issues, or text entering the margins.
5. **Pricing Page:** Select your distribution rights (Worldwide) and set your list price. Amazon will calculate your royalty (typically 70% for eBooks between $2.99-$9.99, and 60% minus printing costs for paperbacks).
6. **Publish:** Click "Publish Your Paperback/eBook." It typically takes 24-72 hours to go live on Amazon.
