CAS Component Mathematics

Forty-two circuit parts, each reduced to one electrical verb, one cognitive verb, and one thing that happens to people. Learn the verb pair and you have the part.

Read the badge before you trust the mapping

ISOExact structural isomorphism. Functional roles correspond one-for-one and the governing relation has the same form. Four parts earn this.
ENGEngineered analog. The role match is explicit, but a cognitive quantity, control input, or conservation rule is stipulated by the design. Fifteen parts.
PEDPedagogical analogy. The resemblance teaches. No quantitative relation transfers. One part, and it is fenced.

The badge grades the mapping, not the existence of the part. Every parameter here is a design choice — a setting you specify, like picking a 50-ohm resistor. None is a measurement of a person, an AI model, or a biological system. Logic and annotation entries carry a plate-level badge rather than an individual one.

S5

Logic and routing

11 · plate-level ENG
21AND—
require all/detect all-active prerequisites or conflict

Keys, wallet and phone all in hand, or you are still standing at the door.

22OR—
require any/accept one or more thresholded flags

Raining, or sore, or running late — any one of them cancels the run.

23NOT—
complement/negate one explicit firing flag

Do Not Disturb does not change who is calling. It turns one permission from yes to no.

24NAND—
reject all-active/invert an AND result

The seatbelt chime keeps sounding until every passenger is buckled. One unbuckled seat keeps it going.

25NOR—
require none/accept only when every flag is inactive

You drift off only when nothing is nagging — no unsent email, no unlocked door, no argument left hanging.

26XOR—
distinguish/accept exactly one active flag

Only one of you can take the last shift. If you both claim it, or neither does, the rota is broken.

27XNOR—
agree/detect equal flags, including both-inactive

You both want takeaway, or you both do not — either way, no argument. Only a split between you starts one.

28MUX—
choose/route one selected input to one output

Your friend’s bad news could get a joke, a fix, or a hug. What you are feeling picks which one comes out.

DMX
29DEMUX—
dispatch/send one input to one selected output

A complaint reaches the front desk and goes to exactly one department — and still has to land somewhere.

30Relay—
isolate and switch/let Cause control a separate Effect path

A quiet word to the manager and a whole shift’s rota changes. Your voice never moved the rota; it closed a switch that did.

31Schmitt trigger—
commit and hold/prevent threshold chatter

Once you finally decide to leave the party, you stay decided. A good song comes on and it does not pull you back.

Your friend says the room is cold. You ask: cold by the thermostat, or cold compared to your last flat?

40Net label—

GUARD

Six ways this goes wrong

check before you classify
  1. ^3 Negative is repulsion. ^6 Male is decomposition. Auditing or breaking down an argument is ^6 — never ^3, however critical it feels.
  2. ^8 Above/Cause is associative triggering, not reasoning. It brings a different Idea to Mind. The thing that arrives is ^9 Below/Effect.
  3. ^5 Female is composition, not a container. The Mind is what it accumulated. It is not a box holding experiences.
  4. A part is a role, not a Noetic. One part may act on Noetic, Domain, Drive, Register, or a board-level control quantity. Never force one component onto one Noetic.
  5. Reference is an engineered zero. Calling a cognitive rest state ^0 Idea is a convention you chose, not an identity.
  6. AND detects both-active. XOR is exactly-one. XNOR includes both-inactive. A both-active conflict detector is AND — reaching for XOR here is the common slip.

What this is. A carry-sized companion to the CAS PCBA Engineer material — the 42 frozen component and annotation entries, each with its verb pair, its mapping grade, and one everyday example. It is a teaching and design reference, not evidence.

What the numbers are. Every parameter shown — β, τ, thresholds, dial ranges — is a design choice you would specify in a schematic. None is a measurement of a person, an AI model, or a biological system, and none is a benchmark result.

Domain colours. P Principled · C Cognitive · E Evaluative · O Operational. Colour reinforces; the labels and shapes carry the claim, so every plate survives greyscale printing.

Source. Verb pairs, grades and circuit parameters are taken from the Complete Memory Deck and plates S1–S6 of the CAS Component Companion. The everyday examples are new here, and were drift-checked against the six guardrails above.
