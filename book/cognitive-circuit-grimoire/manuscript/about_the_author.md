# About the Author

**Little Henderson** is the creator of the Cognitive Architecture System
(CAS) and the developer of Noetic Compass, a cross-model AI assessment and
interpretability platform. His work joins systems architecture, mathematical
formalisation, cognitive modelling, and the practical discipline of inspecting
hardware against a specification.

Henderson writes under the name **Prof. Hermes M. Thoth** for work connecting
CAS with the Tootra Kabbalistic System. His companion books are *Thinking in
Cognitive Architecture*, *CAS PCBA Engineer*, and *Reality Engineering*.

CAS was first filed in the United States as Provisional Patent Application
No. 64/037,173 on April 13, 2026. The filing is a record of the framework at
that date; later CAS extensions are later work and are not represented here as
covered by that provisional.

He publishes through **Noetic Compass Press**.
