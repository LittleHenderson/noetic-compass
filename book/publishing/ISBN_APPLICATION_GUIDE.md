# ISBN Application Guide for *Thinking in Cognitive Architecture*

## What Is an ISBN?

An International Standard Book Number (ISBN) is a unique 13-digit identifier assigned to each edition and format of a book. You need separate ISBNs for:
- **Print paperback** (1 ISBN)
- **Print hardcover** (1 ISBN)
- **eBook / Kindle** (1 ISBN — though Amazon assigns a free ASIN for Kindle)
- **PDF edition** (1 ISBN if sold separately)

## How to Obtain ISBNs

### Option A: Purchase from Bowker (US — Recommended)
- **Website:** https://www.myidentifiers.com
- **Pricing (2026):**
  - 1 ISBN: ~$125
  - 10 ISBNs: ~$295 (best value for multi-format publishing)
  - 100 ISBNs: ~$575
- **Turnaround:** Instant digital delivery after purchase
- **Publisher name:** You set your own imprint name (e.g., "Noetic Press" or "Henderson Publishing")

### Option B: Free ISBN via Amazon KDP
- Amazon provides a **free ISBN** if you publish exclusively through KDP
- **Drawback:** The publisher of record is "Independently Published" — not your imprint
- **Recommendation:** Purchase your own ISBNs if you plan to distribute beyond Amazon

### Option C: Country-Specific Agencies
- **UK:** Nielsen (https://www.nielsenisbnstore.com)
- **Canada:** Library and Archives Canada (free ISBNs for Canadian publishers)
- **Other countries:** Check your national ISBN agency at https://www.isbn-international.org

## Application Steps (Bowker / US)

1. **Create an account** at myidentifiers.com
2. **Purchase ISBN(s)** — recommend the 10-pack for multi-format
3. **Register your imprint name** (your publishing company name)
4. **Assign ISBNs to each format:**
   - Paperback: ISBN-13 #1
   - Hardcover: ISBN-13 #2
   - eBook: ISBN-13 #3
5. **Provide metadata:**
   - **Title:** Thinking in Cognitive Architecture
   - **Subtitle:** A Formal Language for the Human and Artificial Mind
   - **Author:** Little Henderson
   - **Publication date:** [Target date]
   - **Page count:** ~350-400 (estimated at 250 words/page for 64,000+ words)
   - **BISAC subject codes:**
     - COMPUTERS / Artificial Intelligence / General
     - PSYCHOLOGY / Cognitive Psychology & Cognition
     - SCIENCE / Cognitive Science
     - MATHEMATICS / Applied
   - **Language:** English
   - **Country of publication:** United States

## Barcode

- After assigning an ISBN, purchase a **barcode** from Bowker ($25) or generate one free via Amazon KDP
- The barcode encodes the ISBN-13 and the retail price
- Required for physical bookstore distribution

## Library of Congress Control Number (LCCN)

- Apply **before** publication at https://www.loc.gov/publish/pcn/
- Free for US publishers
- Ensures your book is cataloged in the Library of Congress
- Apply 1-2 months before publication date

## Copyright Registration

- Register at https://www.copyright.gov ($65 online filing fee)
- Not legally required (copyright exists automatically upon creation), but registration provides legal standing for infringement claims
- Register the text as a "Literary Work"
