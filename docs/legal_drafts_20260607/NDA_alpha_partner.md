# NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**(One-Way — Recipient Obligations; convertible to mutual per Section 11)**

- **Noetic Compass** (also known as Noetic Compass AI), with a principal place of business at `[Company Address]` (the "**Company**" or "**Disclosing Party**"); and
- **`[Recipient Full Name]`**, an individual, on behalf of `[Recipient Entity, if any]` (the "**Recipient**").

The Company and the Recipient are referred to individually as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**."

## Background

The Company is developing and operating an artificial-intelligence model-audit product known as the **CAS Harness**, delivered on the **CAS Compass** platform (cascompass.ai), built on the Company's proprietary **Cognitive Architecture System (CAS)** framework. The Recipient wishes to receive certain confidential information from the Company in order to evaluate a potential business, commercial, or alpha-partnership relationship between the Parties (the "**Purpose**"). This Agreement is entered into **before** any such confidential information is disclosed, so that the Company's confidential information is protected at and from the moment of first disclosure. The Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any and all non-public information that the Company (directly or through its representatives) discloses or makes available to the Recipient, in any form or medium — whether written, oral, electronic, visual, or otherwise, and whether or not marked or identified as "confidential" — in connection with the Purpose. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

- (a) the CAS framework, including its concepts, structures, methods, definitions, notation, and underlying theory;
- (b) the CAS Harness product and the CAS Compass platform, including their design, architecture, features, interfaces, and operation;
- (c) the Company's audit, scoring, evaluation, and analysis methodology, and any related techniques, models, algorithms, or processes;
- (d) any specification sheet, technical document, datasheet, or other descriptive material concerning the foregoing (the "**Spec Sheet**");
- (e) product, technical, and research roadmaps and development plans;
- (f) business, commercial, financial, pricing, customer, partner, and strategic information; and
- (g) alpha, pilot, evaluation, or pre-release results, findings, metrics, and outputs.

Confidential Information also includes the fact that the Spec Sheet and other materials exist, their contents, and any notes, analyses, summaries, or derivative materials prepared by the Recipient that contain, reflect, or are derived from any of the foregoing.

The Company's methods are **proprietary and patent-pending**. Nothing in this Agreement shall be read as a representation by the Company that any patent has issued or that any particular intellectual-property protection is in force.

## 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information does **not** include information that the Recipient can demonstrate by competent written records:

- (a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of any act or omission of the Recipient or its representatives in breach of this Agreement;
- (b) was rightfully known to the Recipient, free of any obligation of confidentiality, before its disclosure by the Company;
- (c) is rightfully received by the Recipient from a third party who, to the Recipient's knowledge, is not under any obligation of confidentiality to the Company with respect to that information; or
- (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of, reference to, or reliance on the Confidential Information.

**Compelled disclosure.** If the Recipient is required by law, regulation, or valid legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Recipient may do so only to the extent legally required, and shall (to the extent legally permitted) give the Company prompt prior written notice so that the Company may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, and shall reasonably cooperate (at the Company's expense) with any such effort. Information disclosed under this paragraph remains Confidential Information for all other purposes.

## 3. Recipient's Obligations

The Recipient shall:

- (a) **use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose** — namely, to evaluate the potential relationship between the Parties — and for no other purpose;
- (b) hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence and protect it using **at least the same degree of care** the Recipient uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than a **reasonable degree of care**;
- (c) **not copy, reproduce, or duplicate** any Confidential Information except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose, and not remove or obscure any proprietary or confidentiality notices;
- (d) **not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive** the underlying structure, methods, source, or design of any Confidential Information, product, or methodology, or to recreate any part of the CAS framework, the CAS Harness, or the audit/scoring methodology;
- (e) disclose Confidential Information only to those of its directors, officers, employees, affiliates, and professional advisors who have a **bona fide need to know** it for the Purpose, who are informed of its confidential nature, and who are bound by confidentiality obligations (whether by contract, professional duty, or this Agreement) at least as protective as those in this Agreement; and
- (f) be **responsible for any breach** of this Agreement by any person to whom it discloses Confidential Information, as if the breach were the Recipient's own.

## 4. No License; No Rights Granted

All Confidential Information remains the sole and exclusive property of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement, and no disclosure made under it, grants or transfers to the Recipient any right, title, interest, license, or ownership — whether by implication, estoppel, or otherwise — in or to any Confidential Information or any patent, copyright, trade secret, trademark, or other intellectual-property right of the Company. No obligation of either Party to proceed with any transaction or relationship is created by this Agreement; any such transaction would be the subject of a separate written agreement.

The Recipient may retain one archival copy solely to the extent required by law or bona fide internal record-retention policy, or copies created automatically by routine electronic backup; any retained material remains subject to this Agreement for as long as it is retained.

## 6. No Warranty

All Confidential Information is provided "**AS IS**." The Company makes **no representation or warranty**, express or implied, as to the accuracy, completeness, performance, or fitness for any purpose of any Confidential Information, and shall have no liability to the Recipient resulting from the Recipient's use of or reliance on the Confidential Information. Without limiting the foregoing, the Company does not warrant or guarantee any particular audit result, score, or outcome, and nothing disclosed shall be construed as a certification or guarantee of any AI system's behavior.

## 7. Term and Survival

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for a term of `[three (3)]` years, unless earlier terminated by either Party on `[thirty (30)]` days' written notice. ## 8. Remedies; Injunctive Relief

The Recipient acknowledges that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement may cause the Company **irreparable harm** for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, the Company shall be entitled to seek **injunctive relief** and **specific performance** to enforce this Agreement, **without** the necessity of posting a bond or proving actual damages. The prevailing Party in any action to enforce this Agreement shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 9. No Publicity

The Recipient shall **not disclose to any third party** the existence of this Agreement, the fact that discussions or negotiations are taking place between the Parties, the status or terms of any such discussions, or that the Recipient has received or reviewed any Confidential Information, in each case without the Company's prior written consent — except as permitted under the compelled-disclosure provisions of Section 2. Neither Party shall use the other Party's name, logo, or trademarks in any public statement, advertising, or marketing without prior written consent.
