## 8. Honest-disclosure reminders (do not delete these from your process)

- A patent application protects only what it **teaches**, and the non-provisional inherits the 04/13/2026 date **only for matter the provisional already taught**. Trade-secret internals (exact probe construction, fixture design, scoring weights) can be described at a **functional** level while the precise configuration stays out — but undescribed specifics do **not** get the priority date, and post-04/13/2026 additions get no earlier date than their own filing.
- **Do not overclaim.** "Patent pending" and a priority date are not an examined, enforceable patent. The anchoring mechanism is **tamper-evident**, not immutable. No `ACCEPT` verdict stands. Mechanism I is a **prototype**. Effect-size numbers must match your canonical files and must be labeled offline-validation where that is what they are.
