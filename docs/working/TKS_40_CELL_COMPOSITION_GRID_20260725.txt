===== A — Atziluth / Spiritual =====
A1   Mind                 Purest awareness, direct intuitive knowing (gnosis), connection to the collective unconscious or divine intelligence. This isn't analytical thought; it's direct apprehension of essence.
A2   Positive             Pure affinity, unconditional love, the drive towards unity with Source.
A3   Negative             The principle of separation or differentiation at the highest level, the definition of an archetype against the Void.
A4   Vibration            The highest, subtlest frequencies, the resonance of pure potential.
A5   Female               The receptive Void, the matrix of pure potential from which archetypes arise, ultimate sensitivity.
A6   Male                 The inherent structure or definition of an archetype or spiritual law itself.
A7   Rhythm               Cosmic cycles, the great ages (Yugas), the pulse of creation and dissolution.
A8   Spiritual Above      The Big Why. Archetypes, divine whispers, core purpose, esoteric laws. This is the prime mover stuff. Example: The deep-seated purpose (A8) to alleviate suffering triggers the lifelong path (A9/B9/C9/D9) of becoming a doctor.
A9   Spiritual Below      A specific spiritual state achieved, a symbolic vision received, a feeling of connection/disconnection manifested. Example: The feeling of oneness (A9) resulting from (A8/B8) meditation.
A10  Spiritual Idea       The raw essence, the archetype, the spiritual state itself (e.g., 'Oneness', 'Void', 'Love').

===== B — Briah / Mental =====
B1   Mental Mind          Where the ego lies, all memories and past experiences that have contributed to the personality. Processing information for the sake of continuously coming to conclusions, strictly analytical. Decision and action driven by stoical logic.
B2   Mental Positive      Mental stability, mental clarity, optimistic outlook, sane and enlightened thought, benevolent, altruistic thinking.
B3   Mental Negative      Confusion, indecision. A thought that disturbs one's mental state, cognitive dissonance, insanity, pessimistic outlook, malicious thinking.
B4   Mental Vibration     The energy resonating from one's mind, psychic energy. Manifesting as electromagnetic waves emitted by the brain. Indicates psychic states (alpha, beta, delta, theta). The degree with which a thought resonates with the mind.
B5   Mental Female        Right brain thinking, open mindedness, imaginative, creative, the subconscious mind.
B6   Mental Male          Left brain thinking, rigid, closed minded, logical, and critical, the conscious mind.
B7   Mental Rhythm        The ups and downs, patterns, or sequences of mental activity. The ebb and flow of cognitive engagement, clarity, and distraction.
B8   Mental Above         The conscious plan, the strategic objective, the abstract principle guiding thought, the hypothesis you're testing. It's the mental blueprint or directive. Example: The strategic decision (B8) to pivot the company triggers a cascade of planning meetings (B9) and operational changes (D9).
B9   Mental Below         The specific thought, memory, conclusion, or simpler idea generated by a mental trigger. Example: The answer 'Paris' (B9) triggered by the question 'Capital of France?' (B8).
B10  Mental Idea          An idea perceived as being exclusively of the mind, manifested solely in the mental world. The mental aspect of a thing.

===== C — Yetzirah / Emotional =====
C1   Emotional Mind       The heart, the center of feelings and emotions, where the soul lies, processing information strictly based on feelings, intuition, and instinct. Decision and action driven strictly by emotion.
C2   Emotional Positive   Emotional stability, happiness, joy, pleasure, excitement, passion, love, compassion, empathy, courage, hope, faith, optimism, motivation, inspiration.
C3   Emotional Negative   Emotional instability, sadness, fear, anxiety, anger, hatred, jealousy, shame, guilt, depression, despair, loneliness.
C4   Emotional Vibration  Emotional energy, the atmosphere or mood of a place or person, the feeling one gets from a person/place/thing, the vibe.
C5   Emotional Female     Compassion, sympathy, unconditional acceptance, sensuality. The capacity to receive and hold emotional content.
C6   Emotional Male       Passion, emotion in motion, desire, emotional drive, emotional expression, transmitting emotional influence.
C7   Emotional Rhythm     Mood swings, emotional cycles, the ebb and flow of feelings, emotional habits and patterns.
C8   Emotional Above      Core values acting as motivators, a deliberately cultivated emotional state used to influence subsequent states or actions. Example: Invoking a feeling of calm (C8) before a stressful presentation (D9) to maintain composure (C9). The value (C8) of honesty triggering the difficult conversation (D9).
C9   Emotional Below      The specific feeling or mood shift that results from a trigger. Example: Feeling relief (C9) triggered by receiving good news (D8).
C10  Emotional Idea       The specific feeling, mood, desire (e.g., 'Happiness', 'Fear', 'Longing').

===== D — Deha / Physical =====
D1   Physical Mind        The brain, the nervous system, the body's sensory awareness center, processing physical information and sensation.
D2   Physical Positive    Health, vitality, physical attraction, smooth functioning, physical harmony, growth, strength.
D3   Physical Negative    Illness, decay, destruction, physical obstacles, friction, weakness, disease.
D4   Physical Vibration   Physical energy, intensity of physical sensation (loud sound, bright light, strong impact), kinetic force.
D5   Physical Female      A woman, the female of a species, an orifice, hole, receptacle, receiver, the womb. Physical receptivity and structure.
D6   Physical Male        A man, the male of a species, a protrusion, pillar, giver, transmitter, the phallus. Physical action and technique.
D7   Physical Rhythm      Physical cycles (heartbeat, breathing, sleep), routines, timing, seasons, natural rhythms.
D8   Physical Above       The tangible trigger. A ringing alarm clock, a red light, a direct order from someone with authority, a physical law dictating consequences. Example: The loud bang (D8) triggers a startle response (D9/C9). Gravity (D8) triggers the falling (D9) of a dropped apple.
D9   Physical Below       The specific action performed, sensation felt, object produced, or bodily response manifested. Example: Pressing the 'send' button (D9) triggered by finishing an email (B8).
D10  Physical Idea        The specific object, action, sensation, physical state (e.g., 'This chair', 'The act of walking', 'The feeling of warmth', 'The state of illness').
