# Examples: a ground ingredient and a mastered, shock-absorbing prerequisite

## Ground ingredients (raw prerequisite leaves)

Raw stock. Nothing in HELM's world makes it. The cascade stops there and reads the stored amount, checked
against its receipt.

1. **Flour** for Bread. You have 12 cups. No recipe produces flour.
2. **Water** for Bread. It comes from the tap.
3. **Eggs** for Meringue. You count them; you don't cook them up.

## Mastered, shock-absorbing prerequisites (decoupled)

Each is made by its own recipe, and you have far more than the next step needs.

1. **Dough for loaves.** A loaf needs 1 batch of dough and 1 spoon of salt. You have dough for 6 loaves but salt for 2.
   If a dough batch comes out a little short, you still make 2 loaves. The extra dough absorbs the shock.
2. **Starter for a sponge.** You have starter for 10 sponges and sugar for 3. A weak batch of starter changes nothing.
3. **Frosting for a cake.** Frosting for 8 cakes, but only 2 sponges. A spoonful less frosting makes no difference.

All three have been made many times, every record checks out, and you know them well.

**The contrast.** The salt in example 1 is the bottleneck. One spoon short means one loaf fewer. HELM always checks it.

## The CAS reading

- **Ground** = a raw prerequisite leaf: a stock cell with no producing recipe.
- **Decoupled** = Excess Capacity ε (v7.6): the prerequisite is saturated; its ledger cell is COVERED with passing
  receipts; all three faces (Purpose, Wisdom, Power) are at Peak. A small perturbation is absorbed, so the cascade may
  stop there.
- **Coupled** = the Limiting Reagent: it passes a perturbation one-for-one to the outcome, so it is always descended.
