## What this packet is, in plain words

Today the Unity Eye pairs the 7 Foundations (F1 Unity ... F7 Purpose, the "desires") with the 7 CAS faculties
(CURIOSITY, PONDERING, IMAGINATION, VERIFY, ASKING, INTEGRATION, CONSOLIDATE). It learns which faculty pays off at
which Foundation from verifier verdicts only, never writes, and whispers a nudge through the normal gate (RB-I3,
§7.78; wave 6, §7.93 and §7.96: the pairing loop resolved far more of the backlog than the plain loop). The Eye can
only pair what is **filed**. Every unresolved item is filed under one Foundation because that is the only tag a
receipt carries today. Your standing fix G ("redeem 22x40", 2026-10-18) asks for the ledgers to live on the real 40
states, 28 drives and 22 pathways. That is the prerequisite for the wider mixing, so this packet is in two stages:

- **Stage 1 — tags on receipts.** Every receipt and every filed item gets a CAS address: the Foundation (F1-F7), the
  Domain (P, C, E, O), hence the cell of the 28 (7 x 4), the Register (T, A, f, E) where known, and the pathway of
  the 22 where the item is a step in a chain. Tags are written by deterministic rules from what the item already
  says; a tag the rule cannot derive is `UNTAGGED`, never guessed.
- **Stage 2 — the Eye pairs the 28 cells with the 7 faculties** (196 cells in its table instead of 49), with the
  7 x 7 Eye as the baseline it must beat on a sealed exam whose planted backlog is filed by cell.

The 22 pathways and the 40 elements come after, as stages 3 and 4, with the same rule: each new axis must beat the
axis before it on a sealed exam, or it is not kept.
