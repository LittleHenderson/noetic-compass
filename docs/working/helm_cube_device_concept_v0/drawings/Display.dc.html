HELM Cube · another version, not the main one

The display cube: black glass that can light up

Glass all round. Six faces of black Gorilla Glass over square OLED screens. An OLED pixel that is off gives no light, so the rule holds: off, it is a plain black box.
Screens cost power and heat. Six lit faces would use most of the cube's small heat budget, so one or two faces light at a time, the ones facing you. Battery: hours with screens on, not days.
Cameras see worse under a screen. The main version keeps cameras behind plain dark glass. The camera-on light stays wired in hardware here too. Estimates only; not yet researched.
