HELM Cube · the faces

Six faces, and you cannot tell them apart

Unlit, every face is the same dark glass. One of them is the charge face. Nothing marks it: it finds the charger by magnetic pull.

All six faces, unlit, drawn the same. No line, dot, port or mark singles one out.

The charge face (found by feel)
1 Camera. Wide-angle, behind dark glass. Off by default.
2 Camera-on dot. Green, wired to the camera's power. Invisible when off.
3 Depth window. A small time-of-flight sensor for mapping the room.
4 Microphone. A pinhole in the edge seam.
5 Sound slot. A hairline along the edges; reads as the same shadow on every face.
6 Edge light. Light pipes along all four edges, black until lit.
7 Magnet ring and coil. Qi2 magnetic wireless charging.
8 Dock sensor. Feels the charger's magnets.
9 Haptic motor. A short buzz when charging starts; a tick per counted tap.
