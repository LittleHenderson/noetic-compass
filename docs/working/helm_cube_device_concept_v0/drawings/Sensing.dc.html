HELM Cube · knowing where it is
depth sensor Wi-Fi range gyroscope turn

It draws its own map, and only from what it measured

Moved and turned. A gyroscope feels every turn; a motion sensor feels every move and which face is down.
How far are the walls. A small depth sensor behind each face times invisible light to the nearest surfaces.
What it sees. The hidden cameras add landmarks, only when a camera is on and its green dot is lit.
Where it is. Wi-Fi gives distances to the router; the phone app can share the home's location.
All of it feeds HELM. HELM decides which sensors run and when to map. Every fact on the map keeps where and when it was measured. Space it has not measured stays undetermined, and HELM asks rather than guesses.
Two lines outside all software. The camera-on light is wired to each camera's power, and the hard mute is a physical switch. HELM can turn any sensor on or off; it cannot hide that it did.
Concept, checked against real parts (spec Section 8a). The depth sensors read up to about 2 m in fine mode and 3.5 m coarse. Wi-Fi gives a distance to the router; drawing walls from Wi-Fi alone is still research.
