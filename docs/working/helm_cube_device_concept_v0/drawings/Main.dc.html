HELM Cube

A 64 mm black cube that listens, talks, sees and maps its room. No buttons. When it is off, it is a plain black box: the same on all six faces.

Shake to wake. Tap to command: the number of taps is the action, the face you tap is the target.
Light along all twelve edges, invisible when off. It pulses with each syllable HELM speaks.
A camera, a microphone and a sound port behind every face, all hidden.
Magnetic wireless charging: one unmarked face snaps to the charger, and a short buzz confirms.
Feels when it is moved and turned, and maps its room with depth sensors and Wi-Fi.
Everything feeds HELM, which runs the cube. The camera light and the hard mute are wired in hardware.
Concept drawing. Nothing has been built. Sizes from the spec: 64 mm target, 55 mm stretch.
