HELM Cube · charging

You find the charge face by feel

Nothing marks the charge face. A Qi2 magnet ring sits behind one face. Near the charger, that face pulls itself down. A magnet sensor inside knows it is docked.
The docked face rests. Its camera and microphone sit against the charger, so they pause. The other five faces keep listening, watching and speaking (the cyan dots).
Heat sets the speed, not the standard. About 14 W at first, likely 7 to 10 W as it warms; it charges slower while HELM talks. These are estimates until a prototype is measured.
