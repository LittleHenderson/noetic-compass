**H1. The fixes (PLAN_MOUTH_V2 F1–F5), unchanged in substance.**
- F1 word list: the lock's everyday words = every word seen in ≥ 2 checked lessons, minus fact words (names, numbers, months: `FACT_WORDS`) and minus the world's own thing and slot nouns from a fixed written list (`WORLD_NOUNS.json`, from the generator's word tables, never from template sentences). **Gate before training: in every situation ≥ 95 % of the lessons are writable under the lock;** a lesson that is not writable is dropped.
- F2 numbers: the detokenizer joins digits only inside one locked number unit, never across two ("40 40" stays two words). Tests first.
- F3 sense check in the guard. Layer 1 (frozen now): a line fails when a word appears twice in a row, or a content word (not in a fixed list of small words) appears 3+ times. Layer 2 (the CTT's own confidence, average log-probability per piece of the line it wrote): a laptop census on the 989 rated c59 lines first; frozen into the guard only if, at one threshold, it rejects at least half of the 162 rater-rejected chat lines that Layer 1 misses while rejecting at most 5 % of rater-preferred lines; otherwise reported and left out. The decision rule is fixed here, before the census.
- F4/F5 more lessons and variety: up to 3 differently worded kept lessons per input.

**H2. Q1 (a): teach the ear the mouth's own sentences.** Every kept fact lesson (ANSWER, SAYBACK, ASK) is also an ear lesson: the line in English → its reading in the ear's own [B] target form, added to the ear's pool in the same run (pool name `EAR_M`; mix MOUTH : CUBE : CAS : EAR_M = 5 : 2 : 2 : 1, each pool sampled in that ratio). G-ROUNDTRIP is read by the same ear and the same reader; the mark stays ≥ 95 %.

**H3. Q2: ASK is scored on thing, value and month.** For an ASK reply, the round trip counts as the same fact when the ear's unanimous read-back gives the same thing, value and month; the old value (`replaces`) is not compared. ANSWER and SAYBACK keep thing, value, month and old value.

**H4. The corpus (owner's request).** All synthetic, all AI-written, all checked; `LLM_WRITTEN_ONLY`.
- **Vocabulary lists:** three reading levels (5th grade, 10th grade, college), about 500 everyday words each, and the working vocabulary of 30 professions (about 25 words each): nurse, electrician, plumber, carpenter, teacher, lawyer, accountant, software developer, truck driver, farmer, mechanic, barber, firefighter, pilot, pharmacist, librarian, architect, musician, photographer, journalist, real-estate agent, construction worker, dentist, veterinarian, social worker, graphic designer, lab scientist, coach, retail cashier, hairstylist. Each list is written by one family and kept word by word only when a second family agrees the word fits the level or the profession. The lists are prompts for the writers; a word reaches the lock only by H1's rule (seen in ≥ 2 checked lessons).
- **Styles:** plain, warm, formal, casual, barbershop (easy banter), intimate (gentle and close), curious, jovial, storyteller, professional. Every new input carries one style and one level; about half carry a profession.
- **Story form:** about a fifth of the new off-topic lines are short stories (2 to 4 sentences, at most 60 words) the person tells; HELM's reply stays 1 to 3 short sentences.
- **Inputs:** about 2,000 new off-topic lines, 500 small talk, 400 about HELM, 400 I-don't-know, and 600 new fact readings, spread evenly over styles and levels. Writers: anthropic (helper agents, file mode), openai, deepseek; google when its free quota allows (no paid key). Each kept reply still needs two other-family checks and no fail, and must pass the H1 lock check and the H1 Layer 1 sense rule.
- **How style reaches the mouth:** the source becomes `[M] ^1 ^C |R [SEP] style <style> level <level> [SEP] <input>` — the style words are English pieces in their own part, outside the input, so the lock never lets the mouth write a style word unless it is an everyday word anyway. Register `|f` for warm, intimate, jovial, barbershop and small talk; `|A` otherwise. No new tokens; the [M] row of v1's surgery is reused.
- **Rules that still hold for every reply:** no new fact, no opinion ("I think", "that's great"…), 1 to 3 short sentences, no recipe words unless the person used them. Style shows in word choice and warmth, never in a judgement.
