**The owner's words (verbatim, 2026-10-22, in order):** "SO WHAT YOU JUST GAVE IT A BUNCH OF TEMPLATE PHRASES TO PARROT??"; "I THOUGHT WE ALREADY HAD A CTT translator WHAT THE FUCK?!"; the button "Yes, build the mouth" (its text: "Yes: start the trained mouth now (CTT trained CAS-to-English from SDG and AI-written pairs, may choose words never facts, templates stay only as the guard), amendment first, test-first; ask me before any money is spent."); and "BECAUSE EVERYONE OF THOSE TEMPLATES NEEDS TO BE PUT THROUGH THE CTT OR NO?"

**The answer recorded:** yes. The CTT has been trained only to read (English → CAS: directions `[A]`, `[N]`, and `[B]` in CUBE TRAIN c57). It has never been taught to write. The talk layer v1 (A-TALK-2) speaks fixed templates; that is a stopgap, not HELM's voice.

**W1. Every reply HELM speaks is written by the CTT mouth.** The situations: SMALL, SELF, OFF, WORLD (the talk layer's kinds), ANSWER (a cube answer), SAYBACK (the brain side's say-back line), ASK (B6's "is that still right?"). Each situation is an input tag of the mouth. The templates of A-TALK-2/-3 and `helm_cube_osc` stay **only** as the fallback when the guard (W5) rejects the mouth's line; the fallback rate is counted per turn and reported (`MOUTH_MOSTLY_TEMPLATE` when above 0.5, Part M.4).

**W2. One model, the same CTT (the owner's Q2 a).** The three voting copies (A4-s1..s3, c57 final weights) learn a fourth direction, `[M]` (mouth), alongside `[B]` (ear). This supersedes Part M.5's "the ears' copies are not shared with the mouth". `[M]` is a new special token appended at the END of the vocabulary, so every existing id stays the same; the shared embedding and the output bias each gain one row, initialised from the mean of the `[A]`, `[N]`, `[B]` rows; the parameter count is the frozen formula at the new vocabulary size (+257 parameters per A4 copy: one 256-wide row plus one bias). A5-s1 learns it too, reported only.

**W3. The mouth's input.** `[M]` + the situation word + the tone tag (`|T`, `|A`, `|f` or `|E`, and a Cognitive-domain state token `^n` with `^C`, from the CAS vocabulary) + `[SEP]` + the content: for ANSWER / SAYBACK / ASK, the reading (thing, slot words, value, replaces) as English pieces; for SMALL / SELF / OFF / WORLD, the person's line. The tone tag comes from a frozen written rule over the situation and the line's cue words (the strain / good-news cues of A-TALK-2), never from the model; A-TALK-1 §5 / Part M.8 made the rule the label side. v1's rule is small and published in the package.

**W4. The writing lock: words, never facts.** Output tokens are English pieces and `[EOS]` only, never a CAS token. A piece may be written only when it is in the frozen `FUNCTION_PIECES` list or appears in the mouth's own input. `FUNCTION_PIECES` = the pieces of the mouth's training targets after removing every piece of any name, number word, digit, month and subject noun (the generator's pools, its templates' nouns, the brain's ingredient and product names); published in the package. So a name, number or month can only be copied from the input. Decoding samples (top-p 0.9) with the seed `sha256(turn text + situation)`, so a replay is exact and two different turns may word the same fact differently.

**W5. The guard after the lock (unchanged in rule, Part M.2):** 0 names, numbers or months not in the input (`G-NO-NEW-FACT`); for ANSWER / SAYBACK / ASK, the ear (three copies, unanimous, direction `[B]`) reads the mouth's line back to the same reading (`G-ROUNDTRIP`); `helm_voice.cas_tokens == []`; M1–M10 and the A-TALK-2 guards (no recipe-speak on a non-recipe turn, no opinion, 1–3 sentences, exactly one question on OFF). A line that fails is not said; the template is (W1).

**W6. The lessons (synthetic only, Q1 a).**
- **Most rows are varied English written by AI models**, not templates: for every situation, lines written by at least three model families, each line keyed by two other families (it states only facts in the input, no opinion, fits the situation, and for ANSWER / SAYBACK / ASK the keyers read back the same reading). A line that fails a key is dropped and counted. The 1,879 CUBE TRAIN AI-written statement lines (a person stating a fact) are reused as the content side of ANSWER / SAYBACK targets only where a keyed rewrite into HELM's voice exists.
- **Template rows are capped at 10 % of the mouth's rows** (Part M.5's 60,000 template pairs against 6,000 varied ones is withdrawn: a mouth taught mostly templates would parrot them, which is the owner's objection).
- **The ear keeps its skill:** each batch mixes mouth rows with CUBE TRAIN's `[B]` rows and the kept CAS rows (`[A]`/`[N]`), with fixed shares written in the package before training.
- Writers and keyers: Claude helper agents, Codex (gpt-5.6-terra), Gemini (free tier); DeepSeek only if a family runs out, and only after the owner's click on its cost (expected under 0.20 USD).

**W7. The exam and the gates (pre-registered in the package before training; Part M.4 and the ear-and-mouth design §5):** on held-out inputs never in the lessons: `G-NO-NEW-FACT` = 0; `G-ROUNDTRIP` ≥ 95 %; `G-NO-CAS` = 0; M1–M10; **SOUNDS-HUMAN**: blind raters from the three AI families (labelled `AI_RATED`, Q3 a) prefer the mouth's line to the template on ≥ 60 % of pairs; **VARIED**: at least 3 distinct wordings over 5 sampled turns per input; **TONE** (reported in v1: raters judge whether the line fits its tone tag); the fallback rate (reported); and the ear's own gates unchanged from CUBE TRAIN (no invented word; confident wrong ≤ 1 % / ≤ 2 %; right ≥ 40 % / 25 %; the old skill kept within 0.02). Words: `MOUTH_EARNED_KEEP` / `MOUTH_NO_GAIN` / `MOUTH_HARMED` (any new fact, or the ear harmed) / `INCONCLUSIVE`. Labels `SYNTHETIC_ONLY`, `LLM_WRITTEN_ONLY`, `AI_RATED`.
