Yes, I know exactly what you mean, and you are right about the tone. Let me separate two things so the plan is honest.

Today's four wrong answers were not a wording problem. The brain did not apply a stated fact ("the flour is getting old") to its
arithmetic. No amount of nicer phrasing fixes that. The what of an answer must stay exact and checked: the number, the ingredient,
the "I don't know". That is the part that earned every licence HELM has.

The how of an answer is where you are right, and a transformer belongs there. Today the mouth is templates: the same sentence
shapes every time. That is why it sounds like a recipe machine. A transformer trained on CAS symbols that takes the brain's checked
receipt plus the turn's state among the 40 elements and the register, and writes the English, would give varied, human-sounding
replies with the right tone, without a foreign model inside. It fits the design that already exists: the CTT has CAS symbols on one
side and English on the other, so it can translate both ways. The ears read English into CAS; the mouth writes CAS into English.
The receipt tags built last week already stamp each record with its Foundation, Domain and Register, which is exactly the tone
signal to train on.

The one rule that makes it safe: the mouth may choose words, never facts. Every number and name in its sentence must be copied from
the receipt, the say-back check must still round-trip, and the voice guard rejects any sentence that mentions a fact or amount the
receipt does not hold. If the mouth invents, the guard throws the sentence away and the template speaks instead. That way a
generative mouth can only make HELM sound better, never make it wrong.

How I will put it on the record: as Part M of the talk packet amendment, the mouth half of the same CTT retraining, conditioned on
the 40-state and register tags from receipts, with its own marks (sounded human, varied, right tone by the key, 0 invented facts)
and the templated voice as the baseline it must beat. It fits under your TALK TRAIN at 3 USD line if the budget holds; if the cost
estimate exceeds the cap, I come back to you with the number before anything runs. Nothing is licensed until the reader's word.
