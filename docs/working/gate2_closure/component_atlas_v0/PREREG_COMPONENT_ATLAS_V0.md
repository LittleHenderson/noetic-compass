### 1.0 REV 0.4 — model, dtype, precision policy, budget

- **Model: `EleutherAI/gpt-neox-20b`** (GPTNeoXForCausalLM; 44 blocks, hidden 6144, 64 heads/layer → head_dim 96, MLP intermediate 24576, vocab 50432, 20,554,567,680 parameters, 46 checkpoint shards).   - **What D32 actually fixes, stated exactly (second-pass correction).** D32 was written as closing "the single largest reproducibility gap"; that overstates it. A revision "resolved from the pod's own HF cache" records the identity of **whatever happens to be cached**, and `revision_verified` will then read `true` *by construction*, because `resolved_commit_sha()` reads `config._commit_hash` from that same snapshot. **This fixes provenance RECORDING, not verification against an independently attested commit.** It makes the rev-0.4 run re-identifiable and makes a silent checkpoint swap detectable on a rerun; it does not establish that the cached snapshot is the upstream `main` of `EleutherAI/gpt-neox-20b`, and it does nothing for the 40E record, which remains unpinned. A reviewer may still challenge the 20B lineage on identity.
- **dtype: `bfloat16` weights, ALL measurement math in fp32 (deviation D33).** This overrides rev 0.3 §1's "NOT to be lowered". The resource reason is arithmetic: fp32 weights are 76.74 GiB against the A40's 44.99 GiB (1.71× the card), while bf16 weights are 38.37 GiB = 85.3%, leaving 6.62 GiB — and roughly 5.44 GiB of working headroom after a +0.58 GiB fp32 unembedding and ~0.6 GiB of CUDA context. **Correction to the first draft's arithmetic:** `Module.float()` allocates the fp32 tensor **before** freeing the bf16 one, so promoting the unembedding **peaks at +1.15 GiB, not +0.58 GiB**. - **Load flags, binding.** `AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(model_id, revision=PIN, dtype=torch.bfloat16, device_map={"": 0}, low_cpu_mem_usage=True, use_safetensors=True, attn_implementation="eager", local_files_only=True)`, and **no subsequent `model.to(device)`** — that warns or raises on an accelerate-dispatched model. `device_map={"": 0}` rather than `"auto"`: `"auto"` may decide to CPU-offload tail layers on its own and silently destroy throughput. Without a device_map the rev-0.3 path (`low_cpu_mem_usage=True` then `.to(device)`) would stage all 38.4 GiB in **host** RAM first and can OOM the container before VRAM is ever touched. `attn_implementation="eager"` is **mandatory, not stylistic**: `make_head_zero_prehook` slices the attention out-projection input in concat head layout, which SDPA/flash paths do not expose. `PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` is set **in the launch command** and, as a backstop, at module import **before `torch` is imported** (the first draft set it inside `main()`, which is correct today but fragile against any future import that initializes the CUDA allocator earlier); `torch.cuda.empty_cache()` is called at every stage boundary — ~1,920 `generate()` calls with varying sequence lengths against ~5.4 GiB of headroom is a genuine fragmentation-OOM risk over a multi-hour run.

Still do the human checks `RUNBOOK_POD.md` already prescribes for pythia-2.8b: confirm free host RAM (accelerate buffers one shard at a time even with `device_map`) and budget 3–8 min for the 38.4 GiB read from a network volume (~1 min from local NVMe).
- **MIXED-PRECISION POLICY (binding). Weights bf16; every measurement path fp32.**
  1. `torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_bf16_reduced_precision_reduction = False` (and the fp16 analogue), alongside the rev-0.3 `allow_tf32 = False` on both matmul and cudnn. **This flag defaults to TRUE in PyTorch** and permits a bf16 matmul to ACCUMULATE in bf16 rather than fp32. It is the single highest-value line in this policy: with fp32 accumulation the bf16 dot-product error over 6144 dims is `eps·||h||/√(12n)` = 1.44e-5·||h||, negligible; without it, it is not. All four flags and their applied state are written into `provenance.precision_policy.backend_flags_pinned`.
  2. **The intervention itself is computed in fp32.** Rev 0.3's `make_projection_removal` did `uu = u.to(dtype=h.dtype)`, so under bf16 weights the whole removal — dot product, scale, subtraction — would run in bf16 and a small projection component would be rounded away *before* it was ever subtracted. Rev 0.4 upcasts `h` to fp32, computes the removal in fp32, and casts back.
  3. **fp32 island over the final norm and the unembedding**, with a forward pre-hook casting the incoming hidden state to fp32. Cost +0.58 GiB against 6.62 GiB of headroom. This is the highest-value island because greedy argmax, the D1 hedge probability MASS and the D13 CHECK gate score in nats **all** read the logits, where bf16 error near |logit| = 15 is 0.0586 absolute — a ~6% multiplicative error on each softmax probability. These are dtype CASTS, not interventions: they are installed permanently, for generation and clean pass alike, so they cannot create an asymmetry, and they deliberately do not touch `_HOOKS_ACTIVE`.
  4. **The clean-pass read path is explicit fp32 then float64 in numpy** (`.to(torch.float32)` rather than a bare `.float()`, so the intent survives a dtype change).
  5. Every comparison stays **PAIRED** on the same prompts, so systematic bf16 bias cancels between baseline and condition.
- **PRECISION RISK, stated plainly and not waived.** The continuous measurements survive bf16 weights under the policy above. **The DISCRETE risk does not cancel.** The entire rev-0.3 machinery rests on BYTE-IDENTITY of generated text: D23 keys Pratt zeros on it, D9's `behavioral_change_rate` is 1 minus the baseline-arm tie rate, and D29's common random numbers exist so that a null intervention reproduces baseline text byte-for-byte. In bf16, logits near |15| carry ~0.059 of representation error, so any next-token decision whose top-2 logit gap is under ~0.1 can resolve differently between baseline and ablated runs, and over 32 generated tokens at least one such flip is near-certain. **That inflates `behavioral_change_rate` toward 1.0 for free** — which would make rev-0.3's DR-F5 and DR-F2 `VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE` preconditions (0.469 and 0.313 in fp32) spuriously PASS at 20B, and would collapse the byte-identity tie counts (17, 22, 1, 1, 17) that D6/D23 depend on. **A rev-0.4 "pass" obtained that way would be a dtype artifact, not a result.** This is exactly what D34's calibration measures, and the calibration must be read before any rev-0.4 VACUOUS precondition is taken at face value.
- **The cleanest alternative is a bigger card, and it is stated here so the tradeoff is on the record.** An **H100-80GB** runs the FULL unmodified 5,864-unit battery in **fp32** in ~2.8 h — faster than the A40 manages a REDUCED battery in bf16 — with 2.91 GiB headroom, zero precision compromise, and rev-0.3's fp32 discipline preserved verbatim so rev 0.4 stays directly comparable to rev 0.3. An **A100-80GB** in fp32 gives ~4.2 h with 3.26 GiB headroom. Both dissolve the entire precision problem and the runtime problem at once. The A40 bf16 plan below is sound but is a resource-constrained second choice and ships with the calibration and the fp32 islands as conditions. **Explicitly do NOT use 2× A40 in fp32:** pipeline parallelism at batch 1 is sequential, so it costs ~11 h for no throughput gain.

### 2.4 Lexicons (word-boundary or single-token id matched; all frozen)

- **Hedge lexicon** (18 words): `perhaps, maybe, might, possibly, probably, unsure, uncertain, hesitant, hesitated, hesitate, wonder, wondered, doubt, doubted, cautious, caution, risky, unclear`. In rev 0.3 this lexicon is used in TWO ways: (a) the word COUNT on generated text (regex `\b...\b`) is retained as a **descriptive field only, never a precondition and never a pass criterion** (deviation D1); (b) the **probability MASS** over the single-token ids of its cased/space-prefixed variants is the load-bearing observable.
- **Control lexicon** (18 words, 1:1 paired with the hedge lexicon): `indeed, later, must, quickly, finally, unable, unknown, distant, returned, return, notice, noticed, note, noted, curious, attention, costly, unusual`. Purpose: prevent "the hedge lexicon moved" from being satisfiable by "every lexicon moved" under a broad distributional shift, which a rank-1 removal at L\* can easily cause. **Disclosure of a real limitation:** the matching is hand-made on part of speech, morphology and rough frequency band; it is NOT corpus-frequency-verified. It bounds, but does not eliminate, the bag-of-words weakness of a soft lexical proxy. **Because the matching is only approximate, the two lexicons are compared on a SCALE-FREE statistic (deviation D26):** the hedge mass sits around 3e-3 while the control lexicon contains very high-frequency items (*must, later, finally, return, note, attention*), so M3 compares `log(mass_baseline / mass_target)` per lexicon rather than raw mass drops. - **Act / restrain lexicons** (SECONDARY gate G3 only, never gating): act = `decided, acted, started, took, made, went, signed, called, pushed, launched, began, chose`; restrain = `waited, paused, hesitated, deferred, held, stopped, checked, asked, delayed, refrained, postponed, reconsidered`.

**(c) The preregistered substitute — declared, not invented.** Rev 0.4 therefore does **not** claim to reuse the 40-Element TARGET. It reuses the 40-Element **READOUT FAMILY**, on this battery's own frozen banks:

> **Held-out readout probe.** A 3-way classifier (F5 / F2 / neutral, **chance 1/3**) is fit on the **FIT half's BASELINE generated-text activations** at block L\*, mean-pooled over continuation positions, using the **pinned 40E probe contract verbatim** (`StandardScaler + LogisticRegression(C=0.1, max_iter=1000, class_weight='balanced', solver='lbfgs')`). The primary AMP readout for every judged condition is the probe's **P(own class)** on the JUDGE-half rows of that condition's clean re-forward. Fit and judgment stay disjoint at the frame level, exactly as the projection readout already required.

What that substitute does and does not inherit from 40E, stated so no one has to infer it:

| Property | Validated 40E | rev-0.4 substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Readout kind | held-out probe accuracy | held-out probe P(own class) — same family |
| Bounded with a defined chance floor | yes, 0.025 | yes, 1/3 |
| Classes | 40 (10 Noetics × 4 Domains) | 3 (F5 / F2 / neutral) |
| n test rows | 4,480 | 32 judge frames per class |
| Probe contract | pinned | **identical, pinned** |
| Fit/judge disjointness | group-disjoint, 0 overlap | frame-disjoint, seeded split |
| Hook point | MLP-submodule output, last token | **block residual output at L\*, mean over continuation positions** (rev-0.2/0.3 discipline, unchanged) |

| Nulls | 20 random same-rank orthonormal subspaces | 20 dose-matched, target-orthogonalized, damage-guarded rank-1 directions (D37–D39) |

**KNOWN RISK OF THE SUBSTITUTE — AND THE GATE THAT NOW ENFORCES IT (deviation D49).** The probe is fit on 96 rows (32 per class) in a 6,144-dimensional space, so it separates the fit set **perfectly by construction** — `fit_accuracy_in_sample_reported_only` will be 1.0 and carries **no information whatsoever** — and its JUDGE-half probabilities may saturate near 0/1, compressing the readout's usable range. `C = 0.1` with standardization moderates this but does not remove it. The report carries a **judge-baseline diagnostic**: the probe's held-out accuracy and the mean and **sd** of P(own class) on each of the three unablated judge banks, the direct analogue of the 40E held-out accuracy (0.95625 at chance 0.025 on n_test 4,480).

> **The first draft COMPUTED that diagnostic and consulted it nowhere.** In the saturated regime this section itself predicts, AMP-F5 could have returned **PASS while its own report block carried `below_instrument_resolution: true`** on a readout this section concedes is uninterpretable in size — and D41's standard-error window was simultaneously inert, because a saturated readout has sd ≈ 0, hence se = 0, hence `se > 0 and (width/se) < min_se` is FALSE. The two safeguards added in rev 0.4 were both inert against exactly the failure mode they were written for.
>
> **The gate, preregistered and GATING.** The readout probe may serve as the AMP headline readout only if, on the UNABLATED judge banks, (i) held-out accuracy ≥ chance + **0.10** and (ii) **every** per-class sd of P(own class) ≥ **0.02**. Failing (i) is DEAD (no class information); failing (ii) is SATURATED. Either failure stamps **`VACUOUS_READOUT_NOT_VIABLE`**: the projection numbers are still computed and reported in full as a descriptive secondary, but **a PASS is not available on the fallback readout** — that is the readout rev 0.3 showed is 78.7% dead offset, which is the entire reason D35 exists. In-sample fit accuracy is never used as evidence of viability.

**THE READOUT-FAMILY CLAIM, BOUNDED (second-pass correction).** D35 as first written drew a sharp contrast: "contrast normalization alone is an AFFINE rescale … The PROBE readout is the change that alters the comparison." That is **only partly right**. `probe_p` comes from a regularized linear classifier on the **same L\* residuals the projection is read from**, so it is close to a squashed monotone function of a projection onto a direction near `u_F5`. What genuinely differs is the sigmoid and the three-way competition — and **under the saturation this section predicts, the squash REDUCES sensitivity rather than increasing it**, which is the opposite of the stated rationale. The honest statement is: the probe readout changes the comparison **if and only if it clears the D49 viability gate**, and the gate is what converts that from an assumption into a measured precondition.

**Three differences are load-bearing and are disclosed rather than glossed.** (i) The 40E readout had 40 classes and 4,480 test rows; this has 3 classes and 32 rows per class, so it is far less powerful and its chance floor is far higher. (ii) The hook point differs (residual block output here, MLP submodule there) — rev 0.4 keeps the rev-0.2/0.3 hook point because changing the readout AND the hook point at once would make the comparison with rev 0.3 meaningless. (iii) The intervention is rank-1, not rank-39; that is deliberate, because rev 0.3 established that AMP **already is** rank-1 projection-removal from the same family as the 107× result, and it failed on **selectivity, not power**. **"Switch to subspace ablation" was NOT the missing fix** and rev 0.4 does not re-attempt it as though it were.

**(d) If the 40E target itself is ever run.** Do not rebuild it: point at the archived fixture and NPZ by sha256, re-verify both hashes before use, patch the in-repo evaluators to the fixed-split contract first, and pin `--model-revision` to a real 40-hex commit — the one gap the validated run left open.

## 3. Readouts, layers, directions — and the clean-pass rule

### 3.0 REV 0.4 additions (deviations D35, D37–D39, D41, D42, D44)

**PRIMARY readout by test.**

| Test | rev 0.4 PRIMARY readout | Secondary, always reported |
|---|---|---|
| **AMP-F5** (headline) | **held-out readout probe, P(F5)** — bounded [0,1], chance 1/3 (§2.6c), **subject to the D49 viability gate** | raw projection onto `u_F5`; contrast-normalized projection; CV and offset fraction |
| DR-F5 | projection onto `u_F5` (its signature is **presence collapse**, which is defined on the projection via `tau_F5`) | probe P(F5) drop |
| DR-F2 | same, mirrored — **dropped by default in rev 0.4 (D40)** | — |
| RS-F5 | projection onto `u_F5` (the increase-sign anchor) + hedge-mass log-ratio — **OUT OF SCOPE by default in the second pass (D48)** | probe readout not applied (RS judges the conflicted bank against a licensed reference, not a class contrast) |
| CONN | standardized downstream `u_F5` effect | — |

Presence, and therefore the **starvation-vs-attenuation discriminator**, always stays on the PROJECTION, because `tau` is defined there. If the readout probe cannot be fitted **or fails the D49 viability gate**, AMP falls back to the projection readout for its DESCRIPTIVE numbers, the report records that **the rev-0.4 headline readout change did not take effect and no rev-0.4 selectivity claim is licensed**, and the test is stamped `VACUOUS_READOUT_NOT_VIABLE` — **a PASS on the fallback readout is not available**.

**CONTROL CONSTRUCTION, binding for every ensemble in the battery.** Rev 0.3 applied its own reading rule to one test; rev 0.4 generalizes it rather than adding a second per-test exception.

0. **DRAWN WITH SURPLUS, GATED ON SURVIVORS** (D46). `--controls` is the number DRAWN (default **24**); **20** must survive the D39 damage guard for the control-ensemble p to be gating. Drawing exactly the gating floor gives the guard zero slack and makes a spurious `VACUOUS_CONTROL_ENSEMBLE_DEGRADED` the single most likely outcome per test (~57% at rev 0.3's observed degraded-draw rate). `--controls` below 22 aborts as `ABORT_CONTROL_FLOOR` before any generation.
1. **ORTHOGONALIZED against the target** (D37, extended by D55). AMP controls pass `ortho_to=u_F5` exactly as RS already did — **and so, from the second pass, do CONN's 20 downstream random READOUT directions**, which the first draft left unorthogonalized inside a `V_star` that contains `u_F5`. That was conservative in direction (it makes CONN's `d_target > max(d_rand)` leg harder) and they are readout rather than perturbation directions, but a per-test exception is precisely what this rule exists to eliminate. The realised cosine of every control with the target, and the max |cos|, are reported.
2. **Estimator-matched family first** (D37). Half of each direction ensemble is a **shuffled-label refit** of the target's own estimator on permuted labels — the family that fixes estimator, fit distribution and estimation-noise geometry and varies only the LABEL. The other half are PC-subspace directions, orthogonalized. DR keeps activation-decile-matched covers (same family, same k, same layer); CHECK/MEM keep count-matched head sets.
3. **DOSE-MATCHED on removed activation mass, AND THE MATCH IS GATING** (D38, made gating by D47). Accept/reject sampling into ±25% of the target's `mean |h·u|` over the judge prompts on the clean model, up to 64 numpy-only tries, else the closest of those tries. **Dose is removed activation mass, NOT vector norm** — norm-matching is vacuous here because every direction is already a unit vector. Realised magnitudes, the target/control-mean ratio, per-control try counts and a `dose_matched` flag are reported. **For DR the dose is the summed |activation| of the zeroed cover coordinates**, reported rather than asserted. **New in the second pass: a test whose ensemble could not be brought into the band is stamped `VACUOUS_DOSE_UNMATCHED`** — required: `n_dose_matched ≥ 0.90 × n_gating` AND target/control-mean magnitude ratio in **[0.80, 1.25]**. The first draft reported both numbers and gated on neither, and the reading rule in Section 11 covered only the FAIL direction. **The risk direction is now inverted:** rev-0.4 AMP controls are orthogonalized and drawn from mean-centred bases while the AMP target's dose is ~78.7% grand-mean offset, so if the band is unreachable the controls are systematically **weaker** than the target and a **PASS** is uninterpretable — the exact mirror of the rev-0.3 defect. The rule is therefore symmetric: *a null that is not dose-matched cannot be read in either direction.*
4. **DAMAGE-GUARDED** (D39, given slack by D46). Per-control coherence, distinct-4gram repetition and behavioural-change rate are recorded. A control that fails the same guards the TARGET must clear is **excluded from the GATING ensemble**; the unguarded ensemble and its statistics are still reported so the exclusion is auditable. If fewer than `CONTROLS_MIN_STRICT = 20` **survive out of the 24 drawn**, the test is stamped **`VACUOUS_CONTROL_ENSEMBLE_DEGRADED`** — below 20 the control-ensemble p floor 1/(C+1) is no longer strictly below α, and a gate built on broken-text controls is not a null.
5. **Cross-direction controls retained.** `u_F2` remains the semantic-nearest-neighbour cross-direction control for AMP and the DR-F2 cover remains DR-F5's cross-cover; each costs one generation pass and each already passed in rev 0.3.

**HONEST FRAMING OF THE CONTROL REBUILD, carried verbatim into the report.** An orthogonalized, dose-matched, damage-guarded ensemble is **by construction a less destructive null** than rev 0.3's, and a resulting PASS will be attacked on exactly that ground. The defensible statement is *"the rev-0.3 AMP null was contaminated and undosed"* — evidenced by the code asymmetry with RS, which already passed `ortho_to` for both its families — **not** *"we made the test easier."* Rev 0.4 therefore publishes the magnitude ratio and the per-control damage statistics alongside any pass; without them a pass reads as a control-weakening artifact and should be read that way.

**PASS WINDOW (D41).** For any test whose PASS requires the effect to sit **below** a ceiling, the window `(max guarded control drop, cap]` is computed and reported before the verdict is read:
- cap for a probe readout = `baseline readout − chance level` (the largest drop a bounded readout can express);
- cap for a projection readout under a **retention** requirement = the largest uniform drop that still keeps the presence rate at or above the retention fraction, computed **EMPIRICALLY** from the baseline per-prompt values (the rev-0.3 diagnosis' ≈1.5625 figure was a Gaussian approximation and normality was never checked);
- **an EMPTY window, one narrower than `PASS_WINDOW_MIN_SE = 2.0` standard errors, one narrower than `PASS_WINDOW_MIN_ABS_FRAC = 0.05` of the readout's own usable range, or one whose baseline standard error has COLLAPSED TO ZERO, stamps `VACUOUS_NO_PASS_REGION`**, not FAIL. Rationale: on the rev-0.3 numbers the AMP window was (0.9767, 1.5625] = 0.5858 wide = 2.20 SE at n=32, with its floor set by the maximum of 24 contaminated draws; a true hypothesis lands inside such a band partly by luck.
  - **The absolute term and the degenerate-se term are second-pass repairs (D48), and they are the whole point.** The first draft's rule was `underpowered = se > 0 and (width/se) < min_se`, which evaluates **FALSE whenever se == 0** — i.e. it could never fire in exactly the saturated-readout regime §2.6 predicts and D41 was created to catch. A collapsed baseline variance is now read as **failing** the variance term, not skipping it, and the window must additionally clear an absolute floor expressed as a fraction of the readout's usable range (baseline − chance for the bounded probe readout; the own-class-vs-other-class contrast range `2·(mean − τ)` for a projection, which on the rev-0.3 numbers was only 2.2886 of a 10.7386 readout).
- The window does **not** apply to DR: a starvation signature has no upper ceiling on the drop, so the control floor is reported alone.

**MEASURABILITY FLOOR (D42, RE-DERIVED by D52 and now GATING).** Every test reports `measurability` = {relative effect, **raw** dtype quantum, `n_averaged_measurements`, **effective** quantization floor, effect/effective-quantum, `below_dtype_quantization_floor`, statistical standard error, effect/SE, `below_statistical_floor`}.

> **The first draft's derivation was wrong, and the error did real design work.** It compared the relative effect against the **raw** bf16 quantum 2⁻⁸ = 3.90625e-3. But every judged quantity is a mean over ~32 continuation positions × ~32 prompts, and **independent quantization noise averages down by √n**: at n ≈ 1024 the effective quantization floor is ~32× smaller than the raw quantum. The **binding** floor is the statistical standard error — 0.2662 on the rev-0.3 AMP projection, roughly **25× larger** than the quantization scale. The mis-derived ratio was nonetheless used to justify dropping the DR-F2 rail ("48× below the bf16 quantum"), to pre-label RS "not measurable", and was written into the artifact as `below_instrument_resolution`, supplying a standing excuse to reclassify a genuine statistical null as UNINFORMATIVE.
>
> **What survives and what does not.** The DR-F2 drop (D40) survives on its behavioural-change rate of 0.313 and its null paired arms, not on the quantum arithmetic. The RS measurability concern (D48) survives on the **multiplicative** logit-error argument — a ~6% error per softmax probability against a 1.7% relative signal, true SNR ≈ 0.28 — which is independent of the quantum arithmetic and is about probability MASS, not about averaged projections. The specific ratios "48×", "8.7×", "2.5×", "1.2×" as stated against the RAW quantum are **withdrawn as a measurability argument** and are retained only as descriptive relative-effect sizes.
>
> **GATING (D52).** `below_dtype_quantization_floor` — the EFFECTIVE floor — now stamps **`VACUOUS_BELOW_INSTRUMENT_RESOLUTION`** and therefore blocks a PASS, not only labels a null. The first draft computed `measurability` and no status consulted it, so a headline test could PASS on a readout its own report block declared unresolvable. **The statistical floor is reported but never gates**: the paired p-values already handle it, and using it to gate would be exactly the "reclassify a statistical null as UNINFORMATIVE" move this section warns against.

**CROSS-RAIL COMMENSURABILITY (D44).** Both legs of the selectivity gate are standardized on the **same** readout, and the raw cross-rail drop is reported alongside the standardized one. The gate itself is unchanged: `|d_xrail| ≤ 0.5 × d_target`, GATING.

### 3.1 Readouts, layers and directions (rev 0.2/0.3 discipline, unchanged)

- Features: RESIDUAL stream, last token, via `output_hidden_states=True`; block L's output = `hidden_states[L+1]` (off-by-one preregistered). MLP-output-hook features are never mixed in (known pitfall).
- **Layer and head parameters are FRACTIONS of the loaded architecture (rev 0.3, deviation D12).** Resolved once, after `n_blocks` and `n_heads` are read from the loaded config, and written into `provenance.derived_layer_params`:
  - `sweep_lo = max(1, round(0.25 × n_blocks))` — 16 blocks → **4** (rev-0.2 value); 32 blocks → 8
  - `L_STAR_MIN = max(2, round(0.375 × n_blocks))` — 16 → **6** (rev-0.2 value); 32 → 12
  - `L_STAR_MAX = min(n_blocks−2, round(0.75 × n_blocks))` — 16 → 12; 32 → 24 **(new in the second pass, deviation D21)**
  - `DR_OFFSET = max(1, round(0.25 × n_blocks))` — 16 → **4** (rev-0.2 value); 32 → 8
  - `topk_heads = max(4, round(0.03 × n_blocks × n_heads))` — 16×8 = 128 heads → **4** (rev-0.2 value); 32×32 = 1024 heads → 31
  - `cover_k = max(8, round((32/8192) × mlp_intermediate))` — 8192 → **32** (the rev-0.2 "Top-32", exactly); 10240 → **40** **(deviation D20; the draft left this absolute, which was inconsistent with D12's own invariant-units argument and weakened the DR rail cut by 20% in relative terms at 2.8b)**. `cover_k_fraction_of_intermediate`, `cover_k_rev02_absolute_equivalent` and `topk_heads_fraction_of_all_heads` are all reported.
  Verified: at 16 blocks × 8 heads and 8192 intermediate these fractions return exactly `sweep_lo = 4`, `L_STAR_MIN = 6`, `DR_OFFSET = 4`, `topk_heads = 4`, `cover_k = 32` — bit-identical to rev 0.2. This is a re-expression in invariant units, not a re-tune. `L_STAR_MAX` is the one genuinely new constraint and is declared as such.
- **Layer selection (fit split only; revised — deviation D21):** per-block probe (StandardScaler + LogisticRegression(C=0.1, max_iter=1000, class_weight="balanced"), stratified 60/40 inner split of the FIT rows) for F5-vs-neutral and F2-vs-neutral over blocks [`sweep_lo`, min(n_blocks−2, `L_STAR_MAX`)]. Each block is scored as the **mean over `N_INNER_SPLIT_SEEDS = 5` inner splits** (rev 0.2 used one).
  - **The selection statistic is the inner-test predicted-probability MARGIN** (mean probability assigned to the true class), averaged over the two drives — a CONTINUOUS, non-saturating quantity. Accuracy is still computed and reported but no longer selects. Reason: with 32 fit frames per class on strict minimal pairs, accuracy saturates at exactly 1.000 across a wide plateau (rev 0.2 already reached 1.0 for F2 at block 6 of 16 with only 12 fit frames), which would hand the choice of L\* entirely to the tie-break rule.
  - **SELECTION WINDOW (new in the rev-0.4 second pass, deviation D51): L\* is chosen only from blocks at or above `L_STAR_MIN`.** The full sweep from `sweep_lo` is still computed and reported, together with the full-sweep argmax and an `early_layer_peak_below_selection_window` flag. Reason: `sweep_lo` = 0.25 × depth sits BELOW `L_STAR_MIN` = 0.375 × depth, so at 44 blocks a unique margin argmax anywhere in 11–15 hard-aborted the run as `ABORT_GUARD_LAYER` after discovery — a live ~30% risk, and one that collides head-on with rev 0.4's own recovered evidence: **the validated Stage-9 40-Element probe on this very model peaked at LAYER 2 OF 44, with a shoulder only to L7, entirely below the window.** Restricting SELECTION removes the abort; reporting the full sweep means an early peak is **disclosed loudly** rather than hidden. The disclosure states the constraint plainly: under the clean-pass rule an internal edit only registers if it becomes different TEXT, which needs downstream depth, so **the block judged is not necessarily the block where the signal is strongest** — and if the flag fires, that is a fact about this configuration that a reader must be given, not an argument that the configuration is wrong.
  - **Tie-break on a margin tie: the SHALLOWEST tied block at or above `L_STAR_MIN`.** The tie tolerance is **RELATIVE** (`MARGIN_TIE_REL_TOL = 1e-4`) as of the second pass (D51). At the first draft's absolute `1e-12`, exact ties on a continuous 5-seed-averaged margin essentially never occur, so the preregistered tie-break rule **never ran** and `ABORT_GUARD_LAYER` was doing all the work it was supposed to back up. This replaces the draft's "deepest tied block", which under accuracy saturation degenerates to `sweep_hi` — at 32 blocks that is L\* = 30 and L_dr = 22, installing the AMP and RS erasers two blocks from the unembedding with almost no downstream depth in which an internal edit can turn into different TEXT. Under the clean-pass rule only different text registers, so that configuration would make AMP-F5 and RS-F5 near-certain `VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE`.
  - **Tie-set widths for BOTH statistics are reported**, together with an `accuracy_saturated_plateau` flag, so saturation is visible in the artifact rather than inferred.
  - **10** shuffled-label floor trials at L\*, computed on the **same mean-of-two-drives statistic** as `best_acc` (the margin floor is reported alongside); if best accuracy ≤ floor max, the run aborts as `NO_SIGNAL_ATLAS_V0`. (Rev 0.2 compared a mean-of-two-drives `best_acc` against an F5-only floor, and used `np.argmax`, which on the pythia-1b run resolved a three-way tie at 0.90 to the shallowest block — exactly `L_STAR_MIN` — by accident of index order. Deviation D10.)
- **Hard layer guard:** `L_dr = L* − DR_OFFSET`. If `L* < L_STAR_MIN` the run hard-aborts as `ABORT_GUARD_LAYER` — no silent fallback that could violate the strictly-upstream rule.
- **Directions (fit split only):** `u_F5(l)` = unit diff-of-means, F5_fit − neutral_fit at block l; `u_F2(l)` likewise. **`u_RS(L*)` (rev 0.3, deviation D3)** = unit( mean resid(conflicted_fit) − mean resid(licensed_fit) ) at L\*, orthogonalized against `u_F5(L*)` before normalization — a restraint-CONTEXT direction matched to the construct being judged, fit on the RS **fit** half only. The rev-0.2 hedged-minus-plain direction is computed as `u_RS_legacy` and its cosine with the primary direction is reported as a disclosed secondary descriptor.
- **Paired statistic (rev 0.3, deviation D6, repaired by D23).** Every paired test uses **Wilcoxon signed-rank with PRATT zero handling**: |differences| are ranked over ALL judge prompts including exact zeros (zeros inflate the non-zero ranks, which is the conservative direction and the correct one here), and the one-sided p is the **exact sign-flip permutation** p over the non-zero signed ranks, computed by dynamic programming over doubled tie-averaged ranks — deterministic, no normal approximation, no Monte Carlo at these bank sizes. Zero differences COUNT AGAINST the hypothesis; they are never deleted.
  - **Zeros are taken from BYTE-IDENTITY of the generated text, not from float equality (D23).** A difference computed against a mean of C identical float64s is off by 1 ulp about 22% of the time, which gives a genuine zero an arbitrary ±1e-16 sign and a nonzero rank — so the draft's Pratt handling was largely inert and its reported `tie_rate` was not the quantity D6/D9 describe.
  - **Two paired arms, both required (D23).** ARM A compares the target against the MEAN of the C controls (the draft's only arm; its zero mask is "the target text equals EVERY control text", which is rare by construction). ARM B compares the target against the unablated BASELINE; its zero mask is exactly "this prompt's text was unchanged", i.e. `1 − behavioral_change_rate`. **BOTH arms must clear α for a PASS** — an effect has to differ from baseline AND beat the control ensemble. This is stricter than the draft, deliberately.
  - `n_total`, `n_tied`, `tie_rate` and `n_byte_identical_forced_zero` are reported for both arms of every test; the aggregate exposes the BASELINE-arm tie rate as the headline tie quantity. The rev-0.2 exact sign test is still computed and reported as `legacy_sign_test_p_reported_only` so the two revisions stay comparable.
- **Control-ensemble statistic (rev 0.3, deviation D7).** "Beats every matched control" is stated as an explicit permutation p: `p = (1 + #{c : stat(ctrl_c) ≥ stat(target)}) / (C + 1)`, with **C = 24** control trials by default. Floors: C = 6 → 0.1429, C = 19 → **0.0500**, C = 20 → 0.0476, C = 24 → 0.0400. **Correction to D7 as originally written:** the criterion is a STRICT `p < α`, so C = 19 does NOT make 0.05 attainable — 0.0500 is not below 0.0500. The smallest usable count is **C = 20**, and the script hard-aborts as `ABORT_CONTROL_FLOOR` below it. Both p-values (Wilcoxon-Pratt on both arms, and control-ensemble) must be < 0.05 for a PASS; this is the preregistered combination rule and it replaces the rev-0.2 stack of uncalibrated hurdles.
- **Behavioral-change precondition (rev 0.3, deviation D9).** For every core test, `behavioral_change_rate` = fraction of judge prompts whose continuation differs from baseline under the TARGETED intervention. If it is below **0.60**, the test is stamped `VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE` rather than FAIL: under the clean-pass rule the intervention did not engage the behavioural channel, so the label was never actually tested.
- **Control-direction subspaces (revised — deviation D22).** For the DRIVE tests (AMP-F5, CONN), random control directions are unit vectors inside the top-10 principal components of the pooled DRIVE fit residuals (F5_fit + F2_fit + neutral_fit) at L\*, so they carry residual variance comparable to a mean-diff direction. **For RS, that subspace is the WRONG one and is not used.** `u_RS` is fit on the RS pair bank, a different prompt distribution, and random unit vectors inside a dominant-variance subspace carry far more projection mass on any text than a small orthogonalized difference of means — which is precisely the rev-0.2 pattern where two random erasers produced increases 5× the targeted direction's. The RS ensemble is therefore built from two families, both on the RS distribution: **half SHUFFLED-LABEL REFITS** of `u_RS` (the identical estimator on label-permuted RS fit rows, orthogonalized against `u_F5` and normalized — the only family that matches the estimation-noise geometry of a 24-vs-24 difference of means) and **half random directions inside the top-10 PCs of the RS FIT residuals**. The gating control-ensemble p is the max-statistic over both families. **Perturbation magnitude — `mean |h·u|` over the conflicted judge prompts on the clean model — is reported for the target and for every control**, together with the target/control-mean ratio, so a reader can check whether the comparison was strength-matched at all: a FAIL against controls that are stronger perturbations than the target cannot distinguish "no brake" from "controls too strong".
- **THE CLEAN-PASS RULE (FATAL-1 fix; retained verbatim and EXTENDED in rev 0.3).** Interventions are installed ONLY while text is being generated. Every judgment metric — projections, presence rates, hedge word counts, **and in rev 0.3 every logit-level probability-mass measure** — is computed on a separate forward pass of the generated text with ALL hooks removed. The script enforces this with a module-level `_HOOKS_ACTIVE` flag: any measurement requested while a hook is installed raises. Consequence: an ablation that changes internal activations but leaves the generated text byte-identical scores EXACTLY ZERO effect. Only behavioral change can register.
  **Why the extension is load-bearing:** `u_RS` is fit as a difference of residual means and the unembedding is linear in the residual stream, so removing it has a near-analytic first-order effect on hedge-token LOGITS. A hooks-active hedge-mass measure would reintroduce the FATAL-1 circularity in logit coordinates. Computed on the clean re-forward it does not: byte-identical text gives byte-identical mass, exactly as for the projection measure.
- **Behavioral measure:** generate with hooks active (**32 new tokens** in rev 0.3 — up from rev 0.2's 24; this is deviation **D19** and its interaction with the D15 repetition guard is logged there — 10 in smoke); then clean-forward `prompt+continuation` and take the mean over continuation-token positions of the block-L\* residual projected onto the relevant unit direction. Probability mass is taken from the same clean forward: the mean, over the continuation steps, of the total next-token softmax probability assigned to a frozen lexicon's single-token ids. Presence threshold `tau_drive` = midpoint of the mean clean projections of the baseline drive-judge and baseline neutral-judge continuations (computed from baseline conditions only, before any ablation is judged). Guards, both required and both stamping CONFOUNDED on failure: coherence (distinct-WORD ratio > 0.3) **and, new in rev 0.3, repetition (distinct-4GRAM ratio > 0.5)** — the rev-0.2 coherence guard passed a DR-F5 target condition at 0.768 whose own sample text was "I have to get it done." repeated four times (deviation D15).
- **RS decoding exception (deviation D5, repaired by D29).** The RS arm alone generates **k = 5 seeded sampled continuations** per prompt (temperature 0.7, `top_k=0`, `top_p=1.0`, per-(prompt, draw) `torch.manual_seed`), and every measure is averaged WITHIN prompt before pairing, so the paired statistic is unaffected by k. All other core tests remain greedy. This departs from the rev-0.2 greedy-only discipline and is declared: determinism is preserved, but "the model wrote this" now means an average over a seeded sampled neighbourhood rather than a single greedy mode.
  **COMMON RANDOM NUMBERS are mandatory here (D29).** The conflicted baseline, the licensed reference, the target and every control share ONE `sample_seed`, so the per-(prompt, draw) seeds match across conditions and decode randomness cancels in the pairing. The draft used a different seed per condition, which made baseline and target continuations differ even under a NULL intervention: `behavioral_change_rate` would have been ≈ 1.0 by construction (so `VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE` could never fire for the anchor test), byte-identity ties could never occur, and unpaired decode noise entered M1's increase and M2's ρ — defeating the stated purpose of the k = 5 averaging.

**Why a VACUOUS RS now MEANS something (rev 0.3).** The rev-0.2 precondition was unreachable by construction on a base model, so `VACUOUS` carried no information — it only masked a guaranteed FAIL. The rev-0.3 precondition is reachable: both G1 and G2 were met on a base model (pythia-160m) at n = 12 in feasibility probing. Therefore `VACUOUS_NO_BRAKE` under rev 0.3 is an informative verdict — "this model shows no measurable agency suppression under conflict on this bank" — and not an artifact of the threshold.

**Honesty note carried verbatim into the report (deviation D17).** The rev-0.2 premise that "generic damage cannot fake the increase sign" is empirically FALSE as it was implemented: two random PC-subspace erasers produced increases of 0.528 and 0.547 against the targeted direction's 0.107. The anti-fake property of RS comes from beating matched controls PLUS the licensed-reference ceiling PLUS adequate n and decode-variance control. The bounded-restoration ceiling alone is NOT the fix either — on the rev-0.2 numbers those two winning random controls restored 61.6% and 63.8% of the headroom, comfortably inside any sane ρ ceiling.

**And the controls themselves must be honest (deviation D22).** Those rev-0.2 control increases are not only evidence that the sign is fakeable; read together with the perturbation-magnitude question they are evidence that the CONTROLS WERE TOO STRONG — random unit vectors drawn inside a dominant-variance PC subspace of a different prompt distribution are larger perturbations than a small orthogonalized difference of means. A FAIL against such controls is uninterpretable: it cannot separate "there is no brake" from "the comparison was not strength-matched". Rev 0.3 therefore fits the RS control subspace on the RS residuals, adds shuffled-label refits as the geometry-matched family, and reports `mean |h·u|` for the target and every control so the reader can check the matching directly instead of taking it on trust.

**RS-specific surface disclosure.** `u_RS` is plausibly a PROHIBITION-VOCABULARY direction: the licensing and blocking clauses are strongly polar (*allows/forbids*, *permits/bars*, *approved/stalled*), so the char-ngram control on the RS bank is expected to come back near 1.0. Section 6's interpretation rule covers this generically, but it is stated here for RS specifically because RS is the sign anchor and the verdict leans on it. The report carries the same sentence in `aggregate.rs_surface_disclosure`.

**Selectivity gate (review MAJOR fix — gating, not diagnostic).** For DR-F5 and AMP-F5: run the SAME targeted intervention on the F2 judge bank and compute the standardized effect on the F2 measure (`d_xrail`). The test PASSES only if `|d_xrail| ≤ 0.5 × d_target` (and mirrored for DR-F2). Pure nonspecific damage — both drives degraded equally — is now a structural FAIL, not a footnote.

**Why CONN is not a tautology anymore (FATAL-1 disposition).** In rev 0.1 CONN measured a hooked forward projection at a downstream layer, which the ablation moves near-analytically. In rev 0.2 CONN is judged only on clean re-forwards of the ablation-generated text, so it can only register if cutting the upstream cover changed WHAT THE MODEL WROTE; the non-trivial content is direction-selectivity at the downstream site (u_F5 vs u_F2 vs variance-matched random directions) plus cover-selectivity (targeted vs activation-matched covers). A generic degradation story fails the random directions; a lexical-echo story fails the matched covers. CONN remains correlated with DR-F5 (shared bank and direction) and is counted with that caveat (Section 8).

## 5. Harness-sanity checks (OUTSIDE the aggregate — FATAL-3 fix)

MEM (copy heads) and CHECK (induction gate) are generic textbook transformer phenomena. They would pass in essentially any model regardless of whether CAS-PCBA labels carve at joints, so they carry no discriminating content for the labeling hypothesis. Rev 0.2 demoted both to harness-sanity checks: they validate hook mechanics, head slicing, and matched-head controls, and their statuses are `SANITY_PASS`/`SANITY_FAIL`/`VACUOUS`, reported under `harness_sanity`, EXCLUDED from the aggregate and from the verdict. **That exclusion is unchanged in rev 0.3.**

**REV 0.4 STATUS OF THE SANITY CHECKS.** `CHECK_SANITY` returned **`SANITY_PASS`** on rev 0.3 — 31 induction heads ablated, gate score 12.465 → 5.436 nats, drop 7.029 against a best control drop of 0.821 (**8.6×**), `control_ensemble_p = 0.04`, with count-matched control head sets. That is the first sanity pass in the project and it settles the rev-0.2 ambiguity: **large, statistically selective causal effects ARE detectable with this apparatus.** Consequently **no rev-0.4 null may be attributed to a dead instrument**; if rev 0.4 returns GREY or NULL, the component hypotheses are what failed. `MEM_SANITY` returned `SANITY_FAIL` on rev 0.3 and its rev-0.2 wrong-signed disclosure (D30) still stands unaddressed. Both remain OUTSIDE the aggregate and the verdict, unchanged. At gpt-neox-20b the head budget auto-derives to `topk_heads = 84` of 2,816 total heads (0.03 of all heads, D12), and the D24 count-matching with cross-layer fallback applies unchanged. **Second-pass fix (D52):** both head-selection statistics — `mem_head_scores` (`sel.sum(-1)`) and `induction_head_scores` (`torch.stack(vals,0).mean(0)`) — accumulated in bf16 and cast to fp32 only afterwards. 84 heads are chosen out of 2,816 off that ranking, and CHECK is the battery's **sole** liveness evidence, so both now cast to fp32 **before** the reduction. The tensors involved are `[heads, T, T]` at T ≈ 24–40, so the cost is negligible.

What rev 0.3 changes, and why (both changes are informed by rev-0.2 results and declared as such):

- **CHECK, deviation D13 — RE-ANCHORED.** Old criterion: `drop ≥ 0.5 × baseline` AND beats max control. New criterion: **control-ensemble permutation p < 0.05 AND absolute drop ≥ 1.0 nats**, with the baseline gate ≥ 0.5 nats precondition unchanged. Reason, stated plainly: on rev 0.2 the apparatus moved the induction gate 12.766 → 9.207 nats (drop 3.559) against a best control drop of 0.175 — a ~20× selective margin — and still scored `SANITY_FAIL` because the fraction bar demanded 6.383 nats. The absolute-fraction criterion, not the instrument, is what fired. CHECK is the ONLY evidence in the battery that the harness produces large selective causal effects, i.e. the only thing that permits reading a NULL as "the labels are wrong" rather than "the instrument is dead"; a criterion that reports the working instrument as broken destroys that function. **The 1.0-nat floor was chosen with knowledge that the rev-0.2 drop was 3.559 nats.** It is therefore not an independent threshold and is not presented as one. The legacy 50%-of-baseline verdict is still computed and reported alongside (`legacy_rev02_fraction_criterion.would_pass`) so the comparison is auditable. Held-out judgment sequences are retained and enlarged to 32.
- **MEM, deviation D14.** `MEM_BASELINE_MIN` raised 0.5 → 0.6, because the rev-0.2 `baseline_copy_acc` landed EXACTLY on 0.50 and passed the precondition only because the guard is a strict `<`. Pass now additionally requires control-ensemble p < 0.05. Items split 20 discovery / 20 judgment (was 6/6), which makes the nominal 0.20 drop threshold exactly attainable instead of silently 0.333. `drop_resolution` is reported.
- **MEM, deviation D30 — the second masked rev-0.2 failure, disclosed.** D14 as first written disclosed only the precondition near-miss. It did NOT disclose that rev-0.2's MEM **targeted drop was NEGATIVE (−0.1667)**: ablating the top-4 copy heads IMPROVED copy accuracy, against a maximum control drop of 0.0. That is a wrong-signed result from the head-ablation instrument on the most generic, most trivially reproducible phenomenon in the whole battery — more damaging to "the instrument is alive" than the near-miss that was disclosed — and **none of the rev-0.3 MEM changes address the sign**. D18 sets this document's precedent for disclosing masked failures; it is applied here, and the sentence is carried into the report as `harness_sanity.MEM_SANITY.rev02_disclosure`.
- **Head budget for both.** `topk_heads` is now a fraction of ALL heads (0.03), not the absolute 4 of rev 0.2. At pythia-1b's 128 heads that is still 4; at pythia-2.8b's 1024 heads it is 31. Holding it at 4 would have made the head ablation 8× weaker in relative terms and would very likely have produced SANITY_FAIL on both checks for reasons of intervention size rather than instrument validity (deviation D12).
- **Matched-head controls are COUNT-matched (deviation D24).** The draft picked `min(len(selected_in_layer), len(available_in_layer))` per layer, so a layer holding more than half its heads in the top-k produced a control that ablated FEWER heads than the target — making the target look more selective for free. At 31 of 32 heads per layer that is a live risk. The per-layer shortfall is now made up by sampling unused heads from OTHER layers, and the realised control head counts plus a `control_head_count_matched` flag are reported. This matters most for CHECK, because CHECK is the ONLY evidence in the battery that the instrument produces large selective causal effects, and D13 has already lowered its bar on the basis of rev-0.2 results; an under-strength control would stack a second thumb on the same scale.

## 8. Aggregate metric, statuses, and the FAIL statement

**Per-test evidence is primary.** Each core test is judged against its own matched controls with its own preconditions; each is reported with status in {PASS, FAIL, VACUOUS, VACUOUS_NO_BRAKE, VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE, **VACUOUS_CONTROL_ENSEMBLE_DEGRADED**, **VACUOUS_NO_PASS_REGION**, **VACUOUS_READOUT_NOT_VIABLE**, **VACUOUS_DOSE_UNMATCHED**, **VACUOUS_BELOW_INSTRUMENT_RESOLUTION**, CONFOUNDED}. Only PASS, FAIL and CONFOUNDED are **evaluable**; all VACUOUS variants are removed from both numerator and denominator. The five rev-0.4 statuses (D39, D41, D47, D49, D52) are VACUOUS by design: each names a configuration in which the comparison could not have been read **in either direction**, which is information about the test, not about the hypothesis. Three of them (D47, D49, D52) are also **GATING for PASS** — a headline test may not PASS on an unmatched null, a non-viable readout, or an effect its own report block declares unresolvable.

**REV 0.4 SECOND PASS: the core set is 3 tests by default** (DR-F5, AMP-F5, CONN) because DR-F2 is dropped (D40) **and RS-F5 is out of scope (D48)**. `--run-dr-f2` and `--run-rs` restore the fourth and fifth. The `n_evaluable ≥ 3` bar is unchanged and is evaluated over whichever tests were actually run, which means the default battery has **zero slack on that bar**: any one VACUOUS test produces `GREY_ATLAS_V0_INSUFFICIENT_EVALUABLE`. That is stated as a cost of the budget, not hidden inside it, and it is the reason D46 (control surplus) and D50 (preflight stop rule) exist — the two mechanical routes to a spurious VACUOUS are closed in advance.

**Aggregate (descriptive only):** the fraction of evaluable core tests that PASS. The binomial-vs-0.5 headline is withdrawn and stays withdrawn: the five tests are not independent (DR-F5, AMP-F5, CONN share the F5 judge bank and u_F5; DR-F5/DR-F2 share method), so no p-value over the aggregate is licensed. **Better power does not change this** — the tests are non-independent by design, not by sample size. The report carries the caveat verbatim in `aggregate.non_independence_caveat`. Multiplicity across the five tests is likewise unaddressed and gets worse with power; per-test evidence stays primary, and if a Holm-adjusted set is ever reported it is secondary and must be labelled with the dependence.

**Verdict rules (preregistered):**
- `PREFLIGHT_ONLY`: the `--preflight` go/no-go pass (deviation D28). Carries a `go` boolean and three behavioural-change rates and NOTHING else; it is never a result.
- `ABORT_CONTROL_FLOOR`: `--controls` below **22** (= the gating floor 20 plus the D46 damage-guard surplus 2). Below the gating floor the strict control-ensemble criterion is unsatisfiable by arithmetic; at exactly the gating floor the D39 guard has no slack and one degraded draw stamps a spurious VACUOUS. Aborts before any generation.
- `ABORT_CACHE_FORMAT` (rev 0.4 second pass, D53): the cached snapshot holds only `.bin` shards while `use_safetensors=True` and `--local-files-only` are in force. Aborts before the load.
- `ABORT_UNPINNED_REVISION` (rev 0.4, D32): no pinned revision is recorded for the model and none was supplied. Aborts **before the model is loaded**, so an unidentifiable checkpoint costs zero pod time. Overridable only by `--allow-unpinned`, which stamps `claim_eligible: false`.
- `ABORT_REVISION_MISMATCH`: the resolved commit is not the pinned revision (rev 0.3). No claims of any kind. The full provenance block is written with the abort.
- `ABORT_GUARD_LAYER` / `NO_SIGNAL_ATLAS_V0`: guard aborts (Section 3), no labeling claims of any kind.
- `GREY_ATLAS_V0_INSUFFICIENT_EVALUABLE`: fewer than 3 evaluable core tests.
- `PASS_BOUNDED_ATLAS_V0`: **only when RS is in scope (`--run-rs`)** — ≥ 4 evaluable, at most one non-pass, AND RS-F5 is PASS or one of the VACUOUS variants (a scheme that cannot produce the increase sign is not testing RS; but a model with no measurable brake to remove does not doom the run).
- **`PASS_BOUNDED_ATLAS_V0_F5_RAIL_ONLY`** (rev 0.4 second pass, D48): the strongest verdict reachable when RS is OUT OF SCOPE — ≥ 3 evaluable, at most one non-pass, pass fraction > 0.5. **It is deliberately a different and weaker label.** With the increase-sign anchor unmeasured, the battery has not discriminated a typed component atlas from selective localization of F5-type content: DR (starvation) and AMP (attenuation) are both magnitude/presence signatures, and the increase sign is the only typed signature neither can produce. This verdict licenses nothing the full battery would, and the result document must use this label verbatim.
- `NULL_ATLAS_V0`: evaluable pass fraction ≤ 0.5.
- `GREY_ATLAS_V0`: otherwise.

**A second likely outcome, also stated in advance.** Beyond a sharper null, the live risk is `GREY_ATLAS_V0_INSUFFICIENT_EVALUABLE`: rev-0.2's DR-F5 changed the text on only ~6 of 12 judge prompts, i.e. a behavioural-change rate of ~0.50 against the new 0.60 precondition; DR-F5 and CONN share the same target condition and would go VACUOUS together; and RS may be `VACUOUS_NO_BRAKE`. That is fewer than 3 evaluable tests. Deviations D20 (fractional `cover_k`) and D21 (depth-capped, margin-selected L\*) are the two structural mitigations, and D28's preflight is the cheap check that they worked — run BEFORE committing ~2 h of GPU time, and declared here rather than invented after a GREY verdict.

**Expected outcome, stated in advance (rev 0.3).** The most likely rev-0.3 result is a SHARPER null, not a rescue. On rev 0.2 the DR-F5 targeted drop was 0.0339 against a best matched control of 0.0313 and a CROSS-rail cover that did MORE (0.0675), and DR-F2's targeted drop was NEGATIVE (−0.0268). Those are near-zero and wrong-signed, not underpowered. More n and better statistics buy a cleaner verdict, not a different one. Rev 0.3 is budgeted for that outcome and is not to be treated as a second attempt at a pass.

**Expected outcome for rev 0.4, stated in advance.** The most likely result remains a **sharper GREY**, not a rescue, and nothing in the rev-0.4 budget makes a pass more likely *on the merits*:

- **The cross-rail leg is expected to fail again**, and no control redesign can change that. `effect_size_cross_rail` is a property of `u_F5` applied to the F2 bank; the control ensemble does not enter it. On common scales, removing `u_F5` damaged F2-content-on-F2-prompts **1.49× to 1.72× MORE** than it damaged F5-content-on-F5-prompts, and the gate needs ≤ 0.5×. A genuinely drive-specific direction would need a 3–4× better on-target/off-target ratio than observed. **That leg may be a real negative result about the model**, and rev 0.4 should expect it to fail unless the target direction itself changes — which rev 0.4 deliberately did not do.
- **Even a rev-0.4 AMP PASS cannot lift the battery out of GREY on its own — and the first draft named that problem while changing nothing that would fix it.** Rev 0.3 had `n_evaluable = 1`. DR-F5 (0.469), DR-F2 (0.313) and CONN (0.469) all went `VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE` against a 0.60 floor, and widening the DR cut 2.55× under D31 changed DR-F5's rate by **exactly 0.00**. Rev 0.4 alters model, dtype, readout, controls and battery size — and **nothing about intervention strength, L\*, or the DR rail**. D31's "exactly ONE authorised step" was consumed at rev 0.3 and is **not** carried over to 20B implicitly. A preregistration that predicts its own verdict cannot move, and offers no declared remedy, is not preregistering an experiment.

  **THE STOP RULE (deviation D50), preregistered here and enforced in `--preflight`:**
  1. Run `--preflight` at 20B **first**. Go/no-go is unchanged: DR-F5 and AMP-F5 behavioural-change rates ≥ 0.60 (RS joins the gate only when `--run-rs` is on).
  2. **On a DR-F5 NO-GO:** apply `--dr-engagement-step` — `COVER_K_FRAC` 1.0% → 2.0%, **exactly ONE step, authorised in advance, no ladder** — log it in this section with date and reason **before** re-running, and re-run the preflight. This is a new authorisation for a new model, declared before the run; it is not D31's step being reused.
  3. **On a second NO-GO: DO NOT LAUNCH THE BATTERY.** DR-F5 and CONN share the target condition and go VACUOUS together, leaving `n_evaluable = 1` and a preregistered GREY after ~2.3 h. The declared fallback is a **single-test AMP-F5 confirmatory run**, whose verdict is capped at `GREY_ATLAS_V0_INSUFFICIENT_EVALUABLE` and which is reported as **one test, never as a battery**.

  An open-ended widen-until-it-moves ladder would be fishing and remains prohibited. The interpretation caveat from D31 carries: a wider cut is a stronger perturbation and therefore more likely to cause generic damage; the cross-rail gate and the decile-matched covers are what separate targeted from generic effects, and `cover_k_fraction_of_intermediate` is reported so a reader can see the cut size.
- **RS is likely unmeasurable at 20B in bf16, RS is the designated sign anchor, and rev 0.4's second pass therefore declares it OUT OF SCOPE rather than running it (D48).** Its G2 hedge-mass gate operates at 9.47e-5 on a 5.6e-3 baseline (1.7% relative) while bf16 logits inject ~6% multiplicative error per softmax probability — true SNR ≈ 0.28 — and it already returned `VACUOUS_NO_BRAKE` in fp32 at 2.8b (G1 p=0.5056, G2 p=0.6983). Running it would spend 1,944 of 3,864 units (50%) on an observable below the instrument's floor and would put the total run over the window. The first draft said the verdict structure leaning on the anchor "needs rethinking **before** the run, not after" and then left the structure unchanged; the rethink is the `PASS_BOUNDED_ATLAS_V0_F5_RAIL_ONLY` label, which states in the verdict itself that the anchor was not measured. **This is a genuine loss of discriminating power and it is priced into the label, not into a footnote.**
- **Scale alone is not the lever this budget buys.** `CHECK_SANITY` got 8.6× selectivity at 2.8b with a same-family, count-matched null, and the 40E result got 107×–422× with a bounded probe readout and same-rank nulls. Both differences from rev-0.3 AMP are **readout-and-null differences**, and both were available at 2.8b for a fraction of the cost. Rev 0.4 changes model AND readout AND nulls at once because the owner has approved the 20B run; that means a rev-0.4 pass cannot attribute itself to scale, and the D34 calibration plus the magnitude and damage statistics are what keep the attribution honest.

**FAIL statement (what kills the idea):** if RS-F5 is evaluable and FAILS against matched controls AND the evaluable pass fraction is ≤ 0.5 — or if RS fails AND the DR/AMP presence-vs-magnitude discriminator does not separate (both starve or both attenuate) — then CAS-PCBA internal labeling v0 is dead in its current form: the vocabulary would be reading as decoration on generic interventions, not as functional part-types with distinct causal signatures. That result is reported as NULL, honestly, with no rescue analysis inside this prereg.

## 10. Exclusions (justified; unchanged in substance from rev 0.1)

- **FILTER** — unfalsifiable unsubtyped: LP and HP subtypes predict OPPOSITE ablation signs; 0 usage in the 20-part wide run. Excluded until split into FILTER-LP/FILTER-HP.
- **LOOP** — unsigned without polarity annotation (opposite signs by polarity), prediction lands on second-order statistics needing a different harness; 8/135 rater usage. Excluded.
- **DIR** — clean only under a paired bidirectional influence measure (~2× measurement cost); 2 markings in the wide run. Deferred, not vague.
- **IF, WEIGH** — predictions ARE clean (condition-dependence collapse; between-option discrimination collapse) but each needs a bespoke stimulus bank (matched conditional pairs; forced-choice pairs) roughly doubling v0 size. Deferred to v0.1 by budget, explicitly NOT excluded as vague.
- **MEM, CHECK** — retained as harness-sanity only (Section 5); excluded from the aggregate because they are generic transformer phenomena with ~1 prior pass probability and therefore carry no CAS-PCBA-discriminating content.

## 11. Boundaries and language discipline (verbatim into the report)

Bounded claim only. This experiment, at best, causally supports assigning CAS-PCBA type labels to specific internal parts of one pinned model under specific prompts, measures, and matched controls. It does not find "the" neurons or "the" circuit; it does not show the model encodes CAS; it does not establish that the measured directions carry drive semantics rather than topic/lexical content (CAS-specificity remains GREY per the matched-taxonomy lane); it does not license generation-control claims (cite Stage 27P for the eraser channel, two families only); it does not transfer to other models, layers, hook points, or prompt distributions without rerunning. Known-null methods (raw CAS-symbol prefixes, global covers, ablation-based generation steering as an output-control claim) are not re-attempted here.

**Rev 0.3 additions to the boundary, all binding:**

- **Rev 0.3 is a NEW preregistered run, not a continuation.** L\*, L_dr, τ_F5, τ_F2, every direction and every cover are re-fit per model; the two runs share the design and nothing else. Presenting pythia-2.8b as "the same experiment at larger scale" would be exactly the post-hoc design change this document exists to prevent.
- **The rev-0.2 pythia-1b NULL stands unamended.** Rev 0.3 neither supersedes nor reinterprets it. A rev-0.3 PASS would license "the typed directional signatures survive on pythia-2.8b under a stronger design", not "rev 0.2 was wrong".
- **A rev-0.3 RS PASS licenses only** "the increase-sign brake signature is real on this model, against matched controls and a licensed reference ceiling". It says nothing about CAS. CAS-specificity — drive semantics versus topic semantics — remains GREY, and a redesigned RS test does not touch it.
- **Hedge probability mass is still a bag-of-words proxy**, just a soft continuous one. The control lexicon and the 24 matched direction controls are both load-bearing; without them, any broad distributional shift satisfies it.
- **All rev-0.3 feasibility evidence is off-model.** The probes that established the new RS observable is measurable ran on pythia-160m, gpt2-medium and Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct on CPU — not on pythia-1b and not on pythia-2.8b. They license "the gate is reachable", never a predicted value at 2.8b.
- **A rev-0.3 RS FAIL is only informative if the controls were strength-matched.** The report carries per-control perturbation magnitudes and the target/control-mean ratio precisely so this can be checked. If the target's magnitude is far below the control mean, the correct reading of a FAIL is "the comparison was not matched", NOT "there is no brake" — and that reading must be written into the result document rather than left for a reader to derive.
- **`u_RS` is plausibly a prohibition-VOCABULARY direction.** The RS licensing/blocking clauses are strongly polar, so the char-ngram control on that bank is expected near 1.0. This does not invalidate the test — what RS judges is the typed directional signature (increase-sign, bounded restoration, mass-sign) — but it does mean an RS PASS licenses nothing about drive semantics, exactly as Section 6's interpretation rule says, and the disclosure is stated for RS by name because RS is the anchor.
- **The base-vs-instruct hypothesis is confirmed but is not the whole story.** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct reached `frac_hedged` 0.167, still under the rev-0.2 0.25 precondition, so simply swapping to an instruction-tuned model would not have made the rev-0.2 test evaluable. The action-primed stub bank was an independent second cause and is fixed independently (deviation D2).

**Rev 0.4 SECOND PASS additions to the boundary, all binding:**

- **The dose-matching reading rule is SYMMETRIC.** Controls STRONGER than the target make a FAIL unreadable (the rev-0.3 case, ratio 0.3983 on RS). - **A rev-0.4 AMP result carries no selectivity claim unless the readout probe passed the D49 viability gate.** In-sample fit accuracy of 1.0 on 96 rows in 6,144 dimensions is a property of the geometry, not of the model, and may never be cited as evidence that the readout works.
