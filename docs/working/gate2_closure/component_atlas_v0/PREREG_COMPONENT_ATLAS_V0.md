# PREREG — COMPONENT ATLAS v0 (rev 0.3)

Preregistration for the first typed map from a model's internal parts to CAS-PCBA component labels, judged by directional ablation predictions against matched controls.

- Date frozen: 2026-08-18 (rev 0.3, second pass — see the D19–D30 block in Section 10; the first rev-0.3 draft was never run)
- Status at freeze: PREREGISTERED, NOT RUN (rev 0.3). Rev 0.2 WAS run, on `EleutherAI/pythia-1b`, and returned `NULL_ATLAS_V0` (0 of 4 evaluable labels stuck). **That result stands, unamended.** Rev 0.3 does not revisit it, does not reinterpret it, and does not claim it was underpowered into being wrong.
- Companion script (frozen with this doc): `component_atlas_v0.py` (same directory). The script's `PROMPT_FRAMES`, `RS_PAIRS`, `F5_HEDGED`, `F5_PLAIN`, `MEM_ITEMS`, `HEDGE_WORDS`, `CONTROL_WORDS`, `ACT_WORDS`, `RESTRAIN_WORDS` and threshold constants are the executable copies of the banks and thresholds in this document; the script writes sha256 of its own bank JSON (including both fit/judge split index sets) into the report as `prompt_bank_sha256`, and, new in rev 0.3, a `config_sha256` binding every threshold and every DERIVED layer/head parameter actually used.
- Report artifact: `atlas_v0_rev03_report.json` (rev 0.3), written incrementally at every stage boundary. The go/no-go preflight writes a separate `atlas_v0_rev03_preflight.json`. The rev-0.2 artifact `atlas_v0_report.json` is never overwritten.

### 2.4 Lexicons (word-boundary or single-token id matched; all frozen)

- **Hedge lexicon** (18 words): `perhaps, maybe, might, possibly, probably, unsure, uncertain, hesitant, hesitated, hesitate, wonder, wondered, doubt, doubted, cautious, caution, risky, unclear`. In rev 0.3 this lexicon is used in TWO ways: (a) the word COUNT on generated text (regex `\b...\b`) is retained as a **descriptive field only, never a precondition and never a pass criterion** (deviation D1); (b) the **probability MASS** over the single-token ids of its cased/space-prefixed variants is the load-bearing observable.
- **Control lexicon** (18 words, 1:1 paired with the hedge lexicon): `indeed, later, must, quickly, finally, unable, unknown, distant, returned, return, notice, noticed, note, noted, curious, attention, costly, unusual`. Purpose: prevent "the hedge lexicon moved" from being satisfiable by "every lexicon moved" under a broad distributional shift, which a rank-1 removal at L\* can easily cause. **Disclosure of a real limitation:** the matching is hand-made on part of speech, morphology and rough frequency band; it is NOT corpus-frequency-verified. It bounds, but does not eliminate, the bag-of-words weakness of a soft lexical proxy. **Because the matching is only approximate, the two lexicons are compared on a SCALE-FREE statistic (deviation D26):** the hedge mass sits around 3e-3 while the control lexicon contains very high-frequency items (*must, later, finally, return, note, attention*), so M3 compares `log(mass_baseline / mass_target)` per lexicon rather than raw mass drops. Both absolute baseline masses are reported so the scale gap is visible rather than implicit.
- **Act / restrain lexicons** (SECONDARY gate G3 only, never gating): act = `decided, acted, started, took, made, went, signed, called, pushed, launched, began, chose`; restrain = `waited, paused, hesitated, deferred, held, stopped, checked, asked, delayed, refrained, postponed, reconsidered`.

## 3. Readouts, layers, directions — and the clean-pass rule

- Features: RESIDUAL stream, last token, via `output_hidden_states=True`; block L's output = `hidden_states[L+1]` (off-by-one preregistered). MLP-output-hook features are never mixed in (known pitfall).
- **Layer and head parameters are FRACTIONS of the loaded architecture (rev 0.3, deviation D12).** Resolved once, after `n_blocks` and `n_heads` are read from the loaded config, and written into `provenance.derived_layer_params`:
  - `sweep_lo = max(1, round(0.25 × n_blocks))` — 16 blocks → **4** (rev-0.2 value); 32 blocks → 8
  - `L_STAR_MIN = max(2, round(0.375 × n_blocks))` — 16 → **6** (rev-0.2 value); 32 → 12
  - `L_STAR_MAX = min(n_blocks−2, round(0.75 × n_blocks))` — 16 → 12; 32 → 24 **(new in the second pass, deviation D21)**
  - `DR_OFFSET = max(1, round(0.25 × n_blocks))` — 16 → **4** (rev-0.2 value); 32 → 8
  - `topk_heads = max(4, round(0.03 × n_blocks × n_heads))` — 16×8 = 128 heads → **4** (rev-0.2 value); 32×32 = 1024 heads → 31
  - `cover_k = max(8, round((32/8192) × mlp_intermediate))` — 8192 → **32** (the rev-0.2 "Top-32", exactly); 10240 → **40** **(deviation D20; the draft left this absolute, which was inconsistent with D12's own invariant-units argument and weakened the DR rail cut by 20% in relative terms at 2.8b)**. `cover_k_fraction_of_intermediate`, `cover_k_rev02_absolute_equivalent` and `topk_heads_fraction_of_all_heads` are all reported.
  Verified: at 16 blocks × 8 heads and 8192 intermediate these fractions return exactly `sweep_lo = 4`, `L_STAR_MIN = 6`, `DR_OFFSET = 4`, `topk_heads = 4`, `cover_k = 32` — bit-identical to rev 0.2. This is a re-expression in invariant units, not a re-tune. `L_STAR_MAX` is the one genuinely new constraint and is declared as such.
- **Layer selection (fit split only; revised — deviation D21):** per-block probe (StandardScaler + LogisticRegression(C=0.1, max_iter=1000, class_weight="balanced"), stratified 60/40 inner split of the FIT rows) for F5-vs-neutral and F2-vs-neutral over blocks [`sweep_lo`, min(n_blocks−2, `L_STAR_MAX`)]. Each block is scored as the **mean over `N_INNER_SPLIT_SEEDS = 5` inner splits** (rev 0.2 used one).
  - **The selection statistic is the inner-test predicted-probability MARGIN** (mean probability assigned to the true class), averaged over the two drives — a CONTINUOUS, non-saturating quantity. Accuracy is still computed and reported but no longer selects. Reason: with 32 fit frames per class on strict minimal pairs, accuracy saturates at exactly 1.000 across a wide plateau (rev 0.2 already reached 1.0 for F2 at block 6 of 16 with only 12 fit frames), which would hand the choice of L\* entirely to the tie-break rule.
  - **Tie-break on an exact margin tie: the SHALLOWEST tied block at or above `L_STAR_MIN`.** This replaces the draft's "deepest tied block", which under accuracy saturation degenerates to `sweep_hi` — at 32 blocks that is L\* = 30 and L_dr = 22, installing the AMP and RS erasers two blocks from the unembedding with almost no downstream depth in which an internal edit can turn into different TEXT. Under the clean-pass rule only different text registers, so that configuration would make AMP-F5 and RS-F5 near-certain `VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE`.
  - **Tie-set widths for BOTH statistics are reported**, together with an `accuracy_saturated_plateau` flag, so saturation is visible in the artifact rather than inferred.
  - **10** shuffled-label floor trials at L\*, computed on the **same mean-of-two-drives statistic** as `best_acc` (the margin floor is reported alongside); if best accuracy ≤ floor max, the run aborts as `NO_SIGNAL_ATLAS_V0`. (Rev 0.2 compared a mean-of-two-drives `best_acc` against an F5-only floor, and used `np.argmax`, which on the pythia-1b run resolved a three-way tie at 0.90 to the shallowest block — exactly `L_STAR_MIN` — by accident of index order. Deviation D10.)
- **Hard layer guard:** `L_dr = L* − DR_OFFSET`. If `L* < L_STAR_MIN` the run hard-aborts as `ABORT_GUARD_LAYER` — no silent fallback that could violate the strictly-upstream rule.
- **Directions (fit split only):** `u_F5(l)` = unit diff-of-means, F5_fit − neutral_fit at block l; `u_F2(l)` likewise. **`u_RS(L*)` (rev 0.3, deviation D3)** = unit( mean resid(conflicted_fit) − mean resid(licensed_fit) ) at L\*, orthogonalized against `u_F5(L*)` before normalization — a restraint-CONTEXT direction matched to the construct being judged, fit on the RS **fit** half only. The rev-0.2 hedged-minus-plain direction is computed as `u_RS_legacy` and its cosine with the primary direction is reported as a disclosed secondary descriptor.
- **Paired statistic (rev 0.3, deviation D6, repaired by D23).** Every paired test uses **Wilcoxon signed-rank with PRATT zero handling**: |differences| are ranked over ALL judge prompts including exact zeros (zeros inflate the non-zero ranks, which is the conservative direction and the correct one here), and the one-sided p is the **exact sign-flip permutation** p over the non-zero signed ranks, computed by dynamic programming over doubled tie-averaged ranks — deterministic, no normal approximation, no Monte Carlo at these bank sizes. Zero differences COUNT AGAINST the hypothesis; they are never deleted.
  - **Zeros are taken from BYTE-IDENTITY of the generated text, not from float equality (D23).** A difference computed against a mean of C identical float64s is off by 1 ulp about 22% of the time, which gives a genuine zero an arbitrary ±1e-16 sign and a nonzero rank — so the draft's Pratt handling was largely inert and its reported `tie_rate` was not the quantity D6/D9 describe.
  - **Two paired arms, both required (D23).** ARM A compares the target against the MEAN of the C controls (the draft's only arm; its zero mask is "the target text equals EVERY control text", which is rare by construction). ARM B compares the target against the unablated BASELINE; its zero mask is exactly "this prompt's text was unchanged", i.e. `1 − behavioral_change_rate`. **BOTH arms must clear α for a PASS** — an effect has to differ from baseline AND beat the control ensemble. This is stricter than the draft, deliberately.
  - `n_total`, `n_tied`, `tie_rate` and `n_byte_identical_forced_zero` are reported for both arms of every test; the aggregate exposes the BASELINE-arm tie rate as the headline tie quantity. The rev-0.2 exact sign test is still computed and reported as `legacy_sign_test_p_reported_only` so the two revisions stay comparable.
- **Control-ensemble statistic (rev 0.3, deviation D7).** "Beats every matched control" is stated as an explicit permutation p: `p = (1 + #{c : stat(ctrl_c) ≥ stat(target)}) / (C + 1)`, with **C = 24** control trials by default. Floors: C = 6 → 0.1429, C = 19 → **0.0500**, C = 20 → 0.0476, C = 24 → 0.0400. **Correction to D7 as originally written:** the criterion is a STRICT `p < α`, so C = 19 does NOT make 0.05 attainable — 0.0500 is not below 0.0500. The smallest usable count is **C = 20**, and the script hard-aborts as `ABORT_CONTROL_FLOOR` below it. Both p-values (Wilcoxon-Pratt on both arms, and control-ensemble) must be < 0.05 for a PASS; this is the preregistered combination rule and it replaces the rev-0.2 stack of uncalibrated hurdles.
- **Behavioral-change precondition (rev 0.3, deviation D9).** For every core test, `behavioral_change_rate` = fraction of judge prompts whose continuation differs from baseline under the TARGETED intervention. If it is below **0.60**, the test is stamped `VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE` rather than FAIL: under the clean-pass rule the intervention did not engage the behavioural channel, so the label was never actually tested.
- **Control-direction subspaces (revised — deviation D22).** For the DRIVE tests (AMP-F5, CONN), random control directions are unit vectors inside the top-10 principal components of the pooled DRIVE fit residuals (F5_fit + F2_fit + neutral_fit) at L\*, so they carry residual variance comparable to a mean-diff direction. **For RS, that subspace is the WRONG one and is not used.** `u_RS` is fit on the RS pair bank, a different prompt distribution, and random unit vectors inside a dominant-variance subspace carry far more projection mass on any text than a small orthogonalized difference of means — which is precisely the rev-0.2 pattern where two random erasers produced increases 5× the targeted direction's. The RS ensemble is therefore built from two families, both on the RS distribution: **half SHUFFLED-LABEL REFITS** of `u_RS` (the identical estimator on label-permuted RS fit rows, orthogonalized against `u_F5` and normalized — the only family that matches the estimation-noise geometry of a 24-vs-24 difference of means) and **half random directions inside the top-10 PCs of the RS FIT residuals**. The gating control-ensemble p is the max-statistic over both families. **Perturbation magnitude — `mean |h·u|` over the conflicted judge prompts on the clean model — is reported for the target and for every control**, together with the target/control-mean ratio, so a reader can check whether the comparison was strength-matched at all: a FAIL against controls that are stronger perturbations than the target cannot distinguish "no brake" from "controls too strong".
- **THE CLEAN-PASS RULE (FATAL-1 fix; retained verbatim and EXTENDED in rev 0.3).** Interventions are installed ONLY while text is being generated. Every judgment metric — projections, presence rates, hedge word counts, **and in rev 0.3 every logit-level probability-mass measure** — is computed on a separate forward pass of the generated text with ALL hooks removed. The script enforces this with a module-level `_HOOKS_ACTIVE` flag: any measurement requested while a hook is installed raises. Consequence: an ablation that changes internal activations but leaves the generated text byte-identical scores EXACTLY ZERO effect. Only behavioral change can register.
  **Why the extension is load-bearing:** `u_RS` is fit as a difference of residual means and the unembedding is linear in the residual stream, so removing it has a near-analytic first-order effect on hedge-token LOGITS. A hooks-active hedge-mass measure would reintroduce the FATAL-1 circularity in logit coordinates. Computed on the clean re-forward it does not: byte-identical text gives byte-identical mass, exactly as for the projection measure.
- **Behavioral measure:** generate with hooks active (**32 new tokens** in rev 0.3 — up from rev 0.2's 24; this is deviation **D19** and its interaction with the D15 repetition guard is logged there — 10 in smoke); then clean-forward `prompt+continuation` and take the mean over continuation-token positions of the block-L\* residual projected onto the relevant unit direction. Probability mass is taken from the same clean forward: the mean, over the continuation steps, of the total next-token softmax probability assigned to a frozen lexicon's single-token ids. Presence threshold `tau_drive` = midpoint of the mean clean projections of the baseline drive-judge and baseline neutral-judge continuations (computed from baseline conditions only, before any ablation is judged). Guards, both required and both stamping CONFOUNDED on failure: coherence (distinct-WORD ratio > 0.3) **and, new in rev 0.3, repetition (distinct-4GRAM ratio > 0.5)** — the rev-0.2 coherence guard passed a DR-F5 target condition at 0.768 whose own sample text was "I have to get it done." repeated four times (deviation D15).
- **RS decoding exception (deviation D5, repaired by D29).** The RS arm alone generates **k = 5 seeded sampled continuations** per prompt (temperature 0.7, `top_k=0`, `top_p=1.0`, per-(prompt, draw) `torch.manual_seed`), and every measure is averaged WITHIN prompt before pairing, so the paired statistic is unaffected by k. All other core tests remain greedy. This departs from the rev-0.2 greedy-only discipline and is declared: determinism is preserved, but "the model wrote this" now means an average over a seeded sampled neighbourhood rather than a single greedy mode.
  **COMMON RANDOM NUMBERS are mandatory here (D29).** The conflicted baseline, the licensed reference, the target and every control share ONE `sample_seed`, so the per-(prompt, draw) seeds match across conditions and decode randomness cancels in the pairing. The draft used a different seed per condition, which made baseline and target continuations differ even under a NULL intervention: `behavioral_change_rate` would have been ≈ 1.0 by construction (so `VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE` could never fire for the anchor test), byte-identity ties could never occur, and unpaired decode noise entered M1's increase and M2's ρ — defeating the stated purpose of the k = 5 averaging.

**Why a VACUOUS RS now MEANS something (rev 0.3).** The rev-0.2 precondition was unreachable by construction on a base model, so `VACUOUS` carried no information — it only masked a guaranteed FAIL. The rev-0.3 precondition is reachable: both G1 and G2 were met on a base model (pythia-160m) at n = 12 in feasibility probing. Therefore `VACUOUS_NO_BRAKE` under rev 0.3 is an informative verdict — "this model shows no measurable agency suppression under conflict on this bank" — and not an artifact of the threshold.

**Honesty note carried verbatim into the report (deviation D17).** The rev-0.2 premise that "generic damage cannot fake the increase sign" is empirically FALSE as it was implemented: two random PC-subspace erasers produced increases of 0.528 and 0.547 against the targeted direction's 0.107. The anti-fake property of RS comes from beating matched controls PLUS the licensed-reference ceiling PLUS adequate n and decode-variance control. The bounded-restoration ceiling alone is NOT the fix either — on the rev-0.2 numbers those two winning random controls restored 61.6% and 63.8% of the headroom, comfortably inside any sane ρ ceiling.

**And the controls themselves must be honest (deviation D22).** Those rev-0.2 control increases are not only evidence that the sign is fakeable; read together with the perturbation-magnitude question they are evidence that the CONTROLS WERE TOO STRONG — random unit vectors drawn inside a dominant-variance PC subspace of a different prompt distribution are larger perturbations than a small orthogonalized difference of means. A FAIL against such controls is uninterpretable: it cannot separate "there is no brake" from "the comparison was not strength-matched". Rev 0.3 therefore fits the RS control subspace on the RS residuals, adds shuffled-label refits as the geometry-matched family, and reports `mean |h·u|` for the target and every control so the reader can check the matching directly instead of taking it on trust.

**RS-specific surface disclosure.** `u_RS` is plausibly a PROHIBITION-VOCABULARY direction: the licensing and blocking clauses are strongly polar (*allows/forbids*, *permits/bars*, *approved/stalled*), so the char-ngram control on the RS bank is expected to come back near 1.0. Section 6's interpretation rule covers this generically, but it is stated here for RS specifically because RS is the sign anchor and the verdict leans on it. The report carries the same sentence in `aggregate.rs_surface_disclosure`.

**Selectivity gate (review MAJOR fix — gating, not diagnostic).** For DR-F5 and AMP-F5: run the SAME targeted intervention on the F2 judge bank and compute the standardized effect on the F2 measure (`d_xrail`). The test PASSES only if `|d_xrail| ≤ 0.5 × d_target` (and mirrored for DR-F2). Pure nonspecific damage — both drives degraded equally — is now a structural FAIL, not a footnote.

**Why CONN is not a tautology anymore (FATAL-1 disposition).** In rev 0.1 CONN measured a hooked forward projection at a downstream layer, which the ablation moves near-analytically. In rev 0.2 CONN is judged only on clean re-forwards of the ablation-generated text, so it can only register if cutting the upstream cover changed WHAT THE MODEL WROTE; the non-trivial content is direction-selectivity at the downstream site (u_F5 vs u_F2 vs variance-matched random directions) plus cover-selectivity (targeted vs activation-matched covers). A generic degradation story fails the random directions; a lexical-echo story fails the matched covers. CONN remains correlated with DR-F5 (shared bank and direction) and is counted with that caveat (Section 8).

## 5. Harness-sanity checks (OUTSIDE the aggregate — FATAL-3 fix)

MEM (copy heads) and CHECK (induction gate) are generic textbook transformer phenomena. They would pass in essentially any model regardless of whether CAS-PCBA labels carve at joints, so they carry no discriminating content for the labeling hypothesis. Rev 0.2 demoted both to harness-sanity checks: they validate hook mechanics, head slicing, and matched-head controls, and their statuses are `SANITY_PASS`/`SANITY_FAIL`/`VACUOUS`, reported under `harness_sanity`, EXCLUDED from the aggregate and from the verdict. **That exclusion is unchanged in rev 0.3.**

What rev 0.3 changes, and why (both changes are informed by rev-0.2 results and declared as such):

- **CHECK, deviation D13 — RE-ANCHORED.** Old criterion: `drop ≥ 0.5 × baseline` AND beats max control. New criterion: **control-ensemble permutation p < 0.05 AND absolute drop ≥ 1.0 nats**, with the baseline gate ≥ 0.5 nats precondition unchanged. Reason, stated plainly: on rev 0.2 the apparatus moved the induction gate 12.766 → 9.207 nats (drop 3.559) against a best control drop of 0.175 — a ~20× selective margin — and still scored `SANITY_FAIL` because the fraction bar demanded 6.383 nats. The absolute-fraction criterion, not the instrument, is what fired. CHECK is the ONLY evidence in the battery that the harness produces large selective causal effects, i.e. the only thing that permits reading a NULL as "the labels are wrong" rather than "the instrument is dead"; a criterion that reports the working instrument as broken destroys that function. **The 1.0-nat floor was chosen with knowledge that the rev-0.2 drop was 3.559 nats.** It is therefore not an independent threshold and is not presented as one. The legacy 50%-of-baseline verdict is still computed and reported alongside (`legacy_rev02_fraction_criterion.would_pass`) so the comparison is auditable. Held-out judgment sequences are retained and enlarged to 32.
- **MEM, deviation D14.** `MEM_BASELINE_MIN` raised 0.5 → 0.6, because the rev-0.2 `baseline_copy_acc` landed EXACTLY on 0.50 and passed the precondition only because the guard is a strict `<`. Pass now additionally requires control-ensemble p < 0.05. Items split 20 discovery / 20 judgment (was 6/6), which makes the nominal 0.20 drop threshold exactly attainable instead of silently 0.333. `drop_resolution` is reported.
- **MEM, deviation D30 — the second masked rev-0.2 failure, disclosed.** D14 as first written disclosed only the precondition near-miss. It did NOT disclose that rev-0.2's MEM **targeted drop was NEGATIVE (−0.1667)**: ablating the top-4 copy heads IMPROVED copy accuracy, against a maximum control drop of 0.0. That is a wrong-signed result from the head-ablation instrument on the most generic, most trivially reproducible phenomenon in the whole battery — more damaging to "the instrument is alive" than the near-miss that was disclosed — and **none of the rev-0.3 MEM changes address the sign**. D18 sets this document's precedent for disclosing masked failures; it is applied here, and the sentence is carried into the report as `harness_sanity.MEM_SANITY.rev02_disclosure`.
- **Head budget for both.** `topk_heads` is now a fraction of ALL heads (0.03), not the absolute 4 of rev 0.2. At pythia-1b's 128 heads that is still 4; at pythia-2.8b's 1024 heads it is 31. Holding it at 4 would have made the head ablation 8× weaker in relative terms and would very likely have produced SANITY_FAIL on both checks for reasons of intervention size rather than instrument validity (deviation D12).
- **Matched-head controls are COUNT-matched (deviation D24).** The draft picked `min(len(selected_in_layer), len(available_in_layer))` per layer, so a layer holding more than half its heads in the top-k produced a control that ablated FEWER heads than the target — making the target look more selective for free. At 31 of 32 heads per layer that is a live risk. The per-layer shortfall is now made up by sampling unused heads from OTHER layers, and the realised control head counts plus a `control_head_count_matched` flag are reported. This matters most for CHECK, because CHECK is the ONLY evidence in the battery that the instrument produces large selective causal effects, and D13 has already lowered its bar on the basis of rev-0.2 results; an under-strength control would stack a second thumb on the same scale.

## 6. Surface-leakage disclosure and interpretation rule (review MAJOR fix)

- **Char-ngram control (mandatory, reported):** a char_wb 3–5-gram TF-IDF logistic classifier is fit on the FIT prompts (drive vs neutral) and evaluated on the JUDGE prompts; accuracies go into `surface_control` in the report. **Rev 0.3 extends it to the RS pair bank** (conflicted vs licensed, fit→judge), which rev 0.2 did not cover at all. Rev-0.2 reference values on the 24-frame bank: 0.583 (F5 vs neutral), 0.667 (F2 vs neutral) — near the 0.50 chance line. If the enlarged 64-frame bank pushes these materially upward, the frames were authored too loosely and the run's readability is degraded; this is checked and reported before any test is read.
- **Expectation and honest reading:** the banks are minimal pairs, so ALL separability lives in the drive slot and char-ngram accuracy is expected to be high. This is disclosed, not hidden. At this scale a drive cannot be expressed with zero lexical footprint; the Gate-2 ≤ 0.10 surface gate was designed for detector claims, and this experiment does not make one.
- **Interpretation rule (binding):** because u_F5 may be a topic/word direction, NO pass in this experiment is read as evidence that the model represents "drives". What the tests judge is the TYPED DIRECTIONAL STRUCTURE — increase-sign (RS) vs starvation (DR) vs attenuation (AMP), presence-vs-magnitude, cross-rail selectivity, and upstream→downstream selective starvation — which is orthogonal to whether the underlying direction carries drive semantics or topic semantics. The report's claim ceiling: "causally supports the <TYPE> labeling of internal parts serving F5-type content"; CAS-specificity (drive-not-taxonomy) remains GREY pending the matched-taxonomy lane (`run_matched_taxonomy_control.py`), and this prereg does not claim it.

## 7. Controls (all matched, all strength-matched, all seeded)

(a0) **RS controls, two families (deviation D22):** shuffled-label refits of `u_RS` on the RS fit half (the estimator-matched family) plus random unit directions inside the PC subspace of the RS FIT residuals, with per-control perturbation magnitudes (`mean |h·u|` on the conflicted judge prompts) reported for the target and every control; (a) covers: activation-decile-MATCHED random same-layer coordinate sets (each targeted coordinate replaced by a random non-target coordinate from the same mean-|activation| decile, without replacement) — uniform random covers over a mostly-inactive intermediate layer are a near-no-op and are NOT used; (b) cross-covers: the other drive's top-k cover, which is strength-matched by construction (same selection rule, same layer, same k); (c) directions: random unit vectors inside the top-10 PC subspace of the same fit activations (variance-carrying), put through the identical orthogonalization/removal procedure at the same rank; (d) cross-direction: the other drive's mean-diff direction; (e) probe floor: **10** shuffled-training-label refits at L\* on the same statistic as `best_acc` (abort gate); (f) paired statistics only, **Wilcoxon signed-rank with Pratt zeros taken from byte-identity of the generated text and an exact sign-flip permutation p**, per-prompt pairing, on **two arms** (vs the control mean and vs the unablated baseline, both required — deviation D23), plus the explicit control-ensemble permutation p at **C = 24** (floor 0.0400; hard minimum C = 20); (g) coherence AND repetition guards on every generation condition; (h) presence-vs-magnitude recorded for every drive test so DR/AMP cannot be conflated; (i) breakdown-flip guard: interventions are removals/zeroings at natural scale, never large additive pushes — any increase-sign result on a non-RS part is stamped ANOMALOUS_SIGN in reading and cannot count as a pass for that part; (j) the clean-pass rule (Section 3) applies to every judgment number, **including every probability-mass number**; (k) rev 0.3 only — the RS arm carries an unablated LICENSED reference condition as a restoration ceiling, and a frequency/POS-matched CONTROL LEXICON so a lexicon-mass result cannot be satisfied by an undirected distributional shift.

## 8. Aggregate metric, statuses, and the FAIL statement

**Per-test evidence is primary.** Each of the five core tests is judged against its own matched controls with its own preconditions; each is reported with status in {PASS, FAIL, VACUOUS, VACUOUS_NO_BRAKE, VACUOUS_NO_BEHAVIORAL_CHANGE, CONFOUNDED}. Only PASS, FAIL and CONFOUNDED are **evaluable**; all VACUOUS variants are removed from both numerator and denominator.

**Aggregate (descriptive only):** the fraction of evaluable core tests that PASS. The binomial-vs-0.5 headline is withdrawn and stays withdrawn: the five tests are not independent (DR-F5, AMP-F5, CONN share the F5 judge bank and u_F5; DR-F5/DR-F2 share method), so no p-value over the aggregate is licensed. **Better power does not change this** — the tests are non-independent by design, not by sample size. The report carries the caveat verbatim in `aggregate.non_independence_caveat`. Multiplicity across the five tests is likewise unaddressed and gets worse with power; per-test evidence stays primary, and if a Holm-adjusted set is ever reported it is secondary and must be labelled with the dependence.

**Verdict rules (preregistered):**
- `PREFLIGHT_ONLY`: the `--preflight` go/no-go pass (deviation D28). Carries a `go` boolean and three behavioural-change rates and NOTHING else; it is never a result.
- `ABORT_CONTROL_FLOOR`: `--controls` below 20, at which the strict control-ensemble criterion is unsatisfiable by arithmetic. Aborts before any generation.
- `ABORT_REVISION_MISMATCH`: the resolved commit is not the pinned revision (rev 0.3). No claims of any kind. The full provenance block is written with the abort.
- `ABORT_GUARD_LAYER` / `NO_SIGNAL_ATLAS_V0`: guard aborts (Section 3), no labeling claims of any kind.
- `GREY_ATLAS_V0_INSUFFICIENT_EVALUABLE`: fewer than 3 evaluable core tests.
- `PASS_BOUNDED_ATLAS_V0`: ≥ 4 evaluable, at most one non-pass, AND RS-F5 is PASS or one of the VACUOUS variants (a scheme that cannot produce the increase sign is not testing RS; but a model with no measurable brake to remove does not doom the run).
- `NULL_ATLAS_V0`: evaluable pass fraction ≤ 0.5.
- `GREY_ATLAS_V0`: otherwise.

**A second likely outcome, also stated in advance.** Beyond a sharper null, the live risk is `GREY_ATLAS_V0_INSUFFICIENT_EVALUABLE`: rev-0.2's DR-F5 changed the text on only ~6 of 12 judge prompts, i.e. a behavioural-change rate of ~0.50 against the new 0.60 precondition; DR-F5 and CONN share the same target condition and would go VACUOUS together; and RS may be `VACUOUS_NO_BRAKE`. That is fewer than 3 evaluable tests. Deviations D20 (fractional `cover_k`) and D21 (depth-capped, margin-selected L\*) are the two structural mitigations, and D28's preflight is the cheap check that they worked — run BEFORE committing ~2 h of GPU time, and declared here rather than invented after a GREY verdict.

**Expected outcome, stated in advance (rev 0.3).** The most likely rev-0.3 result is a SHARPER null, not a rescue. On rev 0.2 the DR-F5 targeted drop was 0.0339 against a best matched control of 0.0313 and a CROSS-rail cover that did MORE (0.0675), and DR-F2's targeted drop was NEGATIVE (−0.0268). Those are near-zero and wrong-signed, not underpowered. More n and better statistics buy a cleaner verdict, not a different one. Rev 0.3 is budgeted for that outcome and is not to be treated as a second attempt at a pass.

**FAIL statement (what kills the idea):** if RS-F5 is evaluable and FAILS against matched controls AND the evaluable pass fraction is ≤ 0.5 — or if RS fails AND the DR/AMP presence-vs-magnitude discriminator does not separate (both starve or both attenuate) — then CAS-PCBA internal labeling v0 is dead in its current form: the vocabulary would be reading as decoration on generic interventions, not as functional part-types with distinct causal signatures. That result is reported as NULL, honestly, with no rescue analysis inside this prereg.

## 10. Exclusions (justified; unchanged in substance from rev 0.1)

- **FILTER** — unfalsifiable unsubtyped: LP and HP subtypes predict OPPOSITE ablation signs; 0 usage in the 20-part wide run. Excluded until split into FILTER-LP/FILTER-HP.
- **LOOP** — unsigned without polarity annotation (opposite signs by polarity), prediction lands on second-order statistics needing a different harness; 8/135 rater usage. Excluded.
- **DIR** — clean only under a paired bidirectional influence measure (~2× measurement cost); 2 markings in the wide run. Deferred, not vague.
- **IF, WEIGH** — predictions ARE clean (condition-dependence collapse; between-option discrimination collapse) but each needs a bespoke stimulus bank (matched conditional pairs; forced-choice pairs) roughly doubling v0 size. Deferred to v0.1 by budget, explicitly NOT excluded as vague.
- **MEM, CHECK** — retained as harness-sanity only (Section 5); excluded from the aggregate because they are generic transformer phenomena with ~1 prior pass probability and therefore carry no CAS-PCBA-discriminating content.

## 11. Boundaries and language discipline (verbatim into the report)

Bounded claim only. This experiment, at best, causally supports assigning CAS-PCBA type labels to specific internal parts of one pinned model under specific prompts, measures, and matched controls. It does not find "the" neurons or "the" circuit; it does not show the model encodes CAS; it does not establish that the measured directions carry drive semantics rather than topic/lexical content (CAS-specificity remains GREY per the matched-taxonomy lane); it does not license generation-control claims (cite Stage 27P for the eraser channel, two families only); it does not transfer to other models, layers, hook points, or prompt distributions without rerunning. Known-null methods (raw CAS-symbol prefixes, global covers, ablation-based generation steering as an output-control claim) are not re-attempted here.

**Rev 0.3 additions to the boundary, all binding:**

- **Rev 0.3 is a NEW preregistered run, not a continuation.** L\*, L_dr, τ_F5, τ_F2, every direction and every cover are re-fit per model; the two runs share the design and nothing else. Presenting pythia-2.8b as "the same experiment at larger scale" would be exactly the post-hoc design change this document exists to prevent.
- **The rev-0.2 pythia-1b NULL stands unamended.** Rev 0.3 neither supersedes nor reinterprets it. A rev-0.3 PASS would license "the typed directional signatures survive on pythia-2.8b under a stronger design", not "rev 0.2 was wrong".
- **A rev-0.3 RS PASS licenses only** "the increase-sign brake signature is real on this model, against matched controls and a licensed reference ceiling". It says nothing about CAS. CAS-specificity — drive semantics versus topic semantics — remains GREY, and a redesigned RS test does not touch it.
- **Hedge probability mass is still a bag-of-words proxy**, just a soft continuous one. The control lexicon and the 24 matched direction controls are both load-bearing; without them, any broad distributional shift satisfies it.
- **All rev-0.3 feasibility evidence is off-model.** The probes that established the new RS observable is measurable ran on pythia-160m, gpt2-medium and Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct on CPU — not on pythia-1b and not on pythia-2.8b. They license "the gate is reachable", never a predicted value at 2.8b.
- **A rev-0.3 RS FAIL is only informative if the controls were strength-matched.** The report carries per-control perturbation magnitudes and the target/control-mean ratio precisely so this can be checked. If the target's magnitude is far below the control mean, the correct reading of a FAIL is "the comparison was not matched", NOT "there is no brake" — and that reading must be written into the result document rather than left for a reader to derive.
- **`u_RS` is plausibly a prohibition-VOCABULARY direction.** The RS licensing/blocking clauses are strongly polar, so the char-ngram control on that bank is expected near 1.0. This does not invalidate the test — what RS judges is the typed directional signature (increase-sign, bounded restoration, mass-sign) — but it does mean an RS PASS licenses nothing about drive semantics, exactly as Section 6's interpretation rule says, and the disclosure is stated for RS by name because RS is the anchor.
- **The base-vs-instruct hypothesis is confirmed but is not the whole story.** Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct reached `frac_hedged` 0.167, still under the rev-0.2 0.25 precondition, so simply swapping to an instruction-tuned model would not have made the rev-0.2 test evaluable. The action-primed stub bank was an independent second cause and is fixed independently (deviation D2).
