## 6. Key definitions

The paper defines:

- **context** as a frozen typed pre-outcome record, not consciousness or
  unlogged analyst judgment;
- **memory state** as a versioned state with initialization, update, timebase,
  reset, drift, uncertainty, and provenance;
- **injection** as declared mathematical addition at a bound layer, token,
  direction, coefficient, and time, not prompt injection or current injection;
- **steering** as the full preregistered injection and control schedule, not a
  result or causal interpretation;
- **path dependence** as a qualified response difference between distinct
  histories with the same present input, context, and injection;
- **hysteresis** as a restricted repeated cyclic/reversal path-dependence
  design with delay, drift, saturation, reset, and orientation controls;
- **classical gliding-box lacunarity** as a scale-indexed mass-distribution
  statistic;
- **proposed history lacunarity** as a new, unimplemented diagnostic over a
  frozen event-mass ontology; and
- **matched strict memoryless baseline** as a model with identical present-time
  information and matched capacity/training conditions but no recursive state
  or prior-sequence access.

The draft also requires an explicit finite observed-window baseline. That
baseline is not called memoryless.

## 7. Eight open hypotheses

The paper states, but does not test:

1. held-out predictive memory beyond a strict memoryless baseline;
2. added value beyond a finite observed-window baseline;
3. order-sensitive path dependence;
4. qualified reversal-loop behavior;
5. reset and persistence;
6. context specificity;
7. lacunarity-specific incremental value; and
8. injection/steering specificity.

Each has an explicit falsifier. All eight are NOT STARTED.
