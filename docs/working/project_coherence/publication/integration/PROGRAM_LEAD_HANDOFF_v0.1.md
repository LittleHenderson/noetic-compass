## 4. Canonical integration decisions

The lead crosswalk currently preserves 53 explicit notation/type records and
191 imported local claim/control records across formal, category, engineering,
ACBE, Harness, and supplemental memristive lanes. Local identifiers are not
renumbered or overwritten.

The highest-impact decisions are:

- `C_fin` denotes a category whose objects are named finite sets; it is not a
  finite category unless a finite carrier catalog is frozen.
- A typed path, one endomap power, a deterministic standard-basis lift, a
  Markov kernel, an affine channel, a Grothendieck sheaf, and a cellular/Hilbert
  sheaf are different types.
- A `MixedSimplexAddress` and a projector profile are different types and have
  no current canonical equivalence.
- The normalized projector kernel requires common rank `d > 0`.
- Formal Mathematics Paper 3 is the sole mathematical authority for the
  projector theorem and transfer records; engineering copies are read-only
  dependency summaries.
- `J_singleton` is preferred prose for the locally named `J_atom`; no standard
  atomic-topology claim is made.
- `ACBE_legacy`, `ACBE_NT2`, `ACBE-P5`, and `ACA` are separate versioned types.
- CAS-Ohm quantities and circuit language are nonphysical unless a separately
  typed, instrumented physical profile is authorized and validated.
- Evidence Packet v2 is a provenance bundle, not a verdict.
- Local structural evidence, hosted observation, a joined run, scientific
  validation, and product/disclosure authority require different receipts.
- `EIM`, `EMS`, `MET`, `MHS`, `SWI`, `PHD`, and experimental-result evidence
  are noninterchangeable types; there is no CAS electrical mapping or physical
  device in this program.

This lead layer is an index. It does not mutate the shared canonical symbol,
