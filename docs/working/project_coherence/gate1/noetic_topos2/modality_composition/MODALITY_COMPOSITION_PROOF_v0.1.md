## 1. Result

For the exact sixteen-object powerset context site in the Noetic Topos 2.0
v0.1 candidate, the answer is positive. Let `M_A` and `M_R` denote the ACBE
and RPM associated-sheaf reflectors, regarded as endofunctors of
`PSh(L,Set)` by composing sheafification with the fully faithful inclusion.
Let `M_atom` be sheafification for the topology generated by the four
singleton context pieces. Then there are canonical natural isomorphisms

```text
M_A M_R  ~=  M_atom  ~=  M_R M_A.
```

Moreover, the atomic topology is exactly `J_A join J_R`. Consequently,

```text
M_A M_R  ~=  M_R M_A  ~=  M_(J_A join J_R).
```

The symbols are natural isomorphisms, not strict equalities. Nested and flat
Set-products need not be literally equal in a chosen implementation.

This closes only the finite-context obligation on `PSh(L,Set)`. No statement
in this artifact lifts the result to `PSh(C x L)`, includes the observational
topology on `C`, or licenses a claim about enriched, probabilistic, empirical,
or product semantics.

## 2. Exact definitions and assumptions

Let

```text
Omega = {(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)}
L     = P(Omega), ordered by inclusion.
```

An arrow `U -> V` exists exactly when `U` is a subset of `V`. A presheaf is a
contravariant functor `F : L^op -> Set`. Define the two transverse partitions

```text
A_i = {(i,0),(i,1)},       i in {0,1},
R_j = {(0,j),(1,j)},       j in {0,1}.
```

For `P = A` or `P = R`, its generating family at a nonempty `V` is

```text
K_P(V) = {V intersect B -> V | B is a block of P}.
```

The family retains an empty piece when an intersection is empty. At the empty
context, the generator is instead the null family:

```text
K_P(empty) = empty family.
```

Let `J_P` be the Grothendieck topology generated by these families. Let `Q`
be the four-block partition of `Omega` into singletons, with the same null
cover convention at the empty context, and let `J_atom` be its generated
topology.

The proof assumes only:

1. ordinary small Set-valued presheaves in a fixed universe;
2. the powerset inclusion category above;
3. the stated ACBE/RPM generating families and null cover;
4. ordinary Grothendieck-topology and associated-sheaf definitions; and
5. the coordinate transversality `A_i intersect R_j = {(i,j)}`.

No legacy theorem, semantic interpretation of ACBE/RPM, or empirical premise
is used.

For completeness, these generators are a coverage basis. Pullback along
`U <= V` sends each member `V intersect B` to `U intersect B`. If `U` is
nonempty this is `K_P(U)` as an indexed family; if `U` is empty the pullback
is a family of identity arrows on the empty object and is therefore covering
(the registered null cover is covering there as well). Refining a member by
the same partition replaces it by its intersection with all partition blocks;
the only nonempty intersection is the member itself. Thus cover composition
generates the same sieve. Identity covers supply the remaining basis axiom.
The same argument applies to the singleton partition `Q`.

## 3. The explicit one-partition reflector

For a partition `P` among `A`, `R`, and `Q`, put

```text
I_P(V) = {B in P | V intersect B is nonempty}.
```

For every presheaf `F`, define a presheaf `R_P F` by

```text
(R_P F)(V) = product over B in I_P(V) of F(V intersect B).       (1)
```

The empty product is the singleton set `1`. In particular,

```text
(R_P F)(empty) = 1                                               (2)
```

even when `F(empty)` has zero, two, or more elements. For an inclusion
`U <= V`, restriction drops those coordinates whose intersection with `U` is
empty and otherwise applies the corresponding restriction map of `F`:

```text
F(V intersect B) -> F(U intersect B).                            (3)
```

The identity and composition laws follow coordinatewise from those of `F`.
There is a natural unit

```text
eta_P,F,V : F(V) -> (R_P F)(V),
x |-> (x restricted to V intersect B)_B.                         (4)
```

### Lemma 3.1

`R_P F` is a `J_P`-sheaf.

**Proof.** At the empty context, the null-cover sheaf condition requires one
section, which holds by (2). Suppose `V` is nonempty. The blocks of a
partition are disjoint, so distinct pieces of `K_P(V)` meet at the empty
context. On a nonempty piece `V intersect B`, equation (1) has exactly one
factor and is canonically `F(V intersect B)`. On an empty piece it is `1`.
Thus compatibility on every pairwise overlap is automatic: every restriction
to the empty overlap lands in the singleton `(R_P F)(empty)`. The set of
matching families is therefore exactly

```text
product over B in I_P(V) of F(V intersect B),
```

and the gluing map from `(R_P F)(V)` is the identity after the canonical
reindexing. It is a bijection. The generating families form a pretopology, so
checking them suffices for the generated topology. QED.

### Lemma 3.2

The unit `(4)` exhibits `R_P` as the associated-sheaf reflector for `J_P`.

**Proof.** Let `G` be a `J_P`-sheaf and let `alpha : F -> G` be a presheaf
map. For a tuple `(x_B)` in `(R_P F)(V)`, apply
`alpha_(V intersect B)` to each component. Because `G(empty)=1`, the resulting
local sections are a matching family on `K_P(V)`. The sheaf condition for `G`
gives a unique glued section of `G(V)`. Call the resulting map
`bar(alpha)_V`.

Restriction of a glued section is the unique gluing of the restricted local
family. Hence the maps `bar(alpha)_V` are natural in `V`. By construction,
`bar(alpha) eta_P,F = alpha`.

For uniqueness, restrict any candidate factorization to each nonempty piece
`V intersect B`. On that piece, the unit is the canonical singleton-factor
bijection `F(V intersect B) -> (R_P F)(V intersect B)`. The candidate is
therefore forced to equal `alpha` on every local component, and uniqueness of
gluing in `G(V)` forces it to equal `bar(alpha)_V`. At the empty context both
the source and target sheaves have one section. Thus

```text
Hom_Sh(R_P F,G) ~= Hom_PSh(F,G)
```

naturally in `F` and `G`. QED.

Consequently, `R_A`, `R_R`, and `R_Q` may be identified with `M_A`, `M_R`,
and `M_atom`, respectively. This direct universal-property proof avoids
assuming that a single plus construction already sheafifies an arbitrary
presheaf. General associated-sheaf background and the universal property are
recorded in the Stacks Project, Section 7.10, especially Theorem 7.10.10 and
Proposition 7.10.12: <https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00W1>.

## 4. Identification of the joined topology

### Lemma 4.1

`J_A join J_R = J_atom`.

**Proof.** We use the order in which `J <= J'` means every `J`-covering sieve
is `J'`-covering.

First, every singleton `{omega} <= V` lies below exactly one ACBE piece
`V intersect A_i` and exactly one RPM piece `V intersect R_j`. Therefore the
sieve generated by either two-piece family contains every singleton of `V`.
Both `K_A(V)` and `K_R(V)` are covers for `J_atom`. The null family at the
empty context agrees in all three bases. Hence

```text
J_A join J_R <= J_atom.                                              (5)
```

Conversely, in any topology containing `J_A` and `J_R`, first cover `V` by
the ACBE pieces and then cover each nonempty ACBE piece by its RPM pieces.
The nonempty members of the composite family are

```text
V intersect A_i intersect R_j = V intersect {(i,j)}.                (6)
```

Empty members and their repetitions do not change the generated sieve. If an
ACBE piece is empty, its refining family is the null cover, exactly as
required. At `V=empty`, the composite is again the null cover. Closure under
cover composition therefore makes the singleton-generated sieve covering.
Thus

```text
J_atom <= J_A join J_R.                                              (7)
```

Equations (5) and (7) prove equality. QED.

## 5. Composition and commutation

### Theorem 5.1

There are unit-compatible canonical natural isomorphisms

```text
R_A R_R ~= R_Q ~= R_R R_A.                                         (8)
```

**Proof.** For every `F` and `V`, expand equation (1):

```text
(R_A R_R F)(V)
  = product over i with V intersect A_i nonempty
      product over j with V intersect A_i intersect R_j nonempty
          F(V intersect A_i intersect R_j)

  = product over omega in V of F({omega})
  = (R_Q F)(V).                                                      (9)
```

The middle identification uses transversality
`A_i intersect R_j = {(i,j)}`. It flattens a nested tuple and indexes the
result by the atoms in `V`. Its inverse groups atomic coordinates first by
`A_i` and then by `R_j`. These maps are mutually inverse bijections. At the
empty context both sides are the empty product `1`, so the same construction
handles the null cover without an exception to the theorem.

For `U <= V`, every restriction on either side applies the same map

```text
F({omega}) -> F({omega})
```

for atoms retained in `U` and drops atoms not in `U`. Flattening therefore
commutes with restriction, proving naturality in `V`. Given a presheaf map
`tau : F -> F'`, both routes apply `tau_{ {omega} }` independently to each
atomic coordinate, proving naturality in `F`. The flattened composite unit
sends `x in F(V)` to the tuple of restrictions

```text
(x restricted to {omega})_(omega in V),
```

which is exactly the unit of `R_Q`; hence the comparison respects the
reflector units.

Swapping `A` and `R` gives the second isomorphism in (8). Composing one
comparison with the inverse of the other yields
`R_A R_R ~= R_R R_A`. By Lemmas 3.2 and 4.1, `R_Q` is sheafification for
`J_A join J_R`. QED.

### Corollary 5.2

The two finite-context modalities commute up to canonical natural
isomorphism, and either composite lands in the intersection of the ACBE- and
RPM-sheaf subcategories.

### Corollary 5.3

On an already atomic sheaf, both modalities and both composites are naturally
isomorphic to the identity. This does not make either modality the identity
on the ambient presheaf category.
