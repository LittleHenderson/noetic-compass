## Product Vision

CAS Harness should become a platform where any developer can audit open-weight models up to 4 trillion parameters, with the claim ladder kept honest:

- `<=20B`: empirically validated lane.
- `20B-300B`: engineered but not size-validated lane.
- `300B-4T`: `EXPERIMENTAL_NOT_VALIDATED` frontier lane behind explicit gates.
- `>4T`: refused.

1. Create an account.
2. Choose an audit target.
3. Connect a hosted open-weight model, customer endpoint, or reserved GPU job.
4. Run the audit.
5. Receive an Evidence Packet.
6. Verify WORM anchoring.
7. Understand the claim status without reading methodology notes.
8. Share or export the packet for investors, customers, regulators, or internal review.
