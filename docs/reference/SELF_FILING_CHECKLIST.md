# CAS Patent — Self-Filing Checklist (Non-Provisional / Utility Conversion)

**Purpose:** step-by-step for converting US Provisional **64/037,173** (filed 04/13/2026) into a
non-provisional (utility) patent application, self-filed (pro se).
**This is general procedural info, NOT legal advice. Verify all fees/rules on uspto.gov.**

> ⏰ **HARD DEADLINE: ~April 13, 2027.** File a non-provisional (utility) — or a PCT — claiming the
> benefit of provisional 64/037,173 by this date, or the 04/13/2026 priority date is permanently lost.
> A provisional is never examined and never becomes a patent by itself.

## ⭐ Do this FIRST — free attorney help (you don't have to do this alone)
The highest-value move for an under-resourced inventor is the **USPTO Patent Pro Bono Program** — it
matches income-qualified inventors with **volunteer patent attorneys for FREE.** Apply now; matching
takes weeks, and you most want an attorney on the **claims** (where self-filers get weak patents).
- Patent Pro Bono Program: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/using-legal-services/pro-bono
- Pro Se Assistance Program: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/using-legal-services/pro-se-assistance-program
- Law School Clinic Program (free supervised student help): https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/using-legal-services/law-school-clinic-certification-program
- Inventors Assistance Center (free phone help): **1-800-786-9199**

## Step-by-step
- [ ] 0. Apply to the Patent Pro Bono Program.
- [ ] 1. Create/verify USPTO.gov account + Patent Center access (identity verification): https://account.uspto.gov
- [ ] 2. Choose entity status (micro vs small) → fee tier. Micro certification form: PTO/SB/15A.
- [ ] 3. Prior-art search (free): https://ppubs.uspto.gov/pubwebapp/ + Google Patents. Save hits for the IDS.
- [ ] 4. Finalize spec, abstract, formal drawings.
- [ ] 5. Draft claims (≤3 independent / ≤20 total to avoid excess fees). Pro Bono attorney ideally drafts/reviews.
- [ ] 6. Fill the ADS — inventor info + **domestic-benefit claim to 64/037,173 (filed 04/13/2026)**. (Miss this = lose priority.)
- [ ] 7. Sign the inventor declaration (PTO/AIA/01).
- [ ] 8. File in Patent Center → upload PDFs, pay fees, submit: https://patentcenter.uspto.gov
- [ ] 9. Save the filing receipt; docket follow-on deadlines (e.g., Office Action responses).

## Fees — exact, current (USPTO schedule eff. Jan 19, 2025; last revised June 1, 2026)
**Micro entity (likely your tier — 80% off).** Qualify if: small entity **+** gross income ≤ **$251,190** (2025 cap) **+** named on ≤4 prior non-provisionals. Certify with PTO/SB/15A.

| Fee | Micro | Small (60% off) |

| Search | **$154** | $308 |

A clean micro-entity non-provisional with ≤3 independent / ≤20 total claims costs **$400 to file.**
