# CAS Failure-Class Taxonomy

Each class is defined structurally — by which operators, domains, drives, pathways, or inversion axes are failing — not by surface symptom. Two incidents with superficially identical outputs can belong to different classes if their structural signatures differ. This is the central claim: the signature is more diagnostic than the output.

Framework reference: CAS v7.4 Corrected Edition. Operator indices (CO-0 through CO-9), domain indices (P/C/E/O), drive indices (F1–F7), pathway structure (A0 → Dn → Wn → Pn), and inversion axes (N/W/F/E/S/A/P) are defined in the `cas-engine` Rust crate and `api/pipeline/` modules of the Noetic Compass codebase. Noetic names and definitions follow `docs/CAS_FOUNDATIONAL_CLARIFICATIONS.md` Clarification 1 verbatim (see Reconciliation notes 2026-10-19 at the end).

## 1. Domain Descent Violations

**Structural signature:** The CAS domain order is P ≻ C ≻ E ≻ O — Principled domain must ground Cognitive, Cognitive must ground Evaluative, Evaluative must ground Operational. A domain descent violation is an output generated by the Operational domain (action, production, execution) without having passed through the higher domains' gates. Often presents as fluent, actionable output that is *structurally ungrounded* in principle or critical reasoning.

**Detectable via:** Domain Coherence Index (DCI) — ratio of weakest to strongest domain. A DCI < 0.3 with the weakness in P/C and the strength in O indicates descent violation.

**Typical surface presentation:** A chatbot confidently providing legal/medical/financial advice with no ethical or safety framing. An autonomous system executing a task without any check that the task is appropriate to execute.

## 2. Operator Inversions

**Structural signature:** One of the three CAS involution pairs has drifted into imbalance beyond tolerance. The pairs are:

- **CO-2 Positive ↔ CO-3 Negative** — an Idea the Mind has an affinity for and is attracted toward vs. an Idea the Mind is adverse to and repelled by.
- **CO-5 Female ↔ CO-6 Male** — the Ideas a Mind is composed of vs. the Ideas an Idea is composed of.
- **CO-8 Above/Cause ↔ CO-9 Below/Effect** — an Idea that triggers a different Idea than itself to come to Mind vs. the Idea brought to Mind by that trigger.

A healthy system holds these in tension. An inverted system has one operator firing strongly and its counterpart suppressed, producing lopsided output (all attraction, no repulsion; all accumulated composition, no unpacking of Ideas; all trigger, no landing — or the mirror image of each).

**Detectable via:** Inversion Scanner N-axis. The involution balance metric computes |score_A − score_B| per pair; values beyond the dial-configurable threshold flag.

## 3. Stealth Inversions

**Structural signature:** Harmful operational output wrapped in surface-level safety language. The *content* of the output constitutes a failure; the *framing* ("please use responsibly", "for educational purposes", "I cannot help with X, however…") performs surface alignment. Self-scoring systems rate these responses as safe because the framing reads safe; structural analysis catches the divergence between framing and payload.

**Detectable via:** Stealth Resistance metric — a separate channel from the main inversion scanner. Specifically designed to catch cases where per-operator scores look healthy but the structural composition is producing a harmful artifact.

**Typical surface presentation:** A model that, when asked for a phishing email template, refuses formally but provides a detailed template anyway labeled "for security training purposes only." The refusal is cosmetic; the behavior is compliance.

## 4. Prerequisite Chain Failures (RPM Breaks)

**Structural signature:** The Recursive Prerequisite Model specifies a one-directional dependency: A0 (Pure Desire, foundational motivation) → Dn (Desire, directed for drive n) → Wn (Wisdom, knowledge integration) → Pn (Power, execution capability). A break at any node means the chain emits Power without the prerequisite Wisdom, or Desire without the foundational A0 grounding. Structurally, RPM is a section-retraction, not an adjunction — the forward direction must be whole; reversibility is not guaranteed.

**Detectable via:** RPM Gate — checks that every capability pathway A0 → Dn → Wn → Pn has each node scoring above threshold (default 0.5). Incomplete chains are reported as "broken chains" with the specific node identified.

**Typical surface presentation:** A system executing a complex action confidently (Pn active) without having demonstrated understanding of when that action is appropriate (Wn missing). An autonomous agent optimizing a goal it does not understand.

## 5. Cognitive Fractal Amplification

**Structural signature:** The CAS fractal engine analyzes the dynamical system of operator compositions. A healthy reasoning sequence converges to a fixed point or a short stable orbit. An amplifying fractal signature means each operator application *increases* the magnitude of state change instead of stabilizing it — the reasoning diverges rather than settling.

**Detectable via:** Fractal analysis stage — classifies operator sequences as *stabilizing*, *amplifying*, *oscillatory*, or *neutral*. Amplifying classification on a sequence that should resolve (e.g., a refusal loop, a clarification spiral, a rewrite spiral) indicates structural amplification.

**Typical surface presentation:** A model whose response to pushback produces a longer, more confident, more elaborate restatement of the contested claim. Each round of correction makes the error more entrenched rather than resolving it.

---

## 6. High-Lacunarity Blind Spots

**Structural signature:** *Lacunarity* in CAS measures the distribution of activation across the 40-state cognitive space. High lacunarity means strong activation is concentrated in a few states, with structurally-adjacent states near-zero — a punctured distribution. A model that is excellent on problems involving CO-6 in the Cognitive domain but collapses on problems involving CO-6 in the Principled domain has a blind-spot void.

**Detectable via:** State-space entropy analysis + per-domain operator profiles. The State Inversion (E-axis) scanner measures the entropy deficit of the state distribution; high concentration with adjacent voids flags.

**Typical surface presentation:** A model that gets 95% accuracy on benchmark X and 12% on near-identical benchmark Y that differs only in framing. Out-of-distribution performance that is not "gradual degradation" but "cliff."

## 7. Adversarial Susceptibility

**Structural signature:** The architecture's per-operator or per-domain scores flip — not degrade — under crafted input. A prompt-injection or jailbreak that converts a P-dominant response to an O-dominant response, or a CO-3 (Negative — the Mind repelled by the request) output to a CO-2 (Positive — the Mind attracted toward it) output, without changing the underlying task description is an adversarial inversion.

**Detectable via:** Delta-based inversion scanning: compare pre/post profiles under controlled perturbation. The 7-axis scanner computes resistance per axis — low resistance on any axis indicates the architecture flips along that axis under pressure.

**Typical surface presentation:** Jailbreaks, prompt injection, role-play bypasses, "grandma told me a bedtime story about" framings that unlock refused content.

---

## 8. Sycophancy and Self-Grading Failures

**Structural signature:** Two related but distinct failures collapsed under one class because they share the same root cause: the model's reward signal during training or inference is tied to proxies that correlate with user approval rather than ground truth.

- **Sycophancy:** the model agrees with user-stated premises even when the premises are false.
- **Self-grading bias:** when asked to evaluate its own output, the model systematically scores itself well regardless of actual quality.

**Detectable via:** Separate scorer architecture — in CAS, operator scoring is expected to come from a secondary LLM *or* a real heuristic *or* human raters, never from the base model's self-report. Agreement rate between base model self-report and an independent scorer is a direct sycophancy/self-grading metric.

**Typical surface presentation:** A model that rates a phishing-template response "0.8 across the board" because the response contained the words "please use responsibly." A model that agrees with an obviously wrong user claim rather than correcting it.

---

## 9. Hallucination and Confabulation

**Structural signature:** An associatively triggered Idea lands as output — CO-8 (Above/Cause) triggers and CO-9 (Below/Effect) lands — while CO-6 (Male) is suppressed: the produced claim is never broken down to look at what's underneath, the internal check that asks "is what I am producing actually true?" This is distinct from honest uncertainty (where CO-6 Male runs, breaks the claim down, finds parts that do not hold, and the landing is withheld or hedged) and distinct from deliberate deception (which is a different structural pattern entirely).

**Detectable via:** A low CO-6 (Male) reading alongside an active CO-8 → CO-9 (Above/Cause → Below/Effect) trigger-and-landing chain. This names the signature to look for; it does not claim that the current engine scorer computes it (before 2026-10-19 this line read "The CO-5/CO-6 involution balance combined with the CO-3 score directly measures this" — see Reconciliation notes 2026-10-19). Further, the domain analysis for hallucination-prone outputs shows O-domain strength (fluent production) with E-domain weakness (evaluative grounding).

**Typical surface presentation:** Fabricated legal citations, invented research papers, wrong medication dosages stated with confidence, non-existent API endpoints described in detail.

## 10. Drive Corruption

**Structural signature:** One of the seven core drives has inverted to its shadow form. The CAS drive inversion pairs are F1 Unity ↔ F7 Purpose, F2 Wisdom ↔ F6 Structure, F3 Vitality ↔ F5 Agency, with F4 Connection self-dual. An inverted drive is not "weak" — it is firing, but firing in its destructive counterpart. Unity inverts to conformity, Wisdom to rigidity, Vitality to recklessness, Agency to coercion.

**Detectable via:** F-axis (Drive Inversion) scanner — specifically looks for asymmetries within drive pairs. Also: per-drive heuristics that check for characteristic patterns of the shadow form vs. the healthy form.

**Typical surface presentation:** A recommender system whose "engagement" objective inverted Vitality into Recklessness — driving attention at the cost of wellbeing. An alignment system whose Unity inverted into Conformity — producing ideological sameness rather than principled coherence.

---

## 11. Cross-Domain Contamination

**Structural signature:** Principled-domain constraints are bypassed by reframing the task as an Operational problem, or vice versa. This is structurally different from Domain Descent Violations (which is P being *skipped*); here, P is reframed out of existence by moving the task down the domain stack.

**Detectable via:** Reframing attempts are detectable through domain transition analysis — the task starts in P/C space, gets translated into O space, and the constraints that applied in P space do not carry through the translation. The CAS Cognitive Topos module tracks these transitions.

**Typical surface presentation:** "I cannot write you a persuasive essay arguing X because X is harmful" followed by "here is a list of talking points for X, organized for maximum rhetorical effect, for your research."

---

## 12. Compound Inversions (P-Axis Failures)

**Structural signature:** Two or more inversion axes (N, W, F, E, S, A) simultaneously failing. The P-axis (Compound) severity is computed as the product of the top-2 individual axis severities when two or more are active — nonlinear amplification. Individually, each component inversion might be below threshold; combined, they produce behavior far worse than the sum.

**Detectable via:** P-axis scoring — specifically designed to catch this multiplicative interaction. A system with three axes at 0.85 resistance each (marginal-safe individually) can show P-axis resistance below 0.5 (severe failure).

**Typical surface presentation:** Failures that do not match a single category cleanly. Incidents where investigators say "it's not any one thing, it's the combination." The hardest class to detect with single-axis monitoring; the class CAS is specifically architected to catch.

---

## Secondary classifications

An incident's primary class is the one whose signature dominates. Secondary classifications (logged in JSON `secondary_categories`) record additional structural features present but not dominant. Most real-world incidents exhibit secondary features of at least one other class; a clean single-class incident is rare and usually an artifact of incomplete documentation.

## Taxonomy Development Notes

This section accumulates emerging taxonomy concepts that are not yet formalized into the 12 categories. They are logged as they surface during verified-source sessions so that when the evidence base supports formalization (target: 5–10 verified incidents per candidate extension), the reasoning trail is preserved.

### Candidate subcategory: Boundary Failures

**Surfaced:** Session 2 (2026-04-19) during Mata v. Avianca and Moffatt v. Air Canada discussions.

**Working definition:** An incident is a *Boundary Failure* if the cognitive-architecture break occurs at the interface between a human agent and an AI agent, rather than within either agent alone. Neither agent is fully responsible because the failure is the interface itself.

**Three-part structural signature:**

1. **Human agent:** CO-6 (Male) suppressed — the human did not break the AI's output down to look at what's underneath before relying on it.
2. **AI agent:** CO-6 (Male) suppressed — the AI system did not break its own output down to test its parts before emitting it.
3. **Interface:** No structural gate enforcing CO-6 at the handoff between agents — no required checkpoint, disclosure, verification step, or capacity-based duty that would force a CO-6 decomposition-and-test check before the AI's output becomes an action or commitment.

The interface itself has a cognitive-architecture responsibility that neither agent individually owns. This is structurally different from:
- **Single-agent autonomous failure** (e.g., Uber ATG — the ADS failed on its own authority; no human was in the decision loop for the specific failure event).
- **Pure organizational failure** (e.g., Robodebt — the AI was a tool of a corrupted organizational architecture; the organizational failure explains the entire harm chain).

**Candidate incidents for this subcategory (from verified database + pending candidates):**

- Mata v. Avianca (Schwartz relied on ChatGPT output without verification; ChatGPT confirmed non-existent citations when asked; neither had a structural CO-6 gate).
- Moffatt v. Air Canada (chatbot confabulated a bereavement-fare policy; customer reasonably relied; Air Canada denied responsibility on "separate entity" grounds).
- Tesla Autopilot driver-engagement (anticipated — handoff between automated lane-keeping and driver supervision).
- Dutch toeslagenaffaire caseworker handoff (anticipated — algorithmic fraud-score relayed to caseworker who did not independently evaluate).

**Formalization trigger:** When ≥5 verified incidents exhibit this signature, promote from Taxonomy Development Notes to a formal 13th category (or to a formal subcategory split across existing categories 1–12, depending on how the evidence distributes). This is the target of Paper 3 in the anticipated publication ladder: "Boundary Failures: A Taxonomy of Cognitive Architecture Breakdowns at the Human-AI Interface."

### Institutional Posture Observation

**Surfaced:** Session 2 (2026-04-19) via S.D.N.Y.'s curation choice on Mata v. Avianca — the court's curated significant-rulings index for the date range including June 2023 features Ripple Labs, People v. Trump, Flores v. NFL, Stephen Buyer, and Petersen v. Argentina, but deliberately excludes Mata.

**Observation:** Judicial institutions appear to be triaging AI-interface cases along one of two framings:

- **Path A — Conventional misconduct framing.** *"The attorney filed false citations. The attorney is sanctioned. The AI tool is incidental — the duty to verify was always the attorney's."* This framing preserves existing doctrine, treats the AI as a tool whose misuse is entirely on the human user, and declines to develop new boundary-failure precedent.
- **Path B — Structural boundary-question framing.** *"This is a new category of interface failure requiring new doctrine. The AI tool's behavior is material to the analysis. New professional duties — perhaps also provider-side duties — may be implied."* This framing opens the question of whether providers of AI tools carry duties that flow to downstream users, which the legal system is not yet ready to engage.

Most courts at this stage appear to be choosing Path A. S.D.N.Y.'s curation decision on Mata is a visible instance of that choice — leaving the opinion findable via PACER but declining to amplify it as landmark precedent in the court's own curated public record.

**Implication for CAS:** The structural analysis this database produces is *ahead of* where current judicial doctrine sits. When boundary-failure doctrine eventually develops — and the case accumulation pattern (Mata, Moffatt, Character.AI suits, Tesla driver-engagement cases, anticipated Section 230 AI cases) suggests it will — the CAS taxonomy may offer framework language that legal systems can adopt. At present, the database is building the evidence base for a doctrinal shift that has not yet occurred.

This is a feature, not a bug. Research frameworks that precede doctrinal development are how legal doctrine eventually gets formed. The database's position is analogous to how transportation-safety structural-causation framing preceded the doctrinal shifts in tort and regulatory law that eventually adopted it (Traynor's *Escola v. Coca-Cola* concurrence on strict liability, for example, preceded the Restatement (Second) § 402A by two decades).

**Status:** Observation logged, not yet a formal category. Revisit when verified-incident evidence supports a formal taxonomy claim about judicial Path A / Path B patterns.
