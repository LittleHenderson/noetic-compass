\chapter{Glossary}\label{appC:glossary}

This glossary defines the technical terms and identifiers used in the monograph.
Chapter references are given as plain text. Every definition is scoped to this
Ring-4 research candidate; no term carries a safety, causal, physical, or product
claim beyond what the cited chapter states.

\begin{description}

\item[Adapter certificate] The immutable record binding the exact TKS~v8 kernel
bytes, board/component/transition identities, tagged carrier, semantic profile,
and research boundary when a certified transition is adapted to a finite arrow.
Attests type-correct representation only (Chapter~9).

\item[Board] The typed, immutable, identity-bearing record of components, ports,
nets, test points, calibrations, feedback declarations, and provenance metadata
that constitutes a modeled circuit. Board identity includes the complete
canonical structure (Chapter~5).

\item[CCDL] The Cognitive Circuit Description Language of the 743-page source
baseline. Chapter~10 audits the mismatch between its consolidated EBNF and its
own five-board reference corpus.

\item[Check class] One of the five organizing views of the design-rule checker:
structural integrity, typed connectivity, calibration and probe integrity,
feedback analysis, and threshold reachability (Chapter~7).

\item[CJT] The cognitive-junction reference component kind: two activation
inputs (\texttt{cause}, \texttt{bias}), one activation output (\texttt{effect}),
with finite nonnegative decimal gain \texttt{beta} and activation-level
\texttt{saturation} and \texttt{cutoff} parameters (Chapters~4, 5, and~8). The
source's idealized infinite gain is unrepresentable in the candidate's types
(Chapter~11).

\item[Cognitive Safety Board (CSB)] The published safety board whose red-stage
clamp of $0.1$ sits upstream of a fuse threshold of $0.9$, rendering the
protective crossing unreachable under the declared clamp semantics
(Chapter~11).

\item[Decimal context] The single immutable, versioned arithmetic context,
created from primitive constants, under which all decimal operations run; its
identity enters reports and results (Chapter~4).

\item[Design-rule checker (DRC)] The deterministic checker that computes one
diagnostic report from the \texttt{PCBA8-*} catalog; errors fail the board and
warnings never become an implicit clean result (Chapter~7).

\item[Fisher--Rao metric] The information-geometric metric on the open
probability simplex used by the mixed-simplex geometry profile; its binary64
implementation is a disclosed numerical pseudometric at its resolution
(Chapters~2 and~6).

\item[Known-fault fixture] A source artifact retained unmodified as negative
evidence and a regression guard, rather than silently repaired; the CSB is the
principal example (Chapter~11).

\item[NDCell] A Noetic-times-Domain pair; the target of the many-to-one
projection from a state literal that forgets Drive and Register (Chapter~4).

\item[Ring 4] The admission ring denoting a research candidate that is
\textsc{open}, \textsc{not licensed}, and not publicly disclosed. All artifacts
in this monograph are Ring~4 (throughout).

\item[Semigroupoid path] A typed sequence of finite arrows with matching adjacent
objects and a typed composite but no power operation; the form to which component
chains are adapted (Chapter~9).

\item[SimplexAddress40] A strictly positive normalized profile over the forty
canonical ND-cells; neither a one-hot cell nor an element of the finite TKS
kernel (Chapter~4).

\item[State literal] A Noetic-times-Domain-times-optional-Drive-times-optional-Register
tuple; the richest state type, projecting many-to-one onto an NDCell
(Chapter~4).

\item[Tagged exact value] A value carrying its exact type identity, so that
integer $1$, Boolean \emph{true}, and string \texttt{"true"} remain distinct
throughout checking, execution, traces, and adapters (Chapter~4).

\item[Threshold reachability] The check that propagates conservative upper bounds
along an activation path and flags a safety fuse whose threshold exceeds the
resolved upstream bound (\texttt{PCBA8-SAF-001}) (Chapter~7).

\item[TKS v8 finite arrow] The certified total, finite, deterministic, stateless,
non-stochastic transition admitted to the companion TKS kernel; the only
component transitions eligible for adaptation (Chapter~9).

\item[Trace] The canonical, provenance-rich record emitted by an admitted
deterministic simulation, binding source, analysis, context, signal, event,
observation, lineage, and boundary fields (Chapter~8; fields in Appendix~A).

\end{description}
