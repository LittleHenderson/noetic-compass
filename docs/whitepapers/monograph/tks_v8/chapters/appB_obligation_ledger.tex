\chapter{The Proof-Obligation Ledger}\label{appB:ledger}

This appendix reproduces, as a reference, the complete proof and
integration ledger of the TKS v8.0 typed core prototype, exactly as
recorded in the governing specification
(\texttt{TKS\_V8\_CORE\_FORMAL\_SPEC\_AND\_PROOF\_OBLIGATIONS\_v0.1.md},
Section~5) at the bound snapshot. The discussion chapters of this
monograph cite these rows constantly; here they are gathered in one
place, first as a compact table and then row by row with the current
evidence and status text kept faithful to the source document. The
ledger is normative for the artifact: a claim not supported by a row's
recorded status is not a claim the artifact makes. Drafting this
monograph changed no row.

\section{Per-row status and evidence}\label{appB:sec:rows}

\subsection*{PO-TKS8-CORE-01}
\emph{Obligation.} Identity and associativity for finite-function
composition. \emph{Current evidence.} Pointwise argument; unit and
bounded exhaustive tests; exact Lean \texttt{TypedCategory} lemmas.
\emph{Status.} \textsc{partial formal verification present}.
\emph{Reading.} The mathematics is Chapter~4; the executable coverage
is the $19{,}683$ three-point associativity triples and the typed
identity families of Chapter~10; the formal fraction is the
\texttt{TypedCategory} module of Chapter~11. The residual is the
Python-to-Lean model equivalence, which no row of the ledger claims.

\subsection*{PO-TKS8-CORE-02}
\emph{Obligation.} Reject every source/target mismatch.
\emph{Current evidence.} Constructor and negative tests.
\emph{Status.} \textsc{executable validation}.
\emph{Reading.} The rejection sites are \texttt{compose}, the
\texttt{Endomap} validator, the \texttt{SemigroupoidPath} validator,
and catalog evaluation (Chapter~9); the negative fixtures of the
category and normal-form suites drive each site to its typed error.
Executable validation is the row's ceiling by design: the property is
about runtime behavior of the implementation.

\subsection*{PO-TKS8-CORE-03}
\emph{Obligation.} Keep one-carrier powers distinct from heterogeneous
paths. \emph{Current evidence.} Separate runtime types and negative
tests. \emph{Status.} \textsc{executable validation}.
\emph{Reading.} \texttt{Endomap} and \texttt{SemigroupoidPath} are
distinct runtime types; the path type exposes no power operation; the
endomap wrapper rejects non-endo arrows with
\texttt{CompositionTypeError}. The Lean seed's endomap-only powering
discipline (Chapter~11) independently mirrors the separation in the
model, though the ledger row itself records executable evidence.

\subsection*{PO-TKS8-CORE-04}
\emph{Obligation.} $f^{|X|!}$ is idempotent for finite endomaps.
\emph{Current evidence.} Standard finite-semigroup result; bounded
exhaustive test. \emph{Status.} \textsc{standard result /
formalization open}. \emph{Reading.} The tail-and-cycle proof is
Chapter~5; the exhaustive check covers all $289$ endomaps on carriers
through size four via the binary powering pathway. Open: a Lean proof
of the universal statement (functional-graph decomposition,
divisibility of $|X|!$ by all cycle lengths).

\subsection*{PO-TKS8-CORE-05}
\emph{Obligation.} Exact deterministic-lift norm formula,
$\lVert A_f\rVert^2 = \max_{y}|f^{-1}(y)|$.
\emph{Current evidence.} Block-Gram proof; bounded exhaustive
structural tests; Lean checks only the exact two-state expansion, not
the universal norm or the generic matrix/trace/rank bridge.
\emph{Status.} \textsc{informal proof present / universal
formalization open}. \emph{Reading.} The fiber-block spectral proof is
Chapter~7; the structural suites tie the implemented shortcuts (norm,
trace, rank, Gram forms) to matrix reality on the bounded family; the
kernel-checked fragment is the two-state constant-lift expansion and
noncontractivity (ledger record
\textsc{thm-lift-norm-fiber-formula}; counterexample record
\textsc{thm-lift-occupation-counterexample}).

\subsection*{PO-TKS8-CORE-06}
\emph{Obligation.} Soundness and leastness of the shortlex catalog.
\emph{Current evidence.} Finiteness and pruning argument; regression
tests. \emph{Status.} \textsc{informal correctness argument /
formalization open}. \emph{Reading.} The pruning-soundness induction
and the $|Y|^{|X|}$ finiteness bound are Chapter~8; the regression
suite verifies representatives, counts, rejection, and fail-closed
resource guards on hand-checkable catalogs (Appendix~A works one in
full). Open: formalization of the exploration invariants ---
shortlex-ordered dequeueing and first-insertion leastness.

\subsection*{PO-TKS8-CORE-07}

\emph{Current evidence.} Pinned Lean 4.32.1 seed and axiom audit at
\texttt{formal/lean}; exact scope in

\emph{Status.} \textsc{partial / present}.

\subsection*{PO-TKS8-CORE-11}
\emph{Obligation.} The standard-basis lift preserves identities and
composition. \emph{Current evidence.} $1{,}678$ exact bounded

\emph{Status.} \textsc{partial formal verification present /
finite-sum matrix bridge open}. \emph{Reading.} The executable half is

matrix statements the tests check.

\section{Reading conventions}\label{appB:sec:conventions}

Three conventions govern citation of this ledger elsewhere in the
repository and in downstream documents. First, \emph{status strings
are quoted, not paraphrased}: \textsc{executable validation} is not
``verified,'' \textsc{partial / present} is not ``formalized,'' and
\textsc{standard result} is not ``proved here.'' Second, \emph{rows do
not aggregate}: no combination of green rows yields a status any
single row lacks --- in particular, no combination amounts to
canonical TKS v8 status, CAS semantic transfer, or specialist review.
Third, \emph{changes are events}: a row's status changes only with a
new bound artifact snapshot (a revised specification and manifest with
new hashes), never by narrative reference. The ledger as printed here
is the ledger as of the v0.1 specification and the 2026-08-04
validation snapshot; any later document claiming a different status
must exhibit the later snapshot that grounds it.
