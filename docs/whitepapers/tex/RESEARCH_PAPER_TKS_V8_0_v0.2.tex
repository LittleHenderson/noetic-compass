\begin{abstract}
We develop, in full, a deliberately small executable reference kernel for the typed-composition layer of the Tootra Knowledge System (TKS) and its secular derivative, the Cognitive Architecture System (CAS). The kernel supplies five capabilities, each specified and proved here: (1) named finite objects with explicitly ordered carriers, whose identity discipline eliminates hash-order nondeterminism; (2) total finite-function arrows with declared source and target, and partial, type-checked categorical composition satisfying the category laws; (3) a hard type separation between one-carrier endomaps, which admit monoid powers and the exact idempotence theorem $f^{|X|!}\circ f^{|X|!}=f^{|X|!}$, and heterogeneous semigroupoid paths, which have composites but deliberately no powers; (4) a deterministic standard-basis lift $A_f e_x=e_{f(x)}$ into finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, for which we prove the exact operator-norm formula $\lVert A_f\rVert^2=\max_y|f^{-1}(y)|$ via a fiber-block Gram decomposition, the trace, rank, and isometry corollaries, functoriality, and a permanent noncontractivity counterexample; and (5) stable denotational normal forms by shortlex minimization over a completed finite closure, with a soundness proof for representative-first pruning and explicit resource semantics. We document the executable evidence exactly---bounded exhaustive suites covering $19{,}683$ associativity triples, $289$ endomaps, $499$ typed maps, and $1{,}678$ lift-functor composition pairs---and the pinned Lean~4.32.1 formal seed with its verified and open scope. The kernel maps no canonical Noetic or CAS construct onto arrows and makes no empirical claim; the complete proof-obligation ledger, integration boundaries, and reproduction procedure are included.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Motivation}
The TKS/CAS formal program (v7.4 through the v7.5--v7.7 extensions) defines a rich ontology---ten Noetics, four Domains, seven Drives, four Registers, and a disciplined operation notation---but its production notation compiler enforces value \emph{kinds} (state, drive, relation, program) rather than categorical \emph{source and target objects}. The Project Coherence mathematical red-team audit identified three concrete failure modes that a kind system cannot exclude:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Untyped composition.} Nothing prevents composing operations whose intermediate objects disagree; the result is a well-formed expression with no denotation.
\item \emph{Invalid power inference.} A sequence of heterogeneous, time-varying generators is not an element of one finite semigroup. Treating a generator sequence as ``one operator iterated $n$ times'' licenses false fixed-point and idempotence conclusions---for instance, importing Theorem~\ref{thm:factorial} below into a setting where no single endomap exists.
\item \emph{Nondeterministic identity.} If carrier order is left to hash order, basis indices, denotations, and canonical representatives depend on interpreter state (\texttt{PYTHONHASHSEED}), destroying replayability of every downstream digest.
\end{enumerate}

TKS v8.0 is the smallest kernel that turns each failure into a type error. It is an independent reference model: it does not modify, replace, or certify the active CAS notation compiler, and it deliberately refuses to encode the TKS/CAS ontology before that mapping is separately reviewed (Section~\ref{sec:boundaries}).

\subsection{Contributions and their evidential status}
Sections~\ref{sec:category}--\ref{sec:nf} develop the mathematics with complete proofs. Every claim is tagged with its registered status: the category laws and lift functoriality carry partial formal verification in a pinned Lean seed; the norm theorem carries an informal proof and exhaustive structural tests, with universal formalization open; the shortlex results carry an informal correctness argument; the factorial-power theorem is a standard result whose formalization is open. Section~\ref{sec:evidence} states the executable evidence exactly. Section~\ref{sec:ledger} reproduces the full proof-obligation ledger. Nothing in this paper is peer-reviewed, and no empirical, cognitive-dynamics, channel-contractivity, or CAS-efficacy claim follows from any result.

\section{The typed finite-function category}\label{sec:category}

\subsection{Objects}

\begin{definition}[Object]\label{def:object}
An \emph{object} is a pair $X=(\mathrm{name}_X,\mathrm{carrier}_X)$ where $\mathrm{carrier}_X$ is a finite, explicitly ordered sequence of distinct hashable elements. Object identity is the pair: two objects are equal iff their names and their carrier sequences (with order) are equal. We write $|X|$ for the carrier length and index carrier positions $0,\dots,|X|-1$, calling these the \emph{standard indices}.
\end{definition}

Two design decisions deserve justification.

\emph{Name-inclusive identity.} Carrier-only identity would silently coerce semantically distinct objects of equal carrier---for example, two different registers that both carry $\{0,1\}$. Composition legality (Definition~\ref{def:comp}) must track intent, not just arity, so the declared name participates in identity.

\emph{Ordered-input discipline.} Carrier elements, arrow mappings, path arrows, word labels, catalog objects, catalog generators, and lift coordinate vectors all reject sets, mappings, and one-shot iterables at construction. Their order changes basis indices (Section~\ref{sec:lift}), typed denotations, and shortlex rank (Section~\ref{sec:nf}); silently accepting hash-order iteration would make results depend on interpreter state. Callers must provide a replayable ordered sequence. This rule is enforced in the constructors of \texttt{category.py} and tested by negative fixtures.

\subsection{Arrows and composition}

\begin{definition}[Arrow]\label{def:arrow}
An \emph{arrow judgment} $f:X\to Y$ consists of declared source $X$, declared target $Y$, and a total map assigning to every standard index of $X$ one standard index of $Y$, represented as the tuple $(f(0),\dots,f(|X|-1))$. Arrows are \emph{extensionally equal} when source, target, and mapping tuple agree; presentation names (Section~\ref{sec:powers}) never affect equality.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Identity and composition]\label{def:comp}
For each object $X$, $\id_X$ is the arrow with $\id_X(i)=i$. For $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y'\to Z$, the composite $g\circ f$ is defined \emph{iff} $Y=Y'$ as objects (name and carrier), in which case
\[
(g\circ f)(i)=g(f(i)) : X\to Z .
\]
No cardinality-only or carrier-only coercion is performed; a source/target mismatch raises a typed error.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Category laws; \textnormal{status: partial formal verification present}]\label{thm:laws}
Under Definitions~\textup{\ref{def:object}--\ref{def:comp}}, identities are neutral and composition is associative wherever defined; the objects and arrows form a category $\FinArr$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Neutrality: $(f\circ\id_X)(i)=f(\id_X(i))=f(i)$ and $(\id_Y\circ f)(i)=\id_Y(f(i))=f(i)$ for every standard index $i$; sources and targets agree by construction. Associativity: if $f:X\to Y$, $g:Y\to Z$, $h:Z\to W$, then both $(h\circ g)\circ f$ and $h\circ(g\circ f)$ are defined, have source $X$ and target $W$, and satisfy
\[
((h\circ g)\circ f)(i)=h(g(f(i)))=(h\circ(g\circ f))(i).
\]
Extensional equality of arrows is exactly equality of these data.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Verification scope]\label{rem:lawsevidence}
The executable suite checks Theorem~\ref{thm:laws} exhaustively on a bounded family: all $19{,}683=(3^3)^3$ composable triples of endo-arrows on a fixed three-point carrier, plus typed families across distinct carriers ($499$ typed maps with source and target sizes through four). A pinned Lean~4.32.1 module (\texttt{TypedCategory}) kernel-checks the identity and associativity lemmas for the Lean model of these definitions, under the single standard axiom \texttt{propext}, with project axioms and \texttt{sorry} rejected by the verifier. The equivalence between the Python implementation and the Lean model is itself an open obligation (PO-01/PO-07 in Section~\ref{sec:ledger}); neither bounded testing nor the seed constitutes verification of the running code.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Endomaps, powers, and paths}\label{sec:powers}

The kernel's central discipline is a type-level separation of two constructions that informal operator notation conflates.

\begin{definition}[Endomap]\label{def:endomap}
$\mathrm{Endomap}(f)$ is constructible only when $f:X\to X$ with source and target the \emph{same} object. It carries the monoid powers $f^n$ for $n\ge 0$, with $f^0=\id_X$ and $f^{m+n}=f^m\circ f^n$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Semigroupoid path]\label{def:path}
A \emph{semigroupoid path} is a nonempty sequence
\[
X_0\xrightarrow{f_1}X_1\xrightarrow{f_2}\cdots\xrightarrow{f_n}X_n
\]
whose adjacent objects match (Definition~\ref{def:comp}). It has the typed composite $f_n\circ\cdots\circ f_1:X_0\to X_n$ and \emph{intentionally no power operation}: the constructor exposes no $(\,\cdot\,)^n$, and the runtime types are distinct, so a path cannot be passed where an endomap is required.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Why the separation is load-bearing]
Suppose a workflow applies generators $g_1,g_2,\dots$ in temporal order across changing state spaces. Nothing guarantees the $g_t$ are equal, or even endo. Any conclusion of the form ``after enough steps the process is idempotent'' requires a \emph{single} finite endomap (Theorem~\ref{thm:factorial}); for a path, the very expression $p^2$ is ill-typed unless $X_0=X_n$, and even then $p$ need not equal any single generator. The kernel makes the invalid inference unwritable rather than merely discouraged.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Safe finite power; \textnormal{ledger \textsc{thm-finite-idempotent-power}; status: standard result, formalization open}]\label{thm:factorial}
For every finite endomap $f:X\to X$, the power $f^{|X|!}$ is idempotent:
\[
f^{|X|!}\circ f^{|X|!}=f^{|X|!}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write $N=|X|$ and consider the functional graph of $f$ on the standard indices: each weakly connected component consists of trees hanging on a unique cycle. For any point $x$, the tail length (distance from $x$ to its cycle) is at most $N-1$, so after $N!\ge N-1$ (for $N\ge 2$; the cases $N\in\{0,1\}$ are immediate) iterations, $f^{N!}(x)$ lies on a cycle. Let $\ell\le N$ be that cycle's length. Since $\ell\mid N!$, a further $N!$ iterations traverse the cycle a whole number of times:
\[
f^{2N!}(x)=f^{N!}(x).
\]
As $x$ was arbitrary, $f^{2N!}=f^{N!}$, which is the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The suite verifies Theorem~\ref{thm:factorial} exhaustively for all $289=\sum_{k\le 4}$ (per-carrier) endomaps on carriers through size four. Lean formalization is open (PO-04).
\end{remark}

\begin{construction}[Bounded presentation names]\label{con:names}
Exponentiation uses binary powering. Presentation names for intermediate powers are bounded: large exponents are rendered by bit length plus a short SHA-256 label rather than full decimal conversion, so name construction is $O(\log n)$ in the exponent and cannot hit the host language's decimal-digit conversion limit. Regression tests cover $f^{2^{30}}$ and $f^{10^{5000}}$. Presentation names never enter extensional equality (Definition~\ref{def:arrow}).
\end{construction}

\section{The deterministic standard-basis lift}\label{sec:lift}

\subsection{Definition and elementary structure}

\begin{definition}[Lift]\label{def:lift}
For $f:X\to Y$, let $H_X$ and $H_Y$ be complex Hilbert spaces with orthonormal bases $(e_x)_{x\in X}$ and $(e_y)_{y\in Y}$ indexed by the ordered carriers. The \emph{deterministic standard-basis lift} is the linear map
\[
A_f:H_X\to H_Y,\qquad A_fe_x=e_{f(x)} .
\]
In matrix form, $(A_f)_{yx}=1$ if $f(x)=y$ and $0$ otherwise: a column-stochastic $0/1$ matrix with exactly one $1$ per column.
\end{definition}

Three operators derived from $A_f$ are kept distinct in the implementation, because informal usage tends to conflate them:

\begin{enumerate}
\item $A_f^{*}A_f$, the \emph{input-collision Gram matrix}: $(A_f^*A_f)_{x x'}=1$ iff $f(x)=f(x')$;
\item $A_fA_f^{*}$, diagonal with entries the fiber sizes $|f^{-1}(y)|$;
\item the \emph{codomain support projector}, the diagonal $0/1$ matrix marking nonempty fibers---not equal to $A_fA_f^{*}$ unless every fiber has size $\le 1$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The exact norm theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Exact operator norm; \textnormal{ledger \textsc{thm-lift-norm-fiber-formula}; status: informal proof present, universal formalization open}]\label{thm:norm}
For nonempty $X$, with the empty-domain value defined as zero,
\[
\lVert A_f\rVert^2=\max_{y\in Y}\bigl|f^{-1}(y)\bigr| ,\qquad
\lVert A_f\rVert=\sqrt{\max_{y\in Y}|f^{-1}(y)|}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
$\lVert A_f\rVert^2$ is the largest eigenvalue of the positive semidefinite $A_f^{*}A_f$. By the computation above, $(A_f^*A_f)_{xx'}=1$ iff $x,x'$ lie in the same fiber of $f$. Reorder the input basis grouping fibers together (a permutation similarity, which preserves spectrum): the Gram matrix becomes a direct sum $\bigoplus_{y\in\im f}J_{m_y}$ of all-ones blocks, $m_y=|f^{-1}(y)|$. The all-ones matrix $J_m$ has rank one with $J_m^2=mJ_m$; its eigenvalues are $m$ (eigenvector the all-ones vector) and $0$ with multiplicity $m-1$. Hence the spectrum of $A_f^*A_f$ is $\{m_y\}_{y\in\im f}\cup\{0\}$ (with multiplicities), and its maximum is $\max_y m_y$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Implemented consequences]\label{cor:conseq}
$\tr(A_f^{*}A_f)=|X|$; $\rank(A_f)=|\im(f)|$; and $A_f$ is an isometry iff $f$ is injective.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The trace is the sum of fiber sizes, which is $|X|$ by totality. The columns of $A_f$ span $\{e_y \mid y\in\im f\}$, giving the rank. Isometry means $A_f^*A_f=I$, i.e.\ every fiber has size exactly one on the diagonal and no off-diagonal collisions---precisely injectivity.
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[Permanent noncontractivity fixture; \textnormal{ledger \textsc{thm-lift-occupation-counterexample}}]\label{ex:constant}
Let $f$ be the constant map on a two-point carrier. Then
\[
A_f=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A_f^2=A_f,\qquad
\lVert A_f\rVert=\sqrt{2},\qquad
\tr(A_f^*A_f)=2,\qquad \rank A_f=1 .
\]
$A_f$ is idempotent yet not an orthogonal projector (it is not self-adjoint), not a partial isometry, and has norm exceeding one. The fixture permanently blocks two tempting general claims: ``deterministic lifts are contractive'' and ``idempotent lifts are projectors.'' The Lean seed kernel-checks this exact two-state expansion and its noncontractivity.
\end{example}

\subsection{Functoriality}

\begin{theorem}[Lift functor laws; \textnormal{status: partial formal verification, finite-sum matrix bridge open}]\label{thm:functor}
$A_{\id_X}=I_{H_X}$ and, whenever $g\circ f$ is defined, $A_{g\circ f}=A_gA_f$. Thus $A_{(-)}$ is a functor from $\FinArr$ to finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces and linear maps.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On basis vectors, $A_{\id_X}e_x=e_x$ and $A_gA_fe_x=A_ge_{f(x)}=e_{g(f(x))}=A_{g\circ f}e_x$; linear extension determines the maps. Functor laws for composition of three or more arrows follow from Theorem~\ref{thm:laws}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Exact verification scope]
Functoriality is checked by \emph{exact matrix equality} (integer entries, no floating point) for every valid typed-map pair on carriers through size three: $1{,}678$ composition pairs including empty carriers. The Lean seed kernel-checks the identity lift entrywise and on basis vectors, typed composition on basis vectors, and relational-product support composition. Open in Lean: finite-sum numerical matrix multiplication, the generic bridge from Example~\ref{ex:constant} to matrix/Gram/trace/rank definitions, and arbitrary-vector linearity (PO-05, PO-11).
\end{remark}

\begin{boundary}
The lift is a bookkeeping functor for finite deterministic maps. No stochastic kernel, quantum channel, contractivity, or dynamical claim follows; Example~\ref{ex:constant} exists precisely to prevent the contractivity reading.
\end{boundary}

\section{Stable denotational normal forms}\label{sec:nf}

\subsection{Typed words and shortlex}

\begin{definition}[Generator catalog and typed words]\label{def:catalog}
A \emph{catalog} is a finite, explicitly ordered sequence $G=(g_0,\dots,g_{k-1})$ of typed generators (arrows of $\FinArr$) over a finite object set, together with the declared order, which assigns each generator its \emph{rank}. A \emph{typed word} is a start object $X_0$ together with a sequence of generator labels whose arrows compose in sequence from $X_0$. The empty word at $X$ denotes $\id_X$; a nonempty word denotes the composite of its arrows. We write $\llbracket w\rrbracket$ for the denotation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Shortlex]\label{def:shortlex}
For typed words $u,v$ from the same start object, $u<_{\mathrm{slex}}v$ iff $|u|<|v|$, or $|u|=|v|$ and the rank sequence of $u$ is lexicographically smaller. For a reachable denotation $a$,
\[
\NF_G(a)=\min\nolimits_{<_{\mathrm{slex}}}\{\,w \text{ well-typed} \mid \llbracket w\rrbracket=a\,\}.
\]
\end{definition}

\subsection{The closure algorithm and its soundness}

\begin{algorithm}[Representative-first closure]\label{alg:closure}
Explore typed words in shortlex order (breadth-first by length, lexicographic by rank within a length). Maintain a table from extensional denotations to their first-discovered word. When a word's denotation is already in the table, record the duplicate and \emph{do not expand it}; otherwise store it and enqueue its one-generator extensions. Terminate when the frontier is empty; return the table.
\end{algorithm}

\begin{lemma}[Pruning soundness]\label{lem:pruning}
Algorithm~\textup{\ref{alg:closure}} computes $\NF_G$ on every reachable denotation.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Two invariants hold by induction on the exploration order. (i) Words are dequeued in shortlex order: extensions preserve relative shortlex order of their prefixes, since appending a fixed label adds one to both lengths and appends the same rank. (ii) If prefixes $u<_{\mathrm{slex}}u'$ satisfy $\llbracket u\rrbracket=\llbracket u'\rrbracket$, then for any suffix $s$ making $u's$ well-typed, $us$ is well-typed (denotations determine the object at the split point), $\llbracket us\rrbracket=\llbracket u's\rrbracket$, and $us<_{\mathrm{slex}}u's$. Hence pruning $u'$ removes no denotation and no shortlex-least representative: any word extending a pruned prefix is dominated by the corresponding extension of the retained representative. The first-discovered word for each denotation is therefore its shortlex minimum.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Finiteness]\label{prop:finite}
The closure terminates: for finite objects $X,Y$ there are at most $|Y|^{|X|}$ total maps $X\to Y$, and the catalog's object set is finite, so the set of reachable denotations is finite and each is expanded at most once.
\end{proposition}

\begin{construction}[Resource semantics]\label{con:resources}
The implementation returns only after closure completion. It deduplicates queued candidates by typed denotation and separately caps accepted denotations and the pending frontier; exceeding either bound raises \texttt{ClosureLimitExceeded}. A partial table is \emph{never} returned as canonical---the fail-closed alternative to silently truncated ``canonical'' forms.
\end{construction}

\begin{boundary}
Two boundaries are explicit and permanent. (i) $\NF_G$ is denotational minimization over a completed finite closure---\emph{not} a claim that any inherited TKS rewrite system terminates or is confluent; no rewrite system is defined here. (ii) Canonical forms are relative to the declared, versioned generator order: changing the order may change representatives without changing denotations. Cross-catalog comparison of normal forms is meaningless without a catalog identity.
\end{boundary}

\section{Integration boundaries and downstream role}\label{sec:boundaries}

\subsection{What is excluded}
Excluded and still \textsc{open}: parsing or evaluating TKS v7.4/v8 surface syntax; mapping the ten canonical Noetics or any CAS construct to kernel arrows; RPM monad data or laws; a rewrite system for inherited TKS equations; Noetic Topos 2.0 coverage and modalities; stochastic kernels, quantum channels, or empirical dynamics; and any transfer from the Ten Advances manuscript. All such research transfers remain Ring~4 / \textsc{not licensed}.

\subsection{Downstream boundary objects}
Three companion candidates consume the kernel strictly through its types:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{CAS Core 8} bridges legacy fractal digit sequences through an explicit caller-supplied catalog of typed arrows; composition uses semigroupoid paths, and missing digits or mismatches fail closed. The bridge proves type-correct composition only, not the meaning of a legacy digit.
\item \emph{CAS PCBA 8} may adapt only a certified total, finite, deterministic, stateless, non-stochastic component transition to a kernel arrow; chains become paths; feedback powers require a certified endomap. The adapter certificate binds the exact kernel bytes and carries no automatic CAS semantic theorem.
\item \emph{Noetic Topos 2.0} shares the type discipline (deterministic maps admitted; Markov kernels, channels, and enriched sheaves deliberately not identified with functions) and its future reconciliation with kernel objects is exactly PO-08.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

The categorical laws, the finite-semigroup idempotent-power fact, and the spectral analysis of $0/1$ column-selection matrices are standard mathematics \cite{maclane,pin,horn}; the kernel's contribution is an \emph{engineering discipline}---name-inclusive object identity, ordered-input rejection of hash-order nondeterminism, the endomap/path type separation, fail-closed closure semantics, and hash-bound artifacts---rather than new theorems. Property-preserving executable models with bounded exhaustive testing follow the small-scope tradition \cite{jackson}; the pinned single-axiom Lean seed follows standard kernel-checking practice \cite{lean4}. No novelty search has been commissioned; the ledger records all mathematical content as standard results or definitions.

\section{Conclusion}

TKS v8.0 contributes a disciplined foundation rather than a sweeping theory: typed composition that cannot silently mistype (Theorem~\ref{thm:laws}), powers that exist only where a monoid exists (Definitions~\ref{def:endomap}--\ref{def:path}, Theorem~\ref{thm:factorial}), a lift whose exact norm is proved rather than assumed (Theorem~\ref{thm:norm}) and whose one permanent counterexample blocks the two standard over-readings (Example~\ref{ex:constant}), and canonical forms that are deterministic because their inputs are ordered and their closure is completed or refused (Lemma~\ref{lem:pruning}, Construction~\ref{con:resources}). The kernel's refusal to encode the TKS/CAS ontology before review is deliberate: the mapping of canon onto substrate is itself a gated research obligation (PO-08), and preserving that boundary---with the same precision as the proofs---is part of the result.
