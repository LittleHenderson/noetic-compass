## What the Ohm translation means

The equations are useful as hypotheses that can be measured:

- external `V_ext` is desire/stimulus/trigger/`^8` Above-Cause;
- `I_internal` is a registered internal response;
- `G_eff` is the fitted response slope; and
- `R_eff = 1/G_eff` only when the slope is stable and nonzero.

The candidate relationship is `Delta I = G_eff * Delta V_ext + error`.
`R_eff` must be estimated on calibration groups and predict untouched
groups. Calculating `R=V/I` on one observation and using `V=IR` to
restate that same observation is circular and proves nothing.

The CJT bias `B` is a separate control coordinate. Corrected v7.4 RPM is
the one-way Desire-to-Wisdom-to-Power prerequisite gate around or upstream of
the CJT; it is not a fourth CJT terminal. Internal intervention amplitude
`alpha` is not external `V_ext` unless a prospective calibration
establishes the link.
