`docs/working/CAS_HARNESS_CJT_EXTERNAL_DRIVE_ONTOLOGY_20260721.md`.

hidden-state intervention dose are different variables.

Candidate measurements include:

- `V_ext = R_eff * I_internal` within a validated linear operating range;
- `R_eff = Delta V_ext / Delta I_internal`;
- `beta = dE/dC` at fixed bias;
- `g_m = dE/dB` at fixed cause; and
- `dE/dalpha` for a separately registered internal intervention.

An internal dose `alpha` is not automatically `V_ext`. A prospective
calibration must connect them. Competing null, affine, reciprocal, saturating,
Hill, and piecewise curves must be compared on untouched data. If the relation
is linear only locally, CAS-Ohm is a small-signal approximation rather than a
global law.

These quantities can guide hidden-state probing, prompt-injection response
tests, and output-control experiments. They are not physical volts, amperes, or
ohms, and a useful measurement analogy is not proof of a physical electrical
law inside a model.
