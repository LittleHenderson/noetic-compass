## Interpretation boundary

The Stage-7 role-bound bank was generated from original TKS Foundation glosses, and the Foundation-28 v0 bank was generated by the same DRIVES/REGISTERS vocabulary used by the compact comparator. They are shared across arms but are not independently authored neutral holdouts. Stage-7 counterfactual pairs also share broader paraphrase-family and scenario-generator dependencies; pair clustering does not eliminate that family-level dependence. Results can measure source-language alignment and must not be treated as decisive semantic transfer evidence.
