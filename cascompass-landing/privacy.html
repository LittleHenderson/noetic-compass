Current public surface

What the landing site collects

  • The version-controlled landing pages contain no account form and run no client-side application code.
  • The early-access link opens an email addressed to hello@cascompass.ai; the sender chooses what the message contains.
  • Hosting and email providers may process ordinary request or delivery metadata while providing their services.

Invited beta work

Audit data requires a separate agreement

If beta access is offered, audit inputs, generated outputs, run metadata, and evidence artifacts may be processed to perform and verify the requested evaluation. The written beta agreement and disclosed runtime configuration control that work.

Do not submit regulated data, confidential data, personal secrets, access credentials, or production keys through the public email link. Final retention, deletion, and data-subject procedures remain OPEN.

Questions and requests

Contact the project

Send privacy questions or requests to hello@cascompass.ai. Include only the information needed to identify the request.
