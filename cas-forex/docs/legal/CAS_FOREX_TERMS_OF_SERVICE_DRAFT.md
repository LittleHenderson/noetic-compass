# CAS Forex Terms of Service Draft

Status: Draft for local development. Counsel review required before public, hosted, paid, or multi-user use.

CAS Forex is an owner-operated trading cockpit for automation, risk controls, broker/terminal monitoring, and audit evidence. It does not promise profitable results, account growth, execution quality, withdrawal success, or future outcomes.

## Owner Responsibility

The owner is responsible for broker account setup, credentials, funding decisions, strategy configuration, risk limits, live authorization switches, and any decision to use live money.

## Automated Trading

Automated and AI-assisted trading can lose money. CAS Forex may help enforce limits, record decisions, block some unsafe actions, and surface broker/account evidence, but it cannot remove market risk or broker risk.

## Owner-Armed Autonomous Authority

The product intent is owner-armed autonomous execution, not advisory-only
signaling.

If the owner has enabled the relevant live or money controls, CAS Forex may
autonomously place or manage trades, take profit, close or reduce exposure, and
initiate configured money movement inside the delegated authority envelope. The
owner is responsible for deciding whether to arm that authority, how wide to
set the risk and transfer limits, and whether the connected broker/provider
setup is appropriate.

## Runtime Gates And Emergency Controls

Autonomous authority does not mean unrestricted authority. Side effects remain
subject to guarded runtime paths and may be blocked by fresh broker-truth
requirements, provider health, risk caps, margin or exposure checks,
destination allowlists, required receipts, recovery conditions, or master kill
or emergency-stop controls.

## Broker Boundary

Broker pricing, spreads, swaps, leverage, margin, order acceptance, fills, withdrawals, and final account records are controlled by the broker or terminal. CAS Forex should reconcile against broker state as the source of truth.

## Owner Controls and Audit

Live trading and configured money movement require explicit owner authorization
at setup time plus action-time gate checks. Risk-cap loosening, AI-dial
loosening, and manual orders require anti-impulse journal evidence. Audit
records, receipts, rationale summaries, and event hashes are diagnostic
evidence and do not prove profitability or regulatory compliance.

## Public Launch Gap

Before any public launch, these terms need qualified legal review, jurisdiction-specific risk language, support/contact terms, refund/billing terms if applicable, and regulatory/compliance review.
