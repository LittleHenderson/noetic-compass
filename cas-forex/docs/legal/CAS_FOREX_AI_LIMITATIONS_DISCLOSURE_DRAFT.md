# CAS Forex AI Limitations Disclosure Draft

Status: draft for owner review and qualified legal/compliance review before any
public, hosted, paid, or multi-user use.

This draft is not legal advice and is not approval for launch or live-money
use.

## AI Is Fallible

The CAS Forex AI Operator, assistant, and related scoring or strategy systems
can be wrong, stale, incomplete, unavailable, blocked, or overconfident.

AI outputs may be affected by:

- bad or delayed data;
- missing or stale broker/account truth;
- spread, slippage, or liquidity conditions;
- broker rejections or terminal issues;
- misconfigured owner limits or authority settings; and
- strategy behavior that does not generalize across market regimes.

## AI Edge Language

CAS Forex may describe the AI as the adaptive trading edge or adaptive
supervisory layer. That does not mean the AI guarantees profitable results or
superior judgment in every condition.

## No Win-Probability Claim By Default

Confidence, conviction, or edge language should not be treated as a guaranteed
win probability unless a specific audited metric explicitly defines it.

## No Removal Of Human Responsibility

Even when the owner has armed standing autonomous authority, the owner remains
responsible for deciding whether to use the product, which broker/provider to
connect, how much money to risk, and whether the product is appropriate for the
owner's situation.
