The HELM Cube: a concept for HELM's final home

The HELM Cube is a concept for HELM's final home: a small black cube with no buttons that listens, talks, sees and maps its room. This page is a design only. Nothing has been built, bought or measured, and no money was spent.

"Gyrator" means gyroscope. The second ruling is the one the owner called the main thing: when it is off, the cube is a plain black box, the same on every face.

HELM is in charge

Every sensor feeds HELM, and HELM holds the controls: which sensors run, when the cube maps, what the light shows, which home devices it controls. Each action goes through the RPM Gate (HELM's one gate: it lets a step go ahead, sends it to repair, or refuses, and lets the brain say it cannot tell) and leaves a receipt (the record a saved fact leaves behind, with a fingerprint, so it can be checked later). A fact HELM lacks, such as a corner of the room it never measured, stays UNDETERMINED (HELM's honest 'I cannot tell', used when something it needs is missing): HELM asks instead of guessing, and never draws a wall it did not measure.

Two lines stay outside all software, HELM included. The camera-on light is wired to each camera's power, and the hard mute is a physical switch. HELM can turn any camera or microphone on or off; it cannot hide that it did. This keeps six hidden cameras trustworthy. It is the operator's proposal, kept unless the owner overrules it.

Who decides when HELM looks deeper?

The owner also asked whether the Unity Eye (a small watcher that looks over HELM, reports and whispers, and never writes) or another reasoning part could decide when HELM needs to look deeper into a problem. The answer, in short:

  • An exact arithmetic rule should decide, not the Eye. The owner's own decoupling stop already does this. On HELM's made-up cascade exam it read DECOUPLING_EARNED: 203 deeper looks instead of 609, with more good answers (§7.69).
  • The Eye checks the rule from outside. By design it never writes and never gates, and its acting is NOT LICENSED (§7.38). With a new ruling it could ask for more depth, never for less, so the worst it could cause is extra work, not a wrong answer.
  • Today's live chat has no depth choice to make. Every repair opens one level, so a decider there would measure nothing. Depth becomes a real choice when an ingredient has its own recipe.

What is hard

  • Six cameras running at once needs a hotter, phone-class chip; the plan is one camera at a time, chosen by where the voice came from.
  • Hiding a camera behind dark glass costs it light, so pictures get noisy in dim rooms.
  • Six separate speakers do not fit usefully; one driver with two passive radiators sends sound out through slots on every face.
  • The law expects a regulatory mark on the device; the closest fit to the plain-black-box rule is a black-on-black etch on the charge face.
  • In the EU, from 18 February 2027, portable batteries must be removable by the user, so an EU version needs an openable face.
  • It cannot charge fast and talk at the same time: both share one small heat budget.
