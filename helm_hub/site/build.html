Engineered, not validated: built and working; no validated result yet

What this animation does not claim. It shows how the parts are wired and the recorded status of each. It does not claim that any part works for real users, that the intended scene will happen, or anything wider than each part's own record. The moving dot is an illustration, not a measurement.

Today

  1. English in: you type an everyday sentence, such as 'we have 12 flour and 20 water'.
  2. The hand-written reader restates it in HELM's checked form. Its stock-frame rule saves a stock fact only when the sentence really states one (earned its keep; on fresh real sentences safe but not yet useful, §7.87).
  3. The round-trip check turns the checked form back into English and reads it again; it must come back identical. It proves the form, not the truth.
  4. The RPM Gate is HELM's one gate. It picks one of three exits: go ahead, repair (back to the reader), or ask.
  5. Go: the licensed recipe brain divides what is on hand by what one unit needs and names the ingredient that runs out first. It knows 8 ingredients and 4 recipe shapes, with 32 learned slots. Beside it, the wide brain waits behind a switch that is OFF: a candidate, validated for one sentence on real recipes; the first pair wave (§7.93) left it a candidate and the re-run (§7.96) made it the candidate across the switches; the owner's word of 2026-10-20 made it canonical (§7.56), and the live loop now runs it by default.
  6. Side rails: the cube store keeps facts and never fills in an unknown; the triad weighs Purpose, Wisdom and Power on each ingredient; the cascade breaks a problem into smaller ones, with a stop rule and a depth limit.
  7. Receipt: nothing is saved until it is read back and checked; then it leaves a receipt, a fingerprinted record anyone can check later.
  8. Voice: HELM writes one to three plain sentences and reads its own numbers and names back against the receipt.
  9. English out: you get the answer in everyday words.
  10. Ask: if HELM cannot read the sentence or cannot tell, the ask exit sends one question and saves nothing.
  11. Outside the solid line, the CTT ear (a small translator, 9,585,566 parameters) may propose a reading, but only on the ask path, and only as a question you confirm. It shares nothing with the brain. On record: no gain on real sentences (EARS_NO_GAIN) and a failed safety check on AI-written ones (EARS_HARMED), §7.90. A follow-up that lets it speak only when its three copies agree could not read its real-sentence half (INCONCLUSIVE, §7.95); its AI-written half is reported only.
  12. Above it all, the Unity Eye scans HELM's records for damage and whispers suggestions. The crossed-out arrow is the rule: it never writes. Today it is not wired to the live chat window.
  13. Between turns, the idle drive loop moves HELM's attention among the seven Drives (F1 to F7) toward unfinished work. It freezes while a conversation turn passes.

Intended

  1. Same turn, same checks: the reader goes first, then the round-trip check, then the RPM Gate.
  2. The main slot is where the wide recipe brain is meant to go. The pair wave (HELM's sixth test wave) ran twice: the first run (§7.93) read WIDE_WAVE_NO_GAIN on one unexplained ask, a reader defect that was then fixed; the re-run (§7.96) read WIDE_WAVE_EARNED_KEEP: no harm where the marks look, 93 of 94 real recipes learned, 0 wrong, 0 unexplained asks. The wide brain became the candidate across the switches, and on the owner's word of 2026-10-20 it is HELM's canonical brain (§7.56). The live loop and the chat now run it by default (HELM_WIDE_BRAIN=0 runs the licensed reference brain alone).
  3. The licensed brain stays beside it as the reference; its one licensed sentence does not change.
  4. The CTT ear is admitted only through a stricter rule: all three trained copies must agree exactly, and HELM asks by default (CTT-2R, preregistered, nothing scored). Planned.
  5. Anything the ear proposes is still read back to you as a question before it counts; nothing is saved without your yes.
  6. Receipt, voice and English out work as today.
  7. The Unity Eye's scan and pairing are wired to the live chat. It still only reports and whispers, and never writes. Planned.
  8. The idle drive loop runs live between turns, and still pauses during a conversation turn. Planned.
  9. The domain ring widens beyond the kitchen, one sealed test at a time: software work (planned), everyday planning (partly backed), research governance (planned), safety as a recipe (planned), physical systems (planned), science (planned), receipts for outputs (partly backed).
  10. Any claim about real people typing to HELM must come from an exam that people write (E-HUMAN-W). None has run. Planned.
