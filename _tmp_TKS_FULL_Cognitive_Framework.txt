   Plain English: What is the Overton Window?

   The Simple Version: The Overton Window is the range of ideas that society considers
   acceptable to discuss at any given time. Ideas inside the window can be debated openly;
   ideas outside are dismissed as extreme, crazy, or unthinkable. The window is not xedit
   shifts over time as society changes.
