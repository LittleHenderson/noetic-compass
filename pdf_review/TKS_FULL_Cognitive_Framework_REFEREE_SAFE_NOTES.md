## Purpose

This file records the editorial policy used to construct
`TKS_FULL_Cognitive_Framework_REFEREE_SAFE.tex` from the original
`TKS_FULL_Cognitive_Framework.tex`.

## Retained

- Formal symbol definitions and core notation where they can be stated without
  empirical overclaim.
- The D/W/P ordering only as an adopted axiom of the framework.
- Simple mathematically correct consequences of stated definitions.
- Imported standard mathematics such as fixed-point, sheaf, lacunarity, and
  diffusion templates, clearly labeled as imported rather than TKS-specific
  discoveries.
- Psychology, social influence, predictive intelligence, and AI material only
  when relabeled as `Threat Model`, `Heuristic`, or `Application Hypothesis`.

## Removed or Downgraded

- Any theorem-style statement lacking an actual proof.
- Empirical claims about psychological profiling, vulnerability, stabilization,
  or prediction.
- Claims that TKS solves major AI problems.
- Thresholds presented as if calibrated.
- Scenario writing that reads like deployment guidance for manipulation or
  surveillance.

## Editorial Rule

Every retained substantive claim is labeled as one of:

- `Definition`
- `Axiom`
- `Imported Standard Result`
- `Threat Model`
- `Heuristic`
- `Application Hypothesis`
- `Proved Statement`

## Honest Status

The referee-safe reduction is narrower and more honest than the original source,
but it does not validate the original framework scientifically. It only reduces
the claim surface to something a referee can read without being asked to accept
unsupported psychological, social, or AI conclusions.
