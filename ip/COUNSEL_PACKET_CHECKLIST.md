## 3. What Not To Say Publicly Before Filing

Do **not** say:

- [ ] “patent pending”
- [ ] “fully protected IP”
- [ ] “the patent is filed” unless it is actually filed
- [ ] new detailed technical architecture claims in public
- [ ] new public benchmark claims that reveal additional invention detail
- [ ] new public screenshots, diagrams, or PDFs that materially expand the disclosure

Do **not** do:

- [ ] new public repo pushes with invention-critical details unless counsel says timing is safe
- [ ] new public demos without checking whether they broaden disclosure
- [ ] public posting of the provisional drafts or counsel packet
- [ ] treating AI-assisted drafting as a substitute for counsel review
