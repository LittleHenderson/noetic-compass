## Minimum useful episode record

Extend existing records instead of creating a second hierarchy. Required meanings:
- CAS parent definition/version; real components and configuration versions.
- Exact model source, architecture, checkpoint, input and permitted-data split.
- Capture site/shape/coordinate semantics/round and observed coverage.
- Detector/probe identity, training split and CAS-label provenance.
- Predeclared hypothesis, action, donor (if any), matched control and resources.
- Original raw prediction, changed raw prediction, checked task outcome; feedback visibility.
- Native caller/branch reference, accepted effect or refusal, authority and research retention.
- Actor at each consequential decision, uncertainty, failures and alternatives.
- Artifact identities, replay result, exact allowed export, overhead/cost and cleanup.

## Initial experiment outline (planning template, not already frozen)

Use a preserved development-selected checkpoint in a new diagnostic job. Choose a small permitted Parts/Reach fixture set with known structure and no model-visible target. Prove passive neutrality first. Use discovery cases to name a candidate response configuration, then fix the candidate before confirmation. Execute the baseline, no-op, one selected intervention and matched control on the held-apart confirmation structures. Score through the unchanged independent evaluator. Record the task effect even when zero or negative. Do not choose the intervention after seeing the confirmation answer.

A known injected fault is a calibration check for the diagnostic apparatus, not evidence of a natural HELM fault. The empirical natural-failure pilot may follow within the admitted envelope; stop rather than erase the distinction if the first hypothesis fails.
