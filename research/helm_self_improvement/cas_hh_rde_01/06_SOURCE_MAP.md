## Primary methodological references

1. Hewitt & Liang, Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks (EMNLP 2019), https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/. Control tasks and selectivity help distinguish model-readable information from a probe learning the task itself. This supplies a research method, not a result about HELM.
2. Zhang & Nanda, Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching in Language Models: Metrics and Methods, https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042. Intervention construction and scoring choices can materially alter localization findings; fix them and use controls. This is not a claim that HELM is a transformer language model.
3. Elhage et al., Toy Models of Superposition (2022), https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/index.html. Features can be distributed across coordinates; single-neuron and subspace descriptions must be distinguished. Toy-model results are not empirical proof of HELM's internal representation.
