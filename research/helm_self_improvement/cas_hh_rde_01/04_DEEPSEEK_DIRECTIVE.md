## Engineering: independent executable controls

Write probes in your reviewer-only area for:
- observation/no-op neutrality and actual hook coverage;
- a deliberately known injected mechanism, clearly separated from natural failures;
- failure of an intervention that was never applied;
- wrong parent/site/shape/round/basis rejection;
- matching intervention size/rank/norm and capture overhead;
- task-score changes rather than probe-score changes alone;
- restored state/hook cleanup after interruption or invalid action;
- unavailable hidden-state behavior for an output-only endpoint;

- tamper/truncation detection with authoritative identities outside candidate access.

A projected output coordinate is not automatically a neuron. Attention weights or top-K activity are observations, not causal conclusions. Suppression degradation without controls can be nonspecific damage. A rescue from a gold-chosen donor is a diagnostic upper bound, not deployable improvement.
