# NON-GOALS — CAS-RRK v0.1 Phase 1

Frozen from the design brief (cas_only_reasoner/cas_reasoner.txt). The
first kernel pass builds none of these:

- No raw-English understanding.
- No English generation.
- No neural model.
- No LLM inside the reasoning loop.
- No recursive source-code rewriting.
- No autonomous architectural promotion.
- No constitutional learning.
- No external network calls.
- No tool execution.
- No physical CAS-Ohm interpretation.
- No claim of consciousness.
- No claim of general intelligence.
- No production safety claim.

English learning and recursive self-evolution belong on the roadmap, not
in the first kernel implementation. The alternative is a scheduler,
theorem engine, memory system, language learner, self-modifying
architecture, compiler integration, and experimental neural bridge in
one gulp. That is how software becomes soup with version numbers.
