# The mathematical traps we must kill before GPU spend

## 1. V and R are not automatically identifiable

Only the ratio matters:

$$
g=\frac VR
$$

Multiplying both by the same positive constant leaves the conductance unchanged:

$$
\frac{cV}{cR}=\frac VR
$$

Therefore, unless Drive and resistance have independent measurement sources, the system may arbitrarily move meaning between them.

The rule must be:

```text
If V and R are independently grounded:
the separate Drive/resistance interpretation is permitted.

If only V/R is observable:
report CONDUCTANCE_ONLY.
Do not claim the decomposition was identified.
```

## 2. Weight modulation may secretly equal output scaling

For a simple linear layer:

$$
gWx=g(Wx)
$$

Scaling the weight and scaling the output are identical.

Therefore CJT-OHM must carry:

```text
PROOF_OF_NON_EQUIVALENCE_REQUIRED
```

The weight mechanism must operate inside a nonlinear or recurrent transformation where changing \(W^{\text{eff}}\) changes the transformation’s direction, not merely its volume.

A useful test is to compare context-conditioned Jacobians:

$$
J_1=\frac{\partial E}{\partial C}\Big|_{\text{context 1}}
\qquad
J_2=\frac{\partial E}{\partial C}\Big|_{\text{context 2}}
$$

Then test whether one is merely a scalar multiple of the other:

$$
\min_c
\frac{\|J_1-cJ_2\|_F}{\|J_1\|_F}
$$

If this is essentially zero, CAS-Ohm produced gain modulation, not a new transformation.

That is still useful, but it must be labeled honestly.

## 3. Positive resistance cannot encode semantic direction

Resistance should remain nonnegative.

Do not encode:

```text
attraction:
positive resistance

aversion:
negative resistance
```

Instead:

```text
CJT transformation matrices:
carry direction, sign and operation

CAS-Ohm conductance:
controls participation magnitude
```

## 4. V/R cannot see the gold answer

The signals used to compute Drive and resistance must be available before the answer:

```text
current goal
declared knowledge availability
resource budget
context match
prior committed verifier history
operating state
```

They may not contain:

```text
gold operation
gold answer
current verifier verdict
protected label
answer-derived resistance
```

## 5. Same-tick feedback remains prohibited

Current verification may change **next-tick** resistance or Bias only after commitment.

The project’s frozen tick order already requires routing from \(Z_{t-1}\), followed by execution, verification, diagnosis, proposed updates, atomic commitment, and trace sealing.

# First, the ordinary neuron

A normal neuron is:

$$
y=\phi(xW+b)
$$

Your earlier mapping becomes:

```text
x       = incoming Cause

W       = learned transformation

b       = local operating offset

xW + b  = transformed internal state

y       = Effect
```

Now apply the electrical version of Ohm’s law.

Since:

$$
I=\frac{V}{R}=V\left(\frac{1}{R}\right)
$$

we can define conductance:

$$
G=\frac{1}{R}
$$

Then:

$$
I=VG
$$

This looks remarkably close to:

$$
y=xW
$$

So, at the simplest mathematical level:

```text
neural input x       ↔ applied potential V

neural weight W      ↔ conductance G = 1/R

weighted activation  ↔ current I
```

That is a legitimate structural bridge.

But there is one critical correction: in your CAS formulation, **Cause is not Drive**, and attention flow is not the Effect. Cause is the information entering the CJT. Drive is the pressure supporting its activation. The Effect is the transformed output.  

Therefore, we need two interacting paths:

```text
CONTENT PATH:
Cause → CJT transformation → candidate Effect

FLOW PATH:
Drive / Resistance → attention allocation → how strongly that CJT participates
```

# CAS-Ohm could control three different kinds of weight

The word **weight** is hiding three separate jobs.

| Weight type               | Meaning                                   | CAS-Ohm role                                      |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Transformation weight** | How information is converted inside a CJT | Modulate, but not necessarily create from scratch |
| **Routing weight**        | How much each CJT contributes             | Natural CAS-Ohm role                              |
| **Plasticity weight**     | How much each CJT is allowed to learn     | Natural CAS-Ohm role after verification           |

## 1. Routing weights

This is the safest first implementation:

$$
\alpha_j
=
\operatorname{Normalize}
\left(
m_j\rho_j\psi(Z_j)\frac{V_j}{R_j+\epsilon}
\right)
$$

CAS-Ohm determines which CJTs receive attention and compute.

## 2. Effective transformation weights

CAS-Ohm can scale a CJT’s learned matrix:

$$
W^{\text{eff}}_{j,t}
=
\alpha_{j,t}W_j
$$

Or at a finer level:

$$
W^{\text{eff}}_t
=
G^{\text{CAS}}_t
\odot
W^{\text{base}}
$$

where:

$$
G^{\text{CAS}}_t
=
\frac{1}{R_t+\epsilon}
$$

This means the learned semantic transformation remains, but CAS-Ohm dynamically opens or restricts its pathways.

A more powerful low-rank version is:

$$
W^{\text{eff}}_t
=
W_0
+
A
\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_t)
B^\top
$$

Here CAS-Ohm selects how strongly several learned weight directions combine.

That is a practical way for CAS-Ohm to **literally generate part of the active weight matrix** without having to calculate billions of individual parameters.

## 3. Plasticity weights

CAS-Ohm could also decide which parameters are permitted to change:

$$
\Delta\Theta_{j,t}
=
-\eta
\lambda_{j,t}
\nabla_{\Theta_j}L
$$

with:

$$
\lambda_{j,t}
=
m^{\text{learn}}_j
\cdot
\alpha_{j,t}
\cdot
\chi(Z_{j,t})
\cdot
\mathbf{1}[\text{verifier accepts}]
$$

So:

```text
gradient:
proposes the direction of learning

CAS-Ohm:
sets how much learning flow reaches that CJT

verifier:
decides whether the update is based on a valid Effect

governance:
decides whether the update is permitted

commitment:
makes it permanent or rolls it back
```

That could be especially important for catastrophic forgetting.

Instead of updating the entire neural network every time:

```text
only the relevant CJTs receive plasticity
unrelated CJTs remain protected
quarantined CJTs cannot update
saturated CJTs receive reduced learning
failed Effects do not automatically train the system
```

A CJT can operate strongly and still produce the wrong Effect.

## Resistance must not be defined circularly

The CAS-Ohm research paper explicitly rejects that same-sample identity as circular. Resistance must be calibrated on one split and tested on a disjoint split, with uncertainty and model-specific units.

# Your intuition is close. The key correction is where the **Cause** and **Bias** really live.

A normal artificial neuron is usually written:

$$
y=\phi(xW+b)
$$

where:

* \(x\) is the input;
* \(W\) is the learned weight;
* \(b\) is the learned offset;
* \(\phi\) is the activation function;

The first and third ideas are directionally right, but the more exact mapping is:

So the best plain-English sentence is:

The project documents explicitly warn that CJT **Bias is not merely the neural bias term \(b\)**. It is meant to be an entire stateful subcircuit containing experience, memory, readiness, operating condition, transformation parameters, and governance.

# How this improves on an ordinary neuron

## 1. It becomes history-sensitive

An ordinary feed-forward neuron gives the same response to the same \(x\), assuming its weights have not changed.

A CJT can respond differently because:

```text
its recent memory changed
its last Effect was rejected
a prerequisite was acquired
it entered saturation
it was quarantined
its current goal changed
```

So:

$$
E_t
=
f(C_t,B_t)
$$

rather than merely:

$$
E=f(C)
$$

## 2. It knows what transformation it is supposed to perform

Ordinary hidden units are generally anonymous.

A CJT has a registered operation:

```text
attract
avert
decompose
trigger
compare
bind
retrieve
verify
```

That makes it possible to inspect, replace, test, and compose modules by function.

## 3. It can distinguish different reasons for failure

A normal neuron mostly produces a larger or smaller activation.

A CJT can say:

```text
This Cause is irrelevant.

I lack the necessary knowledge.

I understand the task but lack capacity.

The output contradicted the evidence.

The operation saturated.

No permitted expert is ready.
```

Those failures demand different repairs.

## 4. It can open an RPM repair

Instead of merely lowering confidence:

```text
WISDOM block:
retrieve memory, ask a question, use a tool, or learn

```

The frozen CJT tick order already requires readiness, routing, execution, verification, diagnosis, intent emission, atomic commitment, and sealed tracing as separate stages. 

## 5. Its output is a candidate, not automatically truth

A neuron emits a number and the network passes it onward.

A CJT emits:

```text
candidate Effect
```

The verifier then decides whether that Effect receives publication authority.

That creates a distinction between:

```text
the network generated it

and

the system accepted it
```

## 6. Learning becomes governed

In ordinary backpropagation, an optimizer changes weights when the loss says to.

In a CJT network, the intended learning flow is:

```text

        ↓
Effect is evaluated
        ↓
verifier produces evidence
        ↓
CJT proposes an update
        ↓

        ↓
```

The gradient can still calculate a proposed weight change. CAS governs whether that change becomes part of the continuing machine.

## 7. It supports persistent specialization

Each CJT can have:

```text

its own memory

```

That is much more like a cognitive circuit than an anonymous layer.

# The local gain mapping

The neuron’s local gain is not simply \(W\).

For:

$$
y=\phi(Wx+b)
$$

the local derivative is:

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}
=
W\phi'(Wx+b)
$$

That is a strong mapping to CJT gain \(\beta\):

$$
\beta
=
\frac{\partial E}{\partial C}
$$

The same weight \(W\) can produce different gains depending on where the junction is operating.

For example:

```text
cutoff:
φ′ ≈ 0, little Effect change

active:
φ′ is substantial, Cause changes Effect

saturation:
φ′ ≈ 0 again, more Cause adds little Effect
```

This is why the activation function matters. Without \(\phi\), stacking linear neurons gives only another linear transformation.

A full CJT adds semantic verification because a mathematically active response can still be the **wrong Effect**. Numerical activation alone cannot determine correctness.
