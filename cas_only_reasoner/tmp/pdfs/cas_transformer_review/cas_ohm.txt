 A three-stage understanding → evaluation → response cascade. Each stage can have a local Drive, constraint, attention reading, bias,
 and gain.




  What multiplies                                                               What gets handed forward
                                                                                The effect state and its activation become the
    β_total = β₁ × β₂ × ... × βₙ                                                next stage's cause. A cognitive cascade is not
  Gain in a CJT cascade multiplies. High gain amplifies                         identical to a wire carrying one conserved
  both signal and error.                                                        electrical current.



  Common confusion: in a literal electrical series circuit, current is common through every component.
  In a CJT cascade, activation can be amplified, attenuated, saturated, or reshaped at each stage.
  Therefore read I locally at TP1, TP2, TP3, and so on.

  Series subpaths                                Parallel subpaths                              Cascade gain

  Recursive measurement rule: compute the parent equivalent for overview, but never discard the
  child readings. A healthy parent total can hide one overloaded branch and one starved branch that
  cancel numerically.

   branch_A: V=6, R1=3 → I1=2
   branch_B: V=6, R2=6 → I2=1
   parallel: R_eq=2      → I_parent=3
   probes: TP_A, TP_B, TP_parent
 }
