A pair needs the same role, the same Mind, the same place and, where the role has one, the same related thing. Pairs divided by (pairs plus entries only one team wrote) must reach the pass mark. 2. **The presence mark and the minimum counts.** The teams must also agree on which passages hold the role, well beyond luck. This number is kappa: 0 is luck, 1 is full agreement. Proposed: at least 0.60. A role gets a verdict only with at least 50 passages where a team wrote it as clear and at least 50 where neither did.
3. **"Pointing at the same place."** The two labels overlap in the passage, and the words of the shorter one all lie inside the longer one. Small words such as "the" are not counted. A second, stricter number is printed beside the pass-mark number and carries no mark: agreement when the two labels must have the same words in the same order.
4. **Presence follows your ruling of 2026-10-05.** A Mind is any being that thinks. For every other role the labeler applies the Colloquialism Test of your file `docs/CAS_FOUNDATIONAL_CLARIFICATIONS.md` and writes the saying down. A pass shows that the two teams agree, not that the labels are correct. A failed mark is a failure.
