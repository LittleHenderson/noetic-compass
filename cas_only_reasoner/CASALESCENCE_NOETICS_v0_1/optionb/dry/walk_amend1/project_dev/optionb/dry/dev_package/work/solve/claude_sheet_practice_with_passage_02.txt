=== ITEM Q0085 ===
Passage:
Ansel opened the sail loft before dawn. He always reached for the curved needle before the straight needle. The pot stood on the trestle by the door. A bolt of canvas lay beside it. He kept well away from the blunt shears. Every morning for nine years he had dusted the pot first.

Question: Where did the pot stand?
A. on the trestle
B. under the bench
C. behind the gate
D. inside the cart

=== ITEM Q0086 ===
Passage:
Liesel opened the weigh house before dawn. She always reached for the small scale before the large scale. The pan stood on the sill by the door. A box of chalk lay beside it. She kept well away from the cracked weight. Every morning for nine years she had dusted the pan first.

Question: Liesel spends two years far from the weigh house. Going only by the passage, which of these has most likely faded when she comes back?
A. dusting the pan before anything else
B. seeing the chalk beside it
C. knowing where the pan stands
D. finding the sill by the door
E. opening the weigh house before dawn
F. walking in through the door
=== END Q0086 ===
