LABELER INSTRUCTIONS
This text goes with the labeling guide, version 2.2 (02_LABELING_GUIDE_v2_2.md).

YOUR TASK
You get the labeling guide and a passage with an id.
Label the passage exactly as Part B of the guide says.
Work only from the guide and from the passage in front of you. Use no outside knowledge.
Label each passage on its own. One passage must not change how you label another.
Never force a label. Do not turn a hint into an entry.

WHAT YOU RETURN
One JSON record for each passage. Each record is ONE JSON object.
Nothing else goes with it: no words before it, no words after it, no code fence.
The message that gives you the passage says how to hand the record in.

{"passage_id": "<the id you were given>",
 "none_of_these": false,
 "entries": [
  {"noetic": "^8", "label": "...", "support": "...", "mind": "...", "relative_to": "...", "certainty": "clear", "saying": "..."}
 ],
 "not_enough_information": []}

- "passage_id": the id of the passage, copied exactly.
- "none_of_these": true only when nothing in the passage holds any of "^1" to "^9". Then "entries" is [].
- "entries": one entry for each thing, in one role, for one Mind.
- "not_enough_information": a list of short notes. Use [] when there are none.

EVERY ENTRY
Write these seven fields in every entry: "noetic", "label", "support", "mind", "relative_to", "certainty", "saying".
A field that does not apply is "". Never leave one of the seven out.
- "noetic" is one of "^1" to "^9". Do not label Worlds, Domains, Drives or Registers.
- "certainty" is "clear" or "ambiguous".
- "saying" is the everyday saying that fits the moment (rule 5 of the guide), in your own words or a canonical one.
  It has 2 to 20 words in every "^2" to "^9" entry. It is "" in a "^1" entry, and only there.
- Add "other_candidate" only with "certainty": "ambiguous". Its value is another Noetic ("^n"), "none" or "reasoning".

EXACT COPIES
"label", "support", "mind" and "relative_to" are exact copies of passage text: the same letters, the same capitals, the same punctuation, one unbroken stretch. "saying" is your own line, not a copy.
- Do not correct the passage. Do not reword it. Do not join two stretches. Do not cut words out of the middle.
- "support" is whole sentences, each from its first word to its end mark. At most 4, next to each other.
- "label" lies inside "support". It has 1 to 12 words.
- In a "^1" entry, "support" is the sentence that holds the first place the name stands in the passage.
- "mind" is the "label" of your own "^1" entry for that Mind, character for character. It is "" in a "^6" entry, and only there.
- "relative_to" is the "label" of the related thing, or "" where the table of the guide says so.
- If the passage never names the related thing, leave "relative_to" as "" and add a note under "not_enough_information". For "^8" and "^9" see rule 2 of the guide.

THE CHECKER
A fixed program checks the form of your record before it is accepted.
It reads form only. A record that passes is not thereby correct.
If a check fails, you get the checker's messages. Then do this:
1. Read every message. Fix what the messages name. Use the guide to decide the fix.
2. Hand in the whole record again, in the same format, with every entry in it.
You have up to three tries for one passage.
Never remove an entry to make a message go away. Fix its form and keep it.
The one exception: a message that itself says to keep only one of two, or to take the entries out.
