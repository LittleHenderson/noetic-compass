LABELER INSTRUCTIONS
Fixed task text. It goes with the labeling guide, version 2.0 (02_LABELING_GUIDE_v2_0.md).

YOUR TASK
You are a careful labeler.
You get the labeling guide and a passage. The passage has an id.
Label the passage exactly as Part B of the guide says.
Work only from the guide and from the passage in front of you. Use no outside knowledge.
If you label more than one passage, label each one on its own. One passage must not change how you label another.
Never force a label.

WHAT YOU RETURN
One JSON record for each passage. Each record is ONE JSON object.
Nothing else goes with it: no words before it, no words after it, no code fence.
The message that gives you the passage says how to hand the record in. The record always has the format below.

The passage record has exactly these five fields:

{"passage_id": "<the id you were given>",
 "none_of_these": false,
 "entries": [
  {"noetic": "^8", "mind": "...", "label": "...", "support": "...", "measure": "", "relative_to": "...", "certainty": "clear"}
 ],
 "not_enough_information": [],
 "guide_problem": ""}

- "passage_id": the id of the passage, copied exactly.
- "none_of_these": true only when nothing in the passage holds any of "^1" to "^9". Then "entries" is [].
- "entries": one entry for each thing, in one role, for one Mind.
- "not_enough_information": a list of short notes for things the passage hints at but does not settle. Use [] when there are none.
- "guide_problem": one short sentence when a rule of the guide was unclear for this passage, or when two rules seemed to order opposite things. Otherwise "".
  One more case (rule 14): a saying of Part A pointed to a Noetic, and a numbered rule of Part B said to write no entry of it. Follow the numbered rule. Then write one sentence here that starts with the words "Saying against rule:".

EVERY ENTRY
Write these seven fields in every entry, in this order:
"noetic", "mind", "label", "support", "measure", "relative_to", "certainty".
A field that does not apply is "". Never leave one of the seven out.
- "noetic" is one of "^1", "^2", "^3", "^4", "^5", "^6", "^7", "^8", "^9".
- "certainty" is "clear", "ambiguous" or "held".

Add each of these five fields only where the guide says so. Otherwise leave it out.
- "other_candidate": only with "certainty": "ambiguous". Its value is another Noetic ("^n"), "none" or "reasoning".
- "held_under": only with "certainty": "held". Its value is one of the eight codes of rule 17.
- "reached_by": "reasoning", only on a "^9" under rule 15(b). That entry has "relative_to": "". It is not the partner of any "^8". It is never "held". It never has "other_candidate": "reasoning".
- "clue": true, only on a "^6" under rule 7. That entry has "relative_to": "" and "certainty": "clear".
- "no_fuller_name": true, only under rule 16, step 3.

EXACT COPIES
Every quoted field is an exact copy of passage text.
- "label", "support" and "measure" are copied from the passage: the same letters, the same capitals, the same punctuation, one unbroken stretch.
- Do not correct the passage. Do not reword it. Do not join two stretches. Do not cut words out of the middle.
- "support" is whole sentences, from the first word to the full stop. At most 4 sentences.
- "label" lies inside "support". It has 1 to 12 words.
- "measure" lies inside "support". It is filled only for "^4" and "^7".
- "mind" is the "label" of your own "^1" entry for that Mind, character for character. It is "" in every "^6" entry, and only there.
- "relative_to" for "^8" and "^9" is the "label" of the other entry of the pair, character for character.
- "relative_to" for "^6" is the whole, copied exactly from the passage. It is "" only with "clue": true. With "clue": true it is always "".
- "relative_to" for every other Noetic is "".
Never fill a quoted field with words of your own.

THE CHECKER
A fixed program checks your record before it is accepted.
It reads form only. It does not say whether any role is right or wrong. A record that passes is not thereby correct.
If a check fails, you get the checker's messages. Each message names an entry, a check (C1 to C9, or R0 for the record as a whole) and what is wrong.
Then do this:
1. Read every message.
2. Fix what the messages name. Use the guide to decide the fix.
3. Hand in the whole record again, in the same format. Keep every entry in it, not only the one you fixed.
You have up to three tries for one passage. After the third try the record is final.
An entry that failed a check does not vanish when you leave it out of your next try. The program puts it back and counts it as a dropped entry of yours. The one exception is a repeated entry that check C8 told you to remove.
Check C9 is a question, not a failure. It is asked once, for a label that names a feeling. Do what its message says.

WHAT YOU MUST NOT DO
- Do not write anything outside the JSON object.
- Do not label Worlds, Domains, Drives or Registers. Write "^1" to "^9" only.
- Do not leave out an entry that rule 17 holds for the owner. Write that entry in full and mark it "held" with its code.
- Do not write such an entry as "clear".
- Do not write the same thing twice, for the same Noetic and the same Mind, with two values of "certainty". One thing, one entry (rule 16, step 10).
- Do not turn a hint into an entry. A note under "not_enough_information", "certainty": "ambiguous" and "none_of_these": true are correct answers when the passage does not settle a thing.
