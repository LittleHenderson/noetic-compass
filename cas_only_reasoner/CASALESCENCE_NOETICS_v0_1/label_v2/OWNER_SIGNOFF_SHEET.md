For the owner. Every item is a proposal. None is in force, and no new passage is sealed, until you sign. "Sealed" means locked and fingerprinted before any labeling starts. A "bar" is a pass mark. 1. **Entry-agreement bar: 0.80.** Of every role either team wrote down, at least 8 in 10 must be written by both teams: same role, same Mind, same place in the passage.
2. **Presence bar: kappa 0.60.** Kappa scores how well the teams agree that a role is in a passage at all, beyond what luck would give. 0 is luck. 1 is full agreement.
3. **Minimum counts: 50 and 50.** No pass or fail for a Noetic without at least 50 passages where a team found the role, and at least 50 where neither team did.
4. **Eight held kinds.** Each waits on a question that is yours. Each is still written down and counted in its own row. Each is kept out of the pass/fail row until you rule. If you rule before the seal, the kind enters this verdict as clear, or as nothing. If you rule after the seal, the kind is only re-counted for reading. It gets a pass or fail only if you rule before the seal, or in a later attempt on new passages.
   - 4a. H2-COMFORT (`^2`): a thing only gives a good feeling. "Its simple presence offered a small sense of comfort."
   - 4b. H3-NOPULL (`^3`): a thing only fails to draw the person. It "held little appeal".
   - 4c. H3-BORED (`^3`): a thing only bores the person. She found it "tedious".
   - 4d. H5-ROLE (`^5`): a role with a word like seasoned or veteran, and nothing said of what was lived. "the seasoned barber".
   - 4e. H7-RESULT (`^7`): acting without thinking, with no "how often". "Without thinking, he adjusted the ear defenders over his ears."
   - 4f. H7-NOEFFECT (`^7`): a "how often", with nothing said of what it did to the person. "Finally, she misted it daily."
   - 4g. H7-UNBROKEN (`^7`): exposure with no break and no repeats. "The constant hum of the industrial dryers".
   - 4h. H89-FOLLOWS (`^8`, `^9`): a memory just follows something noticed, with no linking word. "Thomas observed the young skier struggling with the boarding gate. He remembered his own clumsy attempts decades ago."

7. **Nine pointing conventions.** They say how the short label is picked. They are conventions 1 to 9 at the end of guide rule 16, in the same order. None is canon. You may reverse any.
   - 7a. The label is the naming in the sentence that holds the evidence. Go back to an earlier naming only when that naming is a pointer such as "This smell". (Other choice: the first naming in the passage.)
   - 7b. For a pull, a push or an impression, label what the passage says it is aimed at: "its mechanical might", not "the heavy excavator". (Other choice: always the thing.)
   - 7c. For `^5`, label the experience ("daily milking"), not the words that say it formed the person.
   - 7d. For `^6`, the whole is the nearest naming before the parts ("The recording"). A naming followed by a doing word is skipped.
   - 7e. A clue on the way to a reasoned conclusion is written with no whole, even when the passage names the whole. Clues are counted apart, with no pass mark. (Other choice: a clue keeps the whole the passage names and counts as a plain `^6`.)
   - 7f. In "every time X, the person does Y", the `^7` label is Y. X stays in the how-often words. (Other choice: label X.)
   - 7g. The program, not the labeler, adds the `^5` that goes with a clear `^7`.
   - 7h. Several noticed things that bring one thing to Mind are written as one pair for each noticed thing.
   - 7i. Four word lists are this study's working tests. Each list gives examples. The guide has the exact words. What a test leaves out gets no clear entry.
       - How-often words (`^7`, guide rule 6a). They pass: every morning, each Tuesday and Friday, every day, daily, nightly, weekly, twice a week, every time, whenever, always, often, regularly, day in and day out, again and again, over and over; countless, hundreds of times, thousands of times, time after time; a length of time with "of" and a thing that comes round again ("years of early mornings"). Left out: a length of time alone, or with a job, a place or an activity ("thirty years on the wards", "years of loading and unloading"); a number of years or winters; practising with no how-often word; "always made her wince". See 9d, 9e and 9f.
       - Bringing words (`^8`, `^9`, guide rule 2): reminded, brought back, made her think of, came to him, was suddenly in, took her back, transported, pulled her thoughts to, put her back in, brought to his thoughts, shifted her thoughts to, flooded his thoughts. Also a memory that arrives with instantly, suddenly, at once, immediately or "for a moment she was". Left out: "remembered", "recalled" or "pictured" alone. That is held under 4h, or gets no pair.
       - How-much words (`^4`, guide rule 10f): strong, powerful, deep, deeply, intense, intensely, keen, keenly, great, profound, overwhelming, sharp; mild, slight, slightly, faint, faintly, small, little, barely, hardly; distinct, undeniable, unmistakable. Left out: what kind of impression it was ("comforting", "jarring"); a flinch with no size word; how faint or loud the thing itself is. See 9i.
       - Experience words (held kind 4d): seasoned, veteran, experienced, long-time, lifelong, master, expert, old hand. Left out: a plain job title. See 9c.
