# CLAIM BOUNDARY

## This study does NOT attempt to

- recover the Transformer's exact weights;
- prove CAS uniquely describes Transformer representations;
- prove the 40-simplex contains all model knowledge;
- prove a global neural section-retraction;
- prove physical Ohm's law inside a model;
- prove CAS-Ohm measures attention;
- create or train a general LLM from scratch;
- establish consciousness, intelligence, safety, or production
  readiness;
- let the CNCM or any language model change protected project authority.

## The four claims stay separate

Passing an earlier claim does not imply a later one.

- CLAIM 1 READOUT: the CAS probe generalizes on a held-out split.
- CLAIM 2 REALIZATION: a computed intervention moves the requested CAS
  coordinate AND improves exact role-binding behavior.
- CLAIM 3 DOSE: the proportional CAS-Ohm model predicts held-out movement
  within tolerance, beats zero/mean, and is within 5% MAE of
  affine/quadratic (it is NOT tested against zero-intercept linear, which
  is the same model).
- CLAIM 4A SAMPLE EFFICIENCY: fewer examples-to-threshold.
- CLAIM 4B OPTIMIZER EFFICIENCY: fewer optimizer steps-to-threshold.
- CLAIM 4C RETENTION: less loss of previously learned skills.
- CLAIM 4D WALL-CLOCK EFFICIENCY: less total time incl. overhead.
No "or" joins 4A-4D; each has its own status and evidence.

## Claim vocabulary

Permitted status words:
`PROPOSED`, `SOFTWARE_VERIFIED`, `EMPIRICALLY_SUPPORTED`, `REFUTED`,
`INCONCLUSIVE`, `REPLICATION_OPEN`.

Forbidden words (unless the exact claim, evidence tier, and scope are in
the same sentence): `CONFIRMED`, `PROVEN`, `VALIDATED`, `DISCOVERED`,
`FAST_LEARNING`.

## Negative-result law

A failed hypothesis is a completed result. Do not rescue it with another
architecture, taxonomy, model, metric, phase, or post-hoc threshold
change. Record the failure and stop.

## Internal-review law

Claude, subagents, and same-provider model votes are internal review
only. They are not independent replication. External status stays
`REPLICATION_OPEN` until an outside party reruns the bundle.

## Role of the implementer

Implementation researcher under a frozen protocol. May implement, test,
measure, report. May NOT change the research question, swap in an easier
target, invent a new architecture, add a product phase, or turn a
negative result into a new success story.
