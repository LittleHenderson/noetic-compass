# CNCM-RRK v0.1 Non-Goals

Status: **required Phase 0 bounded-scope contract candidate — Phase 0 OPEN**

This is the `spec/` artifact required by `cas_reasoner.txt:12570-12580`. It
restates the bounded exclusions in `NON_GOALS.md` under the current ontology,
compiler/runtime, KIR, and conformance contracts. It does not describe a
complete CAS Native Cognitive Machine and does not declare Phase 0 closed.

## 1. Language, media, and source compilation

The following are outside v0.1:

- raw or general-English understanding and free-form English generation; the
  frozen controlled-English facade remains limited to its documented facts,
  rules, questions, pronouns, and clarification form;
- any LLM or neural model inside reasoning, comparison, commitment, or learning;
- images, audio, sensors, embeddings, or multimodal encoders;
- a full CCDL parser, compiler, virtual machine, or complete formal semantics;
- arbitrary prompts, CAS diagrams, model output, or external ontologies as
  executable runtime input.

## 2. Ontology and reasoning semantics

The following are outside v0.1:

- Noetics above `^9`, ordinals, quantum forms, OOP extensions, or other extended
  TKS language features;
- treating `^8` association or a caller-declared candidate as deduction;
  admitted rule conclusions require exact proof reconstruction, while the
  bounded high-level driver records `^6` decomposition;
- inventing meanings for the seven TKS Foundations, 28 TKS Sub-Foundations,
  their adapter to CAS Drive rails, or D/W/P sufficiency;
- treating CAS Drive rails `F1`-`F7` as identical to TKS Foundations
  `TF1`-`TF7`;
- register transfer semantics, substantive domain-stability criteria, or
  executable meanings for `^0`-`^5` and `^7` while their entries remain
  `OPEN-SEMANTICS`;
- CAS-Ohm as a truth, meaning, proof, validity, commitment, permission,
  memory-occurrence/write, state-mutation, halt, causal-identification, action,
  or self-modification authority; as a physical law or identifying causal
  diagnostic; or as a scheduler/attention input in runtime 0.1.0. The adopted
  follow-on attention protocol remains contract-only and requires a separate
  pre-schedule plan plus a later authority/KIR version before native flow or
  resource allocation can execute;
- floating-point activation scores as truth or proof;
- concurrent or nondeterministic scheduling.

An open semantic item is not an invitation to choose an implementation-local
meaning. It MUST remain carried opaquely, rejected, or explicitly abstained from
as required by its current contract.

## 3. Learning, mutation, and action

The following are outside v0.1:

- online semantic, procedural, architectural, kernel, or constitutional
  learning;
- recursive source-code rewriting, descendant generation, autonomous
  architectural promotion, or live self-modification;
- kernel-initiated network access, external tools, file-system actions,
  subprocesses, robots, communications, transactions, or any other external
  side effect;
- autonomous long-term goals, ambition, identity, autobiographical continuity,
  or unrestricted agency.

`EXTERNAL_ACTION` and `PROTECTED_PATCH` remain typed only so attempts can be
rejected and traced. They have no apply semantics. Explicit caller-requested CLI
file I/O remains a host-adapter operation outside the kernel intent graph.

## 4. Evidence and claim limits

The following claims are outside v0.1:

- presenting natural-language diagnostics or explanations as proof; the
  machine-readable trace and derivation record are normative;
- consciousness, sentience, human-like understanding, general intelligence,
  originality, or broad causal understanding;
- formal verification, empirical neural validation, security, safety,
  scalability, reliability, production readiness, or regulatory compliance;
- treating a passing local suite, retained fixture, or golden as evidence beyond
  the exact behavior it covers.

Passing the conformance plan proves only agreement with its frozen schemas,
fixtures, and assertions in the tested environment. It does not resolve an
ontology question or authenticate external facts, rules, issuers, or durable
memory.

## 5. Foundry, qualification, and integration exclusions

The CAS Foundry/Cognitive Board Studio/CADA proposal is outside this runtime.
The build is not a browser canvas, node editor, Board renderer, collaboration
service, registry, publishing workflow, account system, or Foundry workbench.
A separately frozen Board Ontology and Capability Specification is required
before that scope can cross the boundary.

The proposed CAS Reasoning Qualification Standard is also outside v0.1. No
`D0`-`D10` Task Difficulty Grade, `CAS-G` generation, Capability Vector (`CV`),
`E-Tier`, `A-Level`, Reference Model Benchmark (`RMB`), qualification bench,
failure-localization evaluator, repair engine, protected or held-out suite,
causal-ablation harness, or anti-gaming system is implemented.

Current unit, authority, integrity, CLI, replay, fixture, evidence, or golden
results are engineering-conformance evidence only. They earn no CRQS grade or
tier. Capability, evidence maturity, deployment authority, and mutation
permission remain separate contracts: none may be inferred from topology, a
passing suite, another category, or a repair recommendation.

No current Board, Foundry, CRQS, external ontology, or protected sibling object
is a `ProblemPacket`, inference rule, proof, intent, or authority grant.

## 6. Non-goals do not weaken fail-closed protections

Calling a feature a non-goal does not authorize silent acceptance. Where the KIR
or boundary provides a typed rejection path, the implementation MUST preserve
it. Unsupported or open constructs MUST NOT expand the issuer/capability matrix,
write protected state, fabricate proof, trigger learning promotion, or create an
external effect.

## 7. Permitted bounded claim

When every current check actually passes, the strongest claim permitted by
`spec/CONFORMANCE_PLAN_v0.1.md` is that the CNCM-RRK v0.1 deterministic
reference demonstrator passed its retained unit, authority, integrity, CLI,
fixture, and golden checks in that environment. No stronger completeness,
intelligence, qualification, evidence-maturity, deployment-authority,
model-equivalence, safety, or production claim follows.
