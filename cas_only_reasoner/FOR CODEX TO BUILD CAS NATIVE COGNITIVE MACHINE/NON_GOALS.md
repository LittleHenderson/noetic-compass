# CNCM-RRK v0.1 Non-Goals

This contract describes a bounded deterministic reference kernel. It does not describe a complete CAS Native Cognitive Machine.

The following are explicitly outside v0.1:

- Raw or general-English understanding and free-form English generation. The separate frozen controlled-English facade is intentionally limited to explicit facts, rules, questions, pronouns, and clarification.
- Any LLM or neural model inside the reasoning, comparison, commitment, or learning path.
- Images, audio, sensors, embeddings, or multimodal encoders.
- Noetics above `^9`, ordinals, quantum forms, OOP extensions, or other extended TKS language features.
- A full CCDL parser, compiler, virtual machine, or complete formal semantics.
- Treating `^8` association or a caller-declared candidate as deduction. Admitted rule conclusions require exact proof reconstruction; the high-level driver also records `^6` decomposition.
- Inventing meanings for the seven TKS Foundations, 28 TKS Sub-Foundations, their adapter to CAS Drive rails, or D/W/P sufficiency. These remain `OPEN-SEMANTICS`.
- Treating CAS Drive rails `F1`-`F7` as identical to TKS Foundations `TF1`-`TF7`.
- CAS-Ohm as a truth, meaning, proof, validity, commitment, permission,
  memory-occurrence/write, state-mutation, halt, causal-identification, action,
  or self-modification authority; as a physical law or identifying causal
  diagnostic; or as a scheduler/attention input in runtime 0.1.0. The adopted
  follow-on protocol may admit typed native flow and bounded resource allocation
  only through a separate pre-schedule plan, a later authority/KIR version, and
  its required determinism, fairness, non-bypass, and negative tests.
- Floating-point activation scores as truth or proof.
- Concurrent or nondeterministic scheduling.
- Online semantic, procedural, architectural, kernel, or constitutional learning.
- Recursive source-code rewriting, descendant generation, autonomous architectural promotion, or live self-modification.
- Kernel-initiated network access, external tools, file-system actions, subprocess execution, robots, communications, transactions, or any other external side effect. The explicit CLI host adapter may read and write only the paths requested by its caller.
- Autonomous long-term goals, ambition, identity, autobiographical continuity, or unrestricted agency.
- Natural-language diagnostics or explanations presented as proof. The normative evidence is the machine-readable trace and derivation record.
- Claims of consciousness, sentience, human-like understanding, general intelligence, originality, or broad causal understanding.
- Claims of formal verification, empirical neural validation, security, safety, scalability, reliability, production readiness, or regulatory compliance.
- The appended CAS Foundry/Cognitive Board Studio/CADA proposal: the hosted playground is not a browser canvas, node editor, board renderer, collaboration service, registry, publishing workflow, account system, or Foundry workbench. That follow-on scope requires a frozen Board Ontology/Capability Specification first.
- The appended CAS Reasoning Qualification Standard (CRQS v0.1) proposal at source lines 10031-11171: no `D0`-`D10` Task Difficulty Grade, `CAS-G` generation, Capability Vector (`CV`), `E-Tier`, `A-Level`, Reference Model Benchmark (`RMB`), qualification bench, failure-localization evaluator, repair engine, protected/held-out suite, causal-ablation harness, or anti-gaming system is implemented.
- Treating the current fixture, unit, authority, integrity, CLI, replay, or golden results as CRQS qualification. They are engineering-conformance evidence for the bounded v0.1 contract only; they earn no CRQS grade or tier.
- Inferring capability from topology, evidence maturity from a passing local suite, deployment authority from either capability or evidence, or permission to patch from a repair recommendation. Capability, evidence, authority, and mutation permission require separate, versioned contracts.

Passing the conformance plan proves only the behavior covered by its frozen schemas, fixtures, and assertions.
