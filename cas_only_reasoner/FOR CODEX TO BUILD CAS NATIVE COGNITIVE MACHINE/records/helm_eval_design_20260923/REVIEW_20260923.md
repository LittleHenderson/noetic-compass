# Review of evaluation design v0.1 and wiring probe v0.1 (2026-09-23)

**Who reviewed.** Three read-only adversarial reviewers (Claude subagents), workflow `wf_6950946c-6bd`. Each
had one lens:
- **shortcut:** shortcuts and leaks;
- **probe:** whether the probe measures the relevant thing;
- **stats:** whether the design can be built, and whether its statistics hold.

**Rules they worked under.** No file edits. No model run. No sealed file opened.

**Where the evidence is.**
- Full findings: `review_20260923/review_findings.json`.
- Their scratch scripts: `review_20260923/scratch_*`.

**What their verdict means.** They found that **v0.1 would certify the wrong thing in several places**. No
model had been run on v0.1 and no probe reading existed, so every fix below lands before any result.

| Finding (lens id) | Severity | What it showed | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1, stats F2 | BLOCKER | At 3-4 nodes the degree-multiset rule is almost exact for analogy and correspondence. It collides once among 28 shapes, and degree-preserving maps are valid isomorphisms for 20/22 new shapes. So the bar is about 1.0, no model can "beat" it, and a win would not show structure reading | **Accepted.** At 3 and 4 nodes, analogy and correspondence are diagnostic only (they can at most "match the degree rule"). Structure claims for these families move to at least 5 nodes, where 105/267 classes collide in degree multiset. That needs a new checker version (next row) |
| S6, stats F1 | BLOCKER | The frozen checker refuses isomorphism above 4 nodes (`MAX_ISOMORPHISM_NODES = 4`). One 5-node world in a bank breaks every analogy on that bank. 80 partner worlds exceed the 64-world bank | **Accepted.** At 5 nodes the design keeps reach, parts and edge_account only. 5-node analogy and correspondence need a **new, separately named checker version**. That is an owner decision, and it must reproduce the frozen checker bit-for-bit on every record of 4 nodes or fewer |
| S2, stats F6 | BLOCKER | A VIA-only reach cell is passed by label-order rules: the lowest-rank child scores 348/384 = 0.906; a two-hop rule scores 0.969; "participant 2" is the VIA answer in 41 of 56 bank VIA questions | **Accepted.** Reach is scored as a balanced ABSENT/DIRECT/VIA cell, with VIA as a stratum only. New comparators are registered: lowest-rank child, two-hop, participant-2. Ranks and participants are drawn independently of structure, and each flag rule's hit rate is reported per cell |
| S3, stats F5 | BLOCKER | The 3-4-node test shapes cannot be secret: all 22 can be listed | **Accepted.** At 3-4 nodes, commitment replaces secrecy. The builder submits a manifest of every world any update or development evaluation touched; it is checked for isomorphism at evaluation and fails closed. At 5 nodes the test pool is secret AND the manifest is checked. Training on any new shape outside `DEV_NEW` is banned by rule |
| S4, probe F1, stats F8 | BLOCKER | The probe passes a repair that shifts every candidate score equally. Measured on synthetic scores: PRESENT with 0 of 248 order changes | **Accepted.** Probe v0.2 standardises the scores over the cross-domain candidates, and requires a ranking change (Kendall) and a move toward the new accepted set (RC) |
| probe F2 | BLOCKER | "Switch OFF reproduces the before-reading" tests nothing, because zero metrics are zero for any focus-blind model | **Accepted.** New `preservation_digest.py` fingerprints the bits of every slot's logits on all 564 records. Switch OFF must match `20208c92` bit for bit |
| S5, stats F3/F4 | MAJOR/BLOCKER | Only 14 structurally distinct VIA questions exist in the 22 new small shapes. "80 items" were about 7 clusters. A5's power used the wrong test | **Accepted.** The unit of analysis is the structural question cluster (shape x whole x part, up to automorphism). A cluster-level sign test is used, and effective n is printed per cell. A cell with fewer than 10 clusters cannot support a claim. Power is redone per cell against its measured bar |
| probe F3 | MAJOR | A global coupling across worlds, or GPU noise, could pass | **Accepted.** v0.2 adds the M0 noise gate (every forward run twice) and the M4 bystander control, with a `COUPLED_NOT_COMPARISON` verdict |
| probe F4 | MAJOR | Rewiring `reading["worlds"]` left the second copy `reading["focus_world"]` stale | **Accepted.** v0.2 builds every counterfactual at the packet level and re-reads it |
| probe F5, stats F9 | MAJOR | PRESENT on fresh containers only; a zero-initialised gate reads ABSENT, and a trained model could close the path | **Accepted.** PRESENT is required on every trained switch-ON checkpoint that is read. Fresh zero-gated containers use a seeded probe draw, labelled `REACHABILITY` |
| probe F6 | MAJOR | NO_MATCH is a real option; the probe ignored it | **Accepted.** M5 records the NO_MATCH margin change, split by whether the new accepted set is empty or not |
| probe F7 | MAJOR | The focus-domain path was never probed | **Accepted.** M6 is a domain counterfactual, and it is needed for `PRESENT_GRAPH_AND_DOMAIN` |
| S7, stats F6 | MAJOR | Participant and whole marks solve the bank's correspondence (640/640) | **Accepted.** Participants are drawn at random; the whole-first rule is the only fixed part. A flag-matching comparator is registered |
| S8 | MAJOR | There was no focus-blind comparator; the unrepaired arm might score without comparing anything | **Accepted.** Two comparators are registered: the best fixed candidate ranking, and a candidate-only novelty rule. Each item's bank has at least k same-size, same-edge-count, non-partner, non-training-shape decoys, proved at build |
| stats F7, S9 | MAJOR | Fresh declaration words go through untrained byte tokens and change table rows | **Accepted.** The four training domain strings, and the trained settings, kinds and functions, are kept. "Fresh" now means only relabelling nodes, which changes their rank order, drawn at random independently of structure |
| stats F10, S10 | MAJOR/MINOR | The Q4 familiar pool changes with each arm's teaching rows, and at N = 2048 it is empty. The dev split is builder-visible | **Accepted.** A fixed, digest-listed familiar pool, stratified by bare shape, is reserved and excluded from every arm's teaching rows. The vacuous string rule is replaced by a leaf-sequence rule. Dev = `DEVELOPMENT_FEEDBACK_NOT_CLAIM_BEARING`; transfer claims need the sealed eval |
| stats F11 | MINOR | Part C could not be checked as written | **Accepted.** Full query id, named checkpoint, determinism settings and a tolerance are now given |
| probe F8/F9/F10, stats F12 | MINOR | The alternative list, the share denominator, switch verification, clarity | **Accepted.** The alternatives are frozen; shares are counted over the 24 foci; a switch attribute is required; the A5 name clash is fixed; comparators must also be right in every presentation; NO_MATCH negatives have equal node and edge counts |
