1. DOES THE PACKET REACH THE PER-SLOT DECISION? � the round-29 test, on each binding
========================================================================================================

For each slot: sign(d . encoded(packet, slot)) against sign(d . slot_vector) � that is,
the frozen readout's decision WITH the packet against the same decision WITHOUT it.
The column is the fraction of slots where the two AGREE.

           kind   slots    additive    bilinear   bilinear x4
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
        context     267   100.0000%    98.1273%      90.2622%
     definition      70   100.0000%   100.0000%      87.1429%
   edge_account    1029   100.0000%    96.9874%      88.0466%
          parts     207    97.1014%    94.2029%      84.5411%
       role_set     216    94.4444%    93.5185%      83.3333%

100.0000% is the additive binding's signature: the packet moves every slot the same way,
so it can never change one decision relative to another. Round 29 measured exactly that
at 100.0000% for edge_account and context. A rate below 100% means the packet now enters
the per-slot decision, which is what the neurons need in order to learn from it.

NOT MEASURED: any trained result. This is the encoder's geometry, which is deterministic,
