# Conference questions prepared before first reports

This is a neutral response rubric, not a preselected explanation or an executed conference.

Each A1-A6 investigator will, after the first-report barrier, read the peer reports and identify
at least one specific competing explanation within their field. They must state whether existing
evidence establishes, weakens or leaves it unresolved and cite an original record or source.

The conference must keep these distinctions explicit:

- The original pass-rule failure versus useful-behavior changes.
- Improvement from random initialization versus improvement over a simple algorithm.
- What an architecture can represent versus what its trained parameters did.
- Numerical scores versus valid decoded answers.
- Renamed worlds versus new graph structures.
- A defect in an old or newer module versus a defect in the actual historical source.
- Association versus a comparison that isolates cause.

No vote decides the result. Agents reusing one record or checker are not independent experiments.
A0 selects only follow-ups that can alter a conclusion or next-action priority, registers predicted
outcomes first and limits follow-ups to two rounds. A7 will inspect decisive original records.
