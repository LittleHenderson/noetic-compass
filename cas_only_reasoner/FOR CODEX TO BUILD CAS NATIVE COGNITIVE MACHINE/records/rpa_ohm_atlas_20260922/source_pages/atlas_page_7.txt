The 10 Noetics × 4 Processing Domains supply 40 coordinate labels. Their simplex has 39 independent coordinates because of the sum-to-one
constraint. [C1, §2.2–2.3; C5, Definition 8.1]
A profile p describes a modeled mixture over these labels. It is not automatically a probability distribution over
future events. A 60% activation or composition weight does not mean an outcome has a 60% chance of occurring.
Nor does p encode an entire reality. Two different projects can have the same CAS profile. Store the actual idea
content, context, resources, observations and time in the node record; the 40-component profile is a descriptor
attached to it.
Do not collapse the namespaces
Noetic = operation label. Domain = processing level. Drive = local motive. Register = mode of operation.
Foundation = goal class. None is interchangeable with a petal count or a geometric axis. [C1; C3]
Registers retain the source spelling: |T Transcendent, |A Analytical, |f Affective and |E Embodied. The |E register is not the E Evaluative
Domain. [C1, §2.5]
