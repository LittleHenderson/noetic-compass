Left: a three-group illustration with composition (10%, 30%, 60%). Right: a relative coordinate view centered on that state. The triangle is a toy

The cited Noetic Topos defines 40 positive coordinates whose sum is one, giving the open simplex of dimension 39.
Its geometric layer is separate from the site/topos construction. [C5, §8 and Remark 8.6]
Keep that profile unchanged. To make the present p* the origin, use relative log-ratio coordinates for i = 1,…,39:
[M3; proposed centering]
zᵢ(p; p*) = ln(pᵢ / p₄₀) − ln(p*ᵢ / p*₄₀)
At p = p*, every zᵢ is zero. A different reference shifts the coordinates but does not change the underlying state
or its history.
This coordinate map reaches an unbounded real space while the simplex remains bounded. Circles drawn in that coordinate view
generally distort when mapped back. ALR is not distance-preserving; it must not silently replace the source’s Fisher–Rao metric. Exact
zeros require a separate boundary policy. [M3, M4; C5, §8]
