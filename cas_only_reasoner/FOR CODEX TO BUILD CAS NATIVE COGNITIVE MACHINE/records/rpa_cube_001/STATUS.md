## Actual behavior

links: one loaded snapshot, six cube records, 36 faces and five source containment
branches. Three presentation views preserve canonical identities. Source records,
their provenance, context, mapping coverage, availability and revision remain

Current source, inspection focus and a separately bound hypothetical graph remain
distinct. Selecting a face or hypothesis does not advance the native runtime,
alter the source, or expand the declared learner input. REACHED_VIA is a query
over structural containment; it does not become a causal or temporal action.

The bridge invokes the existing pure teacher compiler against a complete authored
graph, then exports only `record_id`, `public` and `encoded_options` to the
learner boundary. Teacher answers, option indices and structural witnesses stay
in a separate sidecar. Public input identities bind exact public bytes, ordered
choices, source revision and supplied encoding identities. No learner is invoked.

The new offline inspector shows actual source faces and separate learner/teacher
panels. It reloads a typed document against original source receipts and fully
recompiles a supplied lesson before showing it as a graph-derived reference.
The resulting HTML is a display copy; editing it does not produce source evidence.

## Preserved review findings and limits

Development evidence preserves the original failures and their fixes: private
answer metadata entering source identity, lossy legacy option encoding, foreign
partial-face references, silently widened partial views, JSON scalar coercion,
paging scalar aliases, and teacher records joined to the wrong learner or option.
The legacy target-free compiler KeyError and earlier negative recurrence results
remain unchanged. No historical neural evidence is relabeled as successful.

Source hashes establish byte identity relative to supplied pins. They do not
authenticate authorship, source timestamps or world truth. Cursor hashes bind
content and ranges, not a history of having visited earlier pages.
`check_teacher_target` deliberately reports local consistency only; a coherent
invented reference can satisfy that check. A source-grounded comparison must
recompile against externally pinned authored bytes, as the inspector does.

Only complete containment presentations and three bounded query operations are
supported. Incomplete inventory cannot justify absence. Why remains a structural
teacher witness; How needs a separately defined action contract. Model and encoder
identities are supplied metadata; no cache capability is demonstrated.

This checkpoint provides faithful projection, inspection and lesson preparation.
It does not demonstrate improved reasoning, neural learning, causal execution,
general procedure construction or the complete cognitive machine. The full
V01–V18, 31-system and integrated 20-step charter remains open. No paid compute,
AWS launch or local neural execution was used for this checkpoint.
