## What the content contains

Five declared dimensions rotate through existing slots: a formal completeness
statement; an authored placement account derived from explicit public graph
lines; a reviewer's stated purpose, which adds no containment fact; a partial
folder correspondence with exact directed connections; and explicitly irrelevant
time-of-day wording. The last is a context/surface variant, not an additional
experience. Records call all examples AUTHORED; text does not establish physical
sensing, embodiment or emotion.

Only supported explicit public graph lines generate relation statements. Private
correct answers never select the dimension, public ID, order, masks or text.
Role-recognition excerpts without graph lines remain intact, and slots 64–71
retain exact maintenance input. No corrective or mapping metadata is silently
presented as unassisted prediction.

The golden Kit/Toolbox family is copied from the user source, compiled into the
real three-choice codec and checked for complete, injective, directed mappings.
The context/analogy variants preserve semantic answers after relabeling. Moving
Wrench from Toolbox to Kit changes both its route and Kit's direct inventory.
Incomplete graphs fail instead of treating unknown relations as absent.

## Measurement boundary

The selected student runs the standalone registered _DecomposeTransform. That
operation emits transformed vectors; it does not expose the CASformer routing
current/total-current/normalized-allocation producer. A native read emits null
values and UNAVAILABLE_NO_NATIVE_FLOW_TAP with the checkpoint and depth bound.
Loss, correctness, confidence and vector norms never supply a substitute.

The current student also has no approved intervention-dose port. Optional sweep
requests first require authorization and complete profile identities, then fail
with UNAVAILABLE_NO_APPROVED_INTERVENTION_PORT. This is a concrete capability
gap, not a simulated neural adapter. No calibration forward calls are included
in the costed request.

Pure fitting of retained rows is implemented: disjoint calibration, holdout and
final families; identical model, tokenizer, input, test point, direction, port,
units and depth; fixed dose grid; positive-slope and held-out error checks;
root-family bootstrap uncertainty; and zero, constant, affine and quadratic
diagnostics. This is ordinary regression. Negative, uncertain or poorly fitting
slopes leave r_eff null. Simulated row provenance is retained and instrumentation
is explicitly unverified. These calculations do not establish physical Ohm's law,
attention, native route resistance, correctness or a universal frequency.

Prediction records are sealed before the teacher join. Reports retain the full
assigned denominator, including missing predictions, and compare semantic answers
rather than option slots. They separate invariant agreement from both-correct
invariance and both-correct changed-relation discrimination. Root-family paired
effect intervals are descriptive; small sample warnings remain explicit.
Native flow, empirical response, competence, exposure rhythm and costs occupy
separate fields. There is no scalar resonance score.

The real worker adds 90 canonical, 90 cross-view and 30 changed-route final tasks
on the parent's 30 fresh root families. These are derived views, not 210 new
families. Both conditions receive the same panels, and the earlier-skill panel
remains separate. Predictions must reconstruct the parent's original pre-target
seal before perspective metrics or per-root IdeaResponseProfile records are
written. Every new artifact is added to the collection manifest and result
sidecar; a parent budget/error failure remains the primary exception.

The observer can emit shadow recommendations from development or runtime
qualification only. Final audit outcomes cannot choose teaching. Direct control,
automatic depth increase, batch changes and adaptive policy remain disabled.
