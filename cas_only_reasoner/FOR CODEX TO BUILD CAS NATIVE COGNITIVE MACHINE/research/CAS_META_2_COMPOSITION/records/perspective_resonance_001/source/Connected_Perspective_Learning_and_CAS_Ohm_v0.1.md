## 4. Define the signature as a response profile, not one magic score

An idea has an authored identity and supported relationships. A model's response to it additionally depends on model weights, input adapter, question, context, assistance, compute depth, and measurement procedure.

Maintain three separate objects:

1. **Concept identity/structure:** what relationship the sources declare, with scope and version.
2. **Response fingerprint:** the learner's measured activation/flow behavior under declared presentations and interventions.
3. **Competence profile:** independent recognition/application, near-miss discrimination, cross-perspective transfer, and retention.

The display alias may be `vibratory_signature`; the canonical data object is `IdeaResponseProfile`. Never use it as a universal concept identifier. Do not require matching hidden vectors across checkpoints or force every face into the same embedding. If a comparison crosses model versions, use common behavioral tasks and separately pinned measurement profiles; raw coordinates do not automatically align.

There is no default scalar `resonance_score`. Larger current, greater confidence, lower entropy, higher pairwise cosine, or a stable argmax cannot by themselves mark an idea learned. Two presentations can produce the same wrong answer. A valid signature should retain both response strength and the ability to tell different relationships apart.

^7 exposure rhythm is recorded as presentations, relative ordering, intervening tasks, and any real elapsed interval. It is not ^4 impression, recurrent pass count, graph depth, or oscillator frequency. Revisiting after intervening training tests interference retention; a process restart alone tests persistence, not biological consolidation or forgetting over time.

## 9. CAS-Ohm integration B: optional calibrated response

Implement compatibility with a separately authorized calibrated profile, disabled by default. The empirical CAS-Ohm paper's quantities are not interchangeable with native routing scores:

```
a       = declared intervention dose at an actual supported port
Delta y = signed activation displacement at a pinned test point/direction
r_eff   = fitted local alpha-per-activation response parameter
```

A proposed directional observation is:

```
Delta y_v(a) = dot(u, z_theta(x_v; a) - z_theta(x_v; 0))
Predicted Delta y_v(a) = a / r_eff,v
```

The baseline subtraction and chosen test point/direction are an explicit NEW measurement profile. The zero-dose condition removes the added intervention, not the graph's legitimate evidence. Keep every case's question, evidence, choices, model and recurrent depth fixed within its sweep.

`u` has frozen source, dimension, norm and sign and is selected using permitted calibration material, never final-test outcomes. A positive projection is not automatically a correct semantic answer. Intervene at an existing permitted port when possible. If there is no appropriate port, the required new steering adapter is a separate declared experiment change; the observer must not fabricate one.

Fit on calibration root families and evaluate on disjoint calibration-holdout families. Keep both distinct from final transfer/retention audits. Freeze dose grid, domain, fitting/uncertainty method, adequacy tolerances and zero/random same-norm controls before a neural sweep. No same-sample `r=a/y` followed by `y=a/r` may count as validation.

For a positive local slope g, `r_eff=1/g` is one possible declared parameterization. A near-zero, negative, sign-uncertain or poorly fitting slope does not license a positive resistance by taking an absolute value. Retain raw response and explicit fit failure. Compare the proposed proportional fit with zero/constant baselines and affine/nonlinear alternatives; meaningful nonlinearity makes a single-resistance law inadequate over that range, not evidence of hidden opposition.

The reciprocal-slope model is algebraically ordinary through-origin linear regression. Do not list a mathematically identical linear model as an independent opponent or claim a new physical law from a good fit.

No fit transfers across weights, view domains, interventions, readouts, runtimes or depths without a specified comparison/validity rule. Pre/post learning comparisons need separately valid fits and comparable coordinates. Keep any estimated shift in effective response separate from improved accuracy.

Calibration calls, counterfactual interventions, zero-dose comparisons and held-out sweeps are real work. They belong in an explicit future execution allocation. Implement the adapter now, but do not automatically run them or silently expand another job.

## 11. Behavioral meaning of 'the idea sticks'

Report a profile, not one scalar:

1. **Cross-view application:** correct answers when the relationship appears in a reserved presentation, with identity mappings applied to the answers.
2. **Invariant-pair correctness:** both predictions are correct on a declared meaning-preserving contrast. Agreement alone is also reported but is not success.
3. **Changed-relation discrimination:** both predictions are correct on a pair whose relationship legitimately changes the expected answer. A mere argmax flip is insufficient.
4. **Component and whole competence:** separately measured so improved local decisions cannot hide failed recomposition.
5. **Retention:** performance after permitted intervening learning, with old-skill audits distinct from maintenance practice. Cold restart tests saved-state usability, not human-like consolidation.
6. **Cost and response:** computation, latency, observed native flow, and any valid local response curves remain descriptive axes, not substitutes for the first five.

A rate uses the full assigned denominator; missing predictions remain visible and do not disappear from the report. Unanswerable cases follow the pinned task semantics and are not silently changed into `absent`. Group correlated views/questions under their root family for uncertainty calculations.

Do not use hidden-vector similarity as an objective to force agreement. Same-role faces need not have identical embeddings. When representations are inspected, retain near-miss controls and coordinate identities. No direction decoder or structural alignment inferred using final-test targets may be used to claim independent understanding.

## 13. Keep oscillatory resonance a separate research claim

The instantaneous relation I=V/R contains no phase, frequency, temporal memory or oscillation-generating state. A dynamic recurrent trajectory may be measured across pass indices, but that is not automatically a sinusoid or a physical frequency in hertz.

No new capacitor, inductor, phase lock, Kuramoto process, memristor, quality factor or resonant-frequency parameter belongs in this addendum. Existing measured oscillatory machinery may be referenced under its own identity, but its state equations and evidence cannot be inferred from the word resonance.

A later dynamic profile would need explicit update law, time/pass unit, damping, measurement protocol, and discriminative tests. More synchrony can destroy distinctions; the historical CMB-P0 finding is one relevant project warning, not a judgment about all oscillatory mechanisms.
