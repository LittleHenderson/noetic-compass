WHY THE REPAIR WAS NEEDED

The cap amendment to $5000 had silently disabled every paid launch.

1. CAMPAIGN_CAP_USD was hardcoded "400.00" in the launchers while
   aws/budget.py carried the owner-amended cap. Launch attempt 1 refused
   with LEDGER_CAP_CHANGED before the reservation. It now derives from
   budget.CAP, so the owner-bound value is the single source and the
   ledger comparison still enforces agreement.

2. validate_state required EVERY write-ahead history entry to carry
   today's cap. After the raise, all 70 entries recorded "400.00", so
   read_locked refused the first one with INVALID_CAMPAIGN_LEDGER and no
   launch could proceed. Each state is now validated against the cap it
   RECORDED; only the operating head must carry the current cap. Free
   space is likewise derived from the state's own cap. History is a
   record of what was authorised THEN.

3. The write-ahead chain digest and the head/tail equality both require
   the ledger head and the newest history entry to be the same bytes.
   The amendment had edited the revision-69 head in place while
   history/00000069.json kept the pre-raise bytes, so the chain could not
   close (attempt 2 wrote its reservation, then failed post-write with
   BROKEN_BUDGET_HISTORY_CHAIN). The amended revision-69 state was
   RECONSTRUCTED and verified by reproducing the digest revision 70 had
   already committed to:
     9ac9970d319e2bb8b55c2154c9968703ddf0c864402196026d6ec0e3488b30cb
   Not one reservation was altered. The pre-raise bytes are preserved in
   records/budget/PRESERVED_history_00000069_pre_cap_raise.json and
   records/budget/campaign_ledger.json.pre_cap_raise_400_to_5000.

No spend guard was weakened: reservations and total_reserved_usd must
still match the history byte for byte, and free space remains derived as
cap minus reserved.
